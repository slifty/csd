  1. Purpose of Policy

    The Board of School Directors recognizes that the foundation of a well-managed debt program is a comprehensive debt policy. This policy sets forth the parameters for issuing debt and managing the debt portfolio and provides guidance to the decision-makers. It is being developed in concert with principles of long-term fiscal sustainability and recognizes a long-term commitment to full and timely repayment of all debt as an intrinsic requirement for entry into the capital markets.  Adherence to this policy will help ensure that the district maintains a sound debt position and that its credit quality is protected.

    This policy is intended to benefit the district by enhancing the quality of decisions, rationalizing the decision-making process, identifying objectives for district employees to implement, demonstrating a commitment to long-term financial planning objectives, and causing the district to be viewed positively by rating agencies.
     
  2. Purpose of Debt

    The Board of School Directors, in any year, may create and incur indebtedness and issue bonds or notes payable as provided by Act No. 1978-52, as amended, known as the “Local Government Unit Debt Act” and Act No. 2 of the Special Session of 2006, as amended. The district will confine long-term borrowing to the following purposes:
     
    1. To purchase or acquire proper sites, buildings, or grounds (including construction of new buildings and facilities) for school use or any lands additional to any existing school sites or grounds.
       
    2. To erect, enlarge, equip, or furnish any building for school use.
       
    3. To make capital improvements (including additions, alterations, and remediation) to existing buildings and facilities of the school district.
       
    4. To fund temporary indebtedness incurred for permanent improvements, or in anticipation of proceeds from a bond issue.
       
    5. To refund certain bonds or notes, provided that there is either: (i) a significant present value saving in the transaction, generally not less than three percent (3%) of the principal amount of debt being refunded for advance refundings, and generally not less than two percent (2%) for current refundings, or (ii) a debt restructuring as recommended by the Board’s Business Affairs Committee and approved by the Board.
       
  3. Type and Structure of Debt

    The district recognizes that it is imperative for capital planning to be integrated with debt-financing activities. The district shall estimate the direct and indirect costs of financing, all as required by the applicable Pennsylvania Department of Education regulations. Long-term borrowing may therefore be used for the renovation or expansion of current buildings and facilities based on the needs of the educational program, the condition of the buildings and the capacity needs of the district. When the district finances capital plans through the issuance of bonds, the payback period (term of the bond issue) will not exceed the expected useful life of the asset renovated or acquired. In no case will the district use long-term debt to fund day-to-day operations.
     
  4. Credit Objectives

    The district will strive to maintain a highly positive credit rating.     The district will also maintain communication with bond rating agencies about its financial condition and will follow a policy of full disclosure in every financial report and official statement provided to bond rating agencies, to legal and professional service providers for bond issues, and to investors. The district will meet all debt service obligations when due and payable and will comply with all federal tax law provisions, such as arbitrage requirements.
     
  5. Continuing Disclosure

    The district will comply with all of its continuing disclosure agreements in a timely manner.
     
  6. Federal Tax Compliance

    With respect to tax-exempt debt, the interest on which is exempt from gross income for federal income tax purposes, the district will comply with the requirements of its Federal Tax Certificate and its Post-Issuance Compliance Policy.
