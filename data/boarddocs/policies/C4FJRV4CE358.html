Guidelines

 

District-owned social media accounts are to be primarily used to keep parents/guardians, students, employees, and community members informed of special events, key initiatives, and emergency notifications.  

 

Guidelines for Social Media Accounts Operated as a Designated Public Forum

 

The following rules apply with respect to public interaction on District-owned social media accounts:

  • The District encourages community members to respond to posts and share comments that are constructive to the school community.
  • Statements and opinions expressed by visitors do not reflect the opinions of the District.
  • Questions regarding information communicated by the District through social media should be directed to the District's Director of Communications to be redirected to the appropriate District administrator.
  • The District Director of Communications may, upon consultation with the District legal advisors through the Superintendent if necessary, limit the accessibility to the public ("redesignate") comments which:
  • Are profane, vulgar, obscene, pornographic, or harmful to minors. 
  • Contain threats.
  • Promote, suggest or encourage illegal activity or incite violence.
  • Promote or endorse commercial products, services or businesses.
  • Promote or endorse political parties or candidates. 
  • Contain confidential information or are in violation of any intellectual property right of another.
  • Contain libelous or defamatory statements.
  • Contain hate speech directed at a protected class of individuals or are harassing or discriminatory on the basis of a protected class.
  • Are fraudulent or spamming in nature (same comment posted repeatedly).
  • Violate any applicable federal, state, or local law or regulation.  

The Superintendent or designee will redesignate individual posts or comments by public users that violate the established social media rules of this Administrative Regulation and the accompanying Board Policy.  Posts or content will not be removed solely on the basis of content or viewpoint.  Posts and comments will also not be redesignated solely because they are critical of the District or District leadership, because they promote an unpopular opinion, or because of their viewpoint if the post or comment otherwise complies with the established social media rules.   Any District representative who redesignates content due to an alleged violation of this Administrative Regulation or the accompanying Board Policy must be able to articulate the reason for removing the specific post, in accordance with this Administrative Regulation and the accompanying Board Policy.  

 

The Superintendent or designee may not block users from accessing or commenting on District-owned social media accounts unless the outside account is identified as a security or system threat or spam account, or unless otherwise permitted by law. 

 

Posting of Personally Identifiable Information

 

The posting of student images in photos or videos depicting the educational process or school-related events is permitted on District-owned social media accounts unless the students’ parents/guardians (or the student, if the student is 18 years of age or older) have opted out of sharing directory information under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act.  

 

The posting of other personally identifiable information of students on District-owned social media accounts is prohibited without the consent of the parent/guardian, except to the extent permitted by applicable law and authorized in advance by the Superintendent or designee.  

 

The posting of staff images in photos or videos is prohibited if the staff member has submitted a request to the Superintendent or designee that their image not be posted publicly online.

 

The Guidelines outlined in this section titled "Posting of Personally Identifiable Information" also apply with respect to posting on the District's website.

 

Accessibility

 

The District is committed to ensuring that people with disabilities have an equal opportunity to that of their nondisabled peers to enjoy the District’s programs, benefits, and services, including those delivered through electronic and information technology – this includes online content and functionality under the control of the District, except where doing so would impose and undue burden or create a fundamental alteration.  District staff who maintain District-owned social media accounts shall post content that is accessible to individuals with disabilities, to the greatest extent possible based on the limitations of the platform. This shall include, but is not limited to:

  1. Including alternate text descriptions or captions for images.
     
  2. Including captions for video content.
     
  3. Ensuring that any text that is posted as an image is duplicated as an accessible version of the text.
     
  4. Creating links and attachments in formats that are accessible to screen readers and other assistive technology.
     
  5. Formatting text so that it is accessible to screen readers and other assistive technology.

If, because of a disability, an individual is unable to access content on a District-owned social media account, or to report barriers to accessing such content, an email should be sent to the Superintendent or designee. 

 

The Guidelines outlined in this section titled "Accessibility" also apply with respect to the accessibility the District's website.

 

Intellectual Property Rights

 

The illegal use of copyrighted, branded or trademarked materials or trade secrets is prohibited on District-owned social media accounts. All content shall be subject to copyright fair use guidelines and applicable laws, regulations and Board Policy.

 

The Guidelines outlined in this section titled "Intellectual Property Rights" also apply with respect to the District's website.

 

Connecting with Other Social Media Accounts

 

Content or information posted to District-owned social media accounts shall not be connected to other social media accounts through linking or tagging if the outside account is for a commercial application, product or service and the District or its employees would receive financial or other compensation as a result of the connection.

 

In order to ensure student privacy, District-owned social media accounts shall not be connected to social media accounts of individual students through linking or tagging without authorization of the Director of Communications.

 

District-owned social media accounts may be connected through linking or tagging to social media accounts of parent-teacher organizations, District-related booster organizations, school-sponsored student activities or similar school-related groups when the content or information has been reviewed and approved by the Superintendent or designee.

 

Personal Social Media Accounts and Personal Websites

 

The District shall not authorize, endorse or participate in posting on private social media accounts of individual school directors or school employees.

 

To ensure student safety, in accordance with Board Policy, school employees should communicate with students through District-provided communication devices or platforms, and shall not follow, accept or make requests to connect or be friends with current students on personal social networking or social media platforms.

 

District employees are not permitted to post any personally identifiable information of students on their personal social media accounts or webpages without prior permission of the student's parent/guardian and the Superintendent or designee.  

 

The District respects employees’ freedom of expression. The District does not actively monitor personal social media accounts or websites of current school employees; however, the District reserves the right to address employees’ job-related speech or employee speech posted on social media that has the potential to affect the District’s operations. Speech that takes place off-site and on an employee’s own time, including posting on personal social media accounts and webpages, may be addressed if the District establishes that the employee’s expression infringed on the interests of the District in promoting the efficient and effective functioning and educational purpose of the District. If employee speech or expression would violate law or Board Policy in a traditional forum, it is also prohibited in an online forum. When an employee speaks as a citizen on a matter of public concern, the District shall consult with the District Solicitor in determining the appropriate course of action, in accordance with applicable law, regulations and Board Policy.  

 

Student use of personal social media accounts shall be addressed in accordance with applicable Board Policies and Administrative Regulations related to student conduct, expression and students’ individual rights and responsibilities.  

 

Consequences

 

A District employee who violates this Administrative Regulation or the accompanying Board Policy may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination, in accordance with applicable law, regulations and Board Policy.
