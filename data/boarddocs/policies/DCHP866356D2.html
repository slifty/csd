Purpose
 

The Board shall operate District schools for the benefit of resident children of the District and nonresident children eligible for attendance in accordance with this Board Policy.

 

Authority

 

The Board permits the admission of non-resident students to District schools if required by law or Court order, or if permitted by the terms of this Board Policy.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Board directs the Superintendent or designee to develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy consistent with applicable law and the below guidelines.
 

Guidelines

The following categories of nonresident students are accepted by Cheltenham School District:

 

Support Gratis Arrangements

When a resident of the District keeps in their home a child of school age, not their own, and supports the child gratis as if the child were their own, such child may be entitled to all free school privileges accorded to resident students; except, however, before accepting such child as a student, the District requires such resident to submit a sworn statement that they are supporting the child gratis, that they will assume all personal obligations for the child relative to school requirements, and that they intend to so keep and support the child continuously and not merely through the school term - on a form provided by the District. Such students, once accepted, are regarded as resident students.

Nonresident Children Placed in Resident’s Home

Any child placed in the home of a District resident by a court or government agency shall be admitted to District schools and shall receive the same benefits and be subject to the same responsibilities as resident students.

Residents of Institutions

A child who is living in or assigned to a facility or institution for the care or training of children that is located within the District is not a legal resident of the District by such placement; but the student shall be admitted to District schools, and a charge shall be made for tuition in accordance with law.

Students Experiencing Educational Instability

The District shall immediately admit students experiencing homelessness, foster care and other forms of educational instability, even if the required documents are unavailable, in accordance with Board Policy, law and regulations.

Children of Active Duty Military Families 

 

Children of active duty military families shall be eligible for enrollment in the District in accordance with the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children, state law and Board Policy.

 

Future Residents

 

Upon completion of an affidavit, nonresident students whose parent(s)/guardian(s) plan to live in Cheltenham Township within thirty (30) calendar days, who would otherwise be entitled to free school privileges in the District, and who have either:

 

  1. Purchased a home within Cheltenham Township;
  2. Signed a contract for construction of a home within Cheltenham Township; or
  3. Entered into a lease agreement within Cheltenham Township may enroll in District schools without payment of tuition.

 

If unforeseen circumstances prevent the actual moving into the Township within the thirty (30) calendar day period, tuition will be charged for each school day from the thirty-first (31st) day to the date the family is actually living in the Township. Parent(s)/Guardian(s) may be required to pay tuition, in advance, in accordance with the most current rate established by the Pennsylvania Department of Education for Cheltenham School District, for the number of school days that the student is expected to attend District schools as a non-resident beyond the initial thirty (30) calendar day period. Students who do not establish residency within the District within the initial thirty (30) calendar day period or pay advanced tuition for the number of school days that the student is expected to attend District schools as a non-resident beyond the initial thirty (30) calendar day period may be subject to removal from school as non-resident students.

 

Former Residents

 

A Cheltenham High School (CHS) student who has attended CHS for their entire sophomore and junior years whose family moves from the Cheltenham School District after the first student day of the student’s senior year shall be allowed to attend CHS as a senior, upon the payment of tuition in advance. This entitlement shall apply only to the school year following the student’s junior year at CHS, and shall not extend beyond the last regular student day of school for the spring term of the school year following the junior year, unless otherwise required by the student’s Individualized Education Program. Transportation for students under this subsection shall be the responsibility of the student's parent or guardian. Tuition may be waived for seniors whose family moves from the District after the first school day of the 4th marking period in the event that a student or parent/guardian presents a compelling case of financial need/hardship to the Superintendent or designee, in accordance with the guidelines outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulation.

 

Additional Guidelines

 

The District will provide transportation to non-resident students only to the extent required by law.

 

Nonresident students, because of unusual or unique circumstances not covered above, may be admitted or retained on an advanced tuition basis when recommended by the Superintendent and approved by the Board.

 

Any nonresident student found to be attending District schools without qualifying under this Policy and without appropriate advanced permission shall be subject to disenrollment in accordance with provisions outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulations.

 

The Board authorizes the administration to investigate credible information, complaints, or circumstances that raise doubt as to a student’s residency or continued residency within the District’s boundaries. Such investigation, and efforts to collect tuition from students found to be attending District schools illegally as non-residents, is permitted to the fullest extent permitted by law.
