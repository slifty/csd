General Guidance

 

As required by applicable law, no school counselor, school nurse, school psychologist, or home and school visitor who, while in the course of their professional duties, has acquired information from a student in confidence shall be compelled or allowed to disclose such information in any legal proceeding, trial, or investigation before any government unit (1) without the consent of the student, if the student is 18 years of age or over; or (2) without the consent of the student’s parent or guardian, if the student is under the age of 18.  Nothing in this paragraph shall excuse or prevent any individual from complying with the required reporting provisions of the Child Protective Services Law.
