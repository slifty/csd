Purpose

 

The buildings and facilities of the District are financed and maintained by the District's taxpayers for the primary purpose of supporting the educational program of the District. The Board believes that District facilities should be available to the community to further the educational, cultural, civic, social and recreational growth of its citizens. Community groups shall be permitted to use District facilities when such uses will not interfere with District programs, in accordance with this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation. Use of District facilities by a specific user or organization does not imply or represent the District’s endorsement of the user, organization, or their program(s).

 

Authority

 

District facilities shall be available to outside organizations to the extent that such use does not interfere with, conflict with or otherwise inconvenience regular school programs/operations.

 

The Superintendent, Director of Facilities and Maintenance, and/or the Business Manager or their designee(s) may limit, restrict, or prohibit proposed uses of District facilities which are determined not to be in the best interests of the District.

 

In addition to assessing facility use fees, the District may assign staff to buildings during periods of time when such facilities are being used by outside users or organizations. The user or organization will be responsible for paying all staff costs, unless otherwise specified in the accompanying Administrative Regulation.

 

Questions regarding facility use eligibility, class categorization, and/or fees owed in connection with a particular use shall be resolved by the Director of Facilities and Maintenance. In the event of a dispute, the matter will be referred to the Business Manager for resolution. If the dispute remains unresolved, the matter shall be referred to the Superintendent.

 

Guidelines

 

Categories of Individuals, Groups and Organizations

 

The individual groups and organizations eligible to use District facilities and their respective classifications are as follows:

Class A. School-Sponsored Organizations, Officially Recognized Parent and Staff Organizations (Approved Activities), Alumni Association of Cheltenham High School, Cheltenham School District Foundation, Montgomery County Board of Elections for Elections, Cheltenham Township Adult School

 

This classification includes organizations whose primary function is to support District educational programs, as well as the Montgomery County Board of Elections on Election Days and the Cheltenham Township Adult School. Examples include school-sponsored student organizations, approved parent-teacher activities (PTOs/PTAs), approved booster club activities, approved staff organization activities, the Cheltenham School District Foundation, the Alumni Association of Cheltenham High School, instructional programs for the benefit of students or District employees, the Montgomery County Board of Elections for elections, and the Cheltenham Township Adult School. Class A organizations do not have to pay a facilities use application processing fee. If admission for any event is charged, the proceeds must exclusively benefit the sponsoring Class A organization; otherwise, fees will be charged to cover the cost of District personnel assigned to the event (i.e. security, custodial, food service, maintenance, etc.)

 

Class B. Non-Profit Organizations within Cheltenham Township whose Primary Purpose Involves Township Pre-School and School Age Children

 

This classification could include, but is not limited to, youth sports organizations, scouting groups, or youth groups sponsored by the Cheltenham Township Parks and Recreation Department. The organization must be physically located in Cheltenham Township and at least 90% of the pre-school and/or school age children participants must reside within the boundaries of Cheltenham Township. Class B organizations are required to pay a facilities use application processing fee. Any entity seeking to qualify as a Class B organization is responsible for providing sufficient documentation evidencing the entity’s status as a non-profit.

 

Class C. For-Profit Groups Within Cheltenham Township; Non-Profit Groups Not Qualifying as Class B Organizations

 

This classification includes any non-profit, community-wide educational, cultural, civic, recreational, character development, charitable, or social service agency that do not meet the criteria to be considered Class B organizations. Any entity seeking to qualify as a Class C non-profit organization is responsible for providing sufficient documentation evidencing the entity’s status as a non-profit. This classification also includes for-profit groups whose primary place of business is located within Cheltenham Township. This classification does not include any for-profit entities that charge an admission fee in order to attend the event or activity taking place in the school facility. Class C organizations are required to pay a facilities use application processing fee.

 

Class D. Events or Organizations Not Meeting Criteria Set Above

 

Class D organizations are required to pay a facilities use application processing fee.

 

Application Procedures

 

  1. All users and organizations requesting to use District facilities are required to submit an application on the designated form to the Director of Facilities and Maintenance or designee. Applications may not be submitted more than 90 days in advance of the proposed use of District facilities. Upon receipt of an application, the Director of Facilities and Maintenance or designee shall determine the applicant’s classification and the corresponding fee to be assessed. No individual or organization may apply to use District facilities on behalf of or for the benefit of another individual or organization.

 

 

Application procedures may include a requirement that the user provide proof of eligibility for a particular class status.

 

Associated Fees and Charges

 

In addition to assessing rental/use fees in accordance with a fee schedule included in the accompanying Administrative Regulation, charges for necessary staff or other services may be assessed, at the discretion of the Director of Facilities and Maintenance. Reasonable efforts will be made to advise the user or organization of such charges prior to actual use of the facility. Added fees may be assessed for damage to District facilities or excessive/unexpected clean up.

 

The fee schedule included in the accompanying Administrative Regulation is subject to periodic revision without prior notice.

 

At the discretion of the Director of Facilities and Maintenance or designee, a security deposit may be required of any user or organization requesting to use District facilities. If two unrelated users or organizations use a facility at the same time and both requests can be honored, each is required to pay all the required charges and fees in full.

 

Contracted Use of District Facilities

 

The Board may, at its discretion, enter into leases, licensing agreements, or other contractual relationships with any organization with respect to use of District facilities when determined to be in the best interests of the District. All such agreements must be in writing and approved by the Board.

 

Policy Violators

 

The District reserves the right to change a user’s classification or prevent access to District facilities by any user or organization that violates this Policy or the accompanying Administrative Regulation. Individuals or organizations found to be using District facilities without having obtained permission in advance to do so in accordance with this Policy will be considered trespassing on District property; law enforcement may be notified.
