Guidelines

 

"Parents of record" means biological or adoptive parents whose parental rights are established by applicable law or court order.

 

In cases where a child lives together with two biological or adoptive parents, both parents are considered to be parents of record.  The District will assume that a child lives with all biological or adoptive parents until it receives information from a parent that this is not the case.  Parents of record have the right, with the school's permission, to remove their child from the school or see their child during school hours. 

 

Joint custody scenarios shall be handled on a case-by-case basis, based upon the terms and conditions agreed to by the parents/guardians or imposed by a court order provided to the District or applicable law.  Absent an agreement by the parents/guardians or a court order to the contrary, the below guidelines will be followed.

 

Rights of Each Parent

 

Parents of record and guardians shall have the right to inspect and review their child's educational records and speak with school officials regarding their child, unless the District is provided with evidence that there is a legally binding instrument, state law, or court order that provides to the contrary.

 

The District will make reasonable efforts to accommodate the requests of non-custodial parents for parent/guardian conferences and for information and access to student records such as report cards, health records and test results, absent a legally binding instrument, state law, or court order that provides to the contrary.

 

Releasing Children from School

 

The District will release a child to the parent of record/guardian (or their indicated designee) with physical custody rights at the time when release is sought, except where the other parent of record/guardian consents otherwise in advance or in the case of an emergency where the student’s welfare is endangered and the parent of record/guardian with physical custody rights is unable to be reached.

 

In the event of a dispute between two parents of record/guardians at school regarding which parent of record/guardian is entitled to pick up or remove their child from school that cannot be resolved by a school official, the matter will be referred to local law enforcement for resolution to minimize a disruption at school.

 

Educational Decisions

 

In cases where a child's parents of record/guardians have shared legal custody, both parents of record/guardians are responsible for making educational decisions affecting the child and will be given an equal opportunity to participate in the decision-making process, absent a legally binding instrument, state law, or court order that provides to the contrary.  In the event the parents of record/guardians cannot agree on making an educational decision, the District will take a course of action based on what it considers to be in the best educational interest of the child.

 

When there is a court order awarding sole legal custody to one parent of record/guardian, the right to make educational decisions for the child resides exclusively with that parent of record/guardian, unless the court's order directs otherwise.  In this case, the parent of record/guardian without legal custody may be included and may participate in the educational process; however, the District will abide by the decisions made by the parent of record/guardian with sole legal custody.
