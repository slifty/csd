Purpose

The Board shall engage in short-term and long-term facilities planning in order to meet the ongoing needs of the District’s educational programs and operations, in accordance with the Comprehensive Plan. The input of a diverse group of stakeholders, including Board members, employees, parents/guardians, community members and business partners is an important and valued part of this process.

Authority

The Board shall maintain enrollment data and other pertinent information (i.e., census information enumeration of all children from birth to age 18 within the District), as required by law, which should be used as a consideration in planning improvements.

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent or designee shall be responsible for leading the District’s efforts regarding short-term and long-term facilities planning and analysis.

In order to inform the Board of the status of existing District facilities and the future needs of the District, the Superintendent or designee shall maintain a written description of existing physical facilities and shall present to the Board on an as-needed basis the following:

  1. A written description of existing facilities, including the current condition of the interior, exterior, and major systems, as well as a prioritized list of future maintenance, repairs, or upgrades;
     
  2. Enrollment by grade level and building during the current school year, enrollment projections by grade level and building for the following school year, and a comparison of actual enrollment figures by grade level and building to the previously projected figures to detect early any changes in population trends; and
     
  3. Recommendations, if any, regarding redistricting or grade configuration modifications based on current student population numbers and/or projected future trends based on periodic demographic studies.

Guidelines

When planning to enlarge, renovate, or otherwise modify existing District facilities, the Board shall consider not only the number of children whose educational needs must be met, but also the physical requirements of the program it deems best suited to meet those needs.

Each school building and site shall provide suitable accommodations to carry out the District’s educational program, including the provision for disabilities, pursuant to law and regulation.

Any planning regarding District facilities shall ensure that current and future facility use by any individuals is in compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.
