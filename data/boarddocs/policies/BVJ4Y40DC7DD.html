Guidelines

 

Professional staff members are expected to thoroughly prepare to deliver their daily lessons. This includes evidence of planning which may be demonstrated through written plans reflecting such preparation and/or through artifacts aligned with Domain 1 (Planning and Preparation) of the Danielson Framework in accordance with Pennsylvania's Act 82, as determined by the Superintendent or designee. The format of written plans shall be determined by the Assistant Superintendent or designee.

 

Professional staff members shall ensure that their written plans are readily available in their classroom, and shall be prepared to share their written plans or other documentation of their prior planning with their building principal or designee upon request, including the content of their lessons, lesson objectives, tasks assigned, and assessments administered.

 

Professional staff members shall provide adequate directions for substitutes, the purpose of which shall be to continue the instructional program or provide a meaningful educational alternative that relates to the subject area during periods of the professional staff member's absence. Plans for substitutes should be readily available at all times in the professional employee's classroom.

 

In addition to written plans, the following information should be available for substitutes: (1) Daily schedule; (2) Up-to-date seating chart of all classes, if applicable; (3) Copies of textbooks, workbooks, etc. being used in class; (4) Procedures for taking attendance; (5) Evacuation/fire drill route and emergency procedures plan; and (6) lessons posted in the appropriate learning management system for student access.
