Authority

 

The Board shall offer a program of career and technical education in order to:

 

  1. Prepare students for employment as skilled workers or technicians in recognized, new, and/or emerging occupations;
  2. Prepare students for enrollment in postsecondary education programs; and
  3. Assist students in choosing career pathways.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall establish the number of credits to be awarded toward graduation for career and technical education courses of study and for cooperative education programs.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall ensure that reasonable efforts are made to ensure that all students and parents/guardians are informed of students' rights to participate in career and technical education programs and courses as contemplated in this Policy and that students with disabilities enrolled in such programs are entitled to services under state and federal laws and regulations.

 

Guidelines

 

The Board supports a program of career and technical education in which District students may attend the EASTERN Center for Arts and Technology (EASTERN) for participation in a cooperative program of career development.

 

District students may also enroll in an authorized career and technical education program outside of the District, provided that such program is not offered through EASTERN and the student's enrollment has been approved by the selected career and technical education program and the student's parent(s)/guardian(s). The District assumes no responsibility for transportation to such career and technical education programs unless required by law.

 

Students enrolled in the District who are participating in recognized career and technical programs shall be subject to applicable Board Policies and Administrative Regulations, in addition to those rules and regulations in effect at EASTERN or the career and technical education program that the student attends.
