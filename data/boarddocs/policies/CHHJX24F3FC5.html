Legislative

 

The Board shall exercise its rule-making power by adopting procedures and policies for the organization and operation of the District. Those procedures and policies which are not dictated by the statutes, or rules of the State Board, or ordered by a court of competent authority, may be adopted, amended or repealed at any meeting of the Board, provided the proposed adoption, amendment or repeal shall have been proposed at a previous Board meeting and, once proposed, shall have remained on the agenda of each succeeding Board meeting until approved or rejected.

Changes in a proposed procedure or policy, except for minor editorial revisions, at the second reading shall cause that reading to constitute a first reading.

The Board may, upon a majority vote, suspend at any time the operation of a Board procedure or policy, provided the suspension does not conflict with legal requirements. Such suspension shall be effective until the next meeting of the Board, unless an earlier time is specified in the motion to suspend.

Procedures and policies may be adopted or amended at a single meeting of the Board in an emergency. An emergency shall be defined for purposes of this rule as any situation or set of circumstances which the Board has reason to believe will close the schools or jeopardize the safety or welfare of the students or employees of the District.

Procedures of the Board shall be adopted, amended, or repealed by a two-thirds vote of the full Board. Policies shall be adopted, amended or repealed by a majority vote of the full Board.

The adoption, modification, repeal or suspension of a Board procedure or policy shall be recorded in the minutes of the Board. All current procedures and policies shall be maintained in the Board Policy Manual.

Board policies and revisions thereto are made only after careful and deliberate consideration. While not typically granted, exceptions to Board policies may be made by the affirmative vote of at least five members of the Board occurring at a public Board meeting.

The Board directs that all Board policies be reviewed periodically to determine if they require amendment or repeal.  It is recommended that each Policy be reviewed periodically on a schedule determined by the Policy Committee, the Superintendent, and the District administration with input from the Solicitor.

The Board Policy Committee shall have the authority to change non-­substantive features of a Board Policy for the purpose of administrative clarity. This authority shall not be subject to the procedures set forth above. The Policy Committee Chair or their designee will publicly notify the Board of any such non-­substantive changes during the next regular Board meeting or through dissemination of the minutes of the Policy Committee to the full Board.

Non-­substantive features include, but are not limited to, the following:

  1. Policy numbers;
  2. Policy titles;
  3. Changes in titles of District employees contained within policies;
  4. Grammatical and punctuation changes;
  5. Elimination of redundant language;
  1. Additions, revisions, or elimination of legal citations; and﻿
  1. Replacement or elimination of outdated terminology.

Executive

The Board shall exercise its executive power by the appointment of a District Superintendent, who shall enforce the statutes of the Commonwealth, the rules of the State Board of Education, and Board policy.

The Superintendent shall be responsible for implementing Board Policy and establishing administrative regulations, when applicable, that: (1) are not inconsistent with applicable law or regulations; (2) are dictated by Board Policy; and (3) are binding on District employees and students when issued. The Board reserves the right to review and to direct revisions to administrative regulations ﻿when it considers the administrative regulations to be inconsistent with Board P﻿olicy or District practices. However, the Board shall not adopt administrative regulations unless required by law or requested by the Superintendent.

The Board delegates to the Superintendent the authority to take necessary action in circumstances not provided for in Board Policy. The Superintendent's action may be subject to review by the Board.

Whenever responsibility is delegated to the Superintendent or other individual, it is understood that such individual may delegate such responsibility or designate an appropriate individual to act on their behalf. ﻿

Review

The Board may assume jurisdiction over controversies or disputes arising within the District concerning any matter over which the Board has authority granted by statute or where the Board has retained jurisdiction in contract or policies.

In furtherance of its adjudicatory function, the Board may hold hearings in accordance with law which shall offer the parties to a dispute, on notice duly given, a fair and impartial forum for the resolution of the matter.

Beyond the basic requirements of due process, a hearing may vary in form and content in line with the severity of the consequences which may flow from it, the difficulty of establishing findings of fact from conflicting evidence, and the impact of the Board's decision on the District.
