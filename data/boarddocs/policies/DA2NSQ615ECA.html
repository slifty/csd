Purpose

It is the policy of the District to provide an equal opportunity for all students to achieve their maximum potential through the programs and services offered in the schools regardless of, among other factors, gender, gender identity, and gender expression.
 

Delegation of Responsibility

In an effort to ensure that transgender and gender and gender expansive students are provided with such an equal opportunity to achieve their maximum potential through District programs and services, and in order to ensure that transgender and gender expansive students are provided with equal access to all school programs and activities, the Board authorizes the Superintendent or designee to develop and promulgate Administrative R﻿egulations designed to ensure the safety, comfort, and healthy development of transgender and gender expansive students while maximizing such students’ social integration with other students and minimizing stigmatization and isolation.
