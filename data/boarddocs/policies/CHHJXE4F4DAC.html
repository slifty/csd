Purpose

 

As a matter of good governance, the Board believes it is important to provide an orientation program to its new members to familiarize them with school and District operations and to assist them with carrying out their responsibilities as member of the Board.

 

Guidelines

 

In addition to complying with the minimum training requirements upon election, re-election, or appointment to the Board, as required by applicable law, newly elected or appointed Board m﻿embers shall also be provided with a District-specific orientation program.

 

All new Board members should endeavor to participate in all sessions of the District-specific orientation program. Sessions should be scheduled at times mutually convenient to all parties.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The President of the Board and the Superintendent or designee will plan jointly and execute an orientation program for new members of the Board.  That program shall be held preferably within two months of appointment or election.  Topics and activities may include, but are not limited to, the following:

 

  1. Visits to schools
     
  2. Information sessions concerning the following topics:
    1. Budget Process
    2. Board Policy
    3. Facilities
    4. Student Services and Special Education
    5. Human Resources
    6. Legal Compliance
    7. Curriculum and Instruction
    8. Board Protocol
    9. Committee Function
    10. Sunshine Law
    11. Organizational Structure
