Early Admission to First Grade

 

The following guidelines apply when a parent/guardian seeks early admission to first grade for their child in the Cheltenham﻿ School District.

 

Students who Completed Kindergarten in a Public School in the United States

 

Students who have completed a year of kindergarten in a public school in the United States but have not reached the entrance age of six years by September 15th are eligible to enter first grade in the Cheltenham﻿ School District.

 

Students who Completed Kindergarten in a Non-Public School or Public School Outside of the United States

 

Students who have completed a year of kindergarten in a non-public school or a public school not in the United States but have not reached the entrance age of six years as of September 15th may request early admission to first grade by making a written request to the Supervisor of Gifted and Professional Development by April 30 of the school year preceding the proposed admission to first grade. The written request shall include, at a minimum, the child's name, address, date of birth, and the reasons(s) why the parent(s)/guardian(s) believe(s) that their child should be considered for early admission.

 

No child may be admitted to first grade unless the child has or will attain the chronological age of five (5) years and seven (7) months or more by September 1.

 

Upon receipt of a written parent/guardian request for early admission of their child to first grade, the Supervisor of Gifted and Professional Development or a representative from the Office of Education will contact the parent/guardian to arrange for the child to be assessed by the District to determine whether the child has demonstrated skills ﻿to indicate readiness for first grade. The Director of Student Services or designee will review the results of the assessment and make a recommendation to the Superintendent regarding whether to approve the request.  The Superintendent, after consulting with the Director of Student Services and Supervisor of Gifted and Professional Development, has the final discretion to approve or deny the request.

 

﻿Students who did not complete Kindergarten

Students who did not complete kindergarten and have not reached the entrance age of six years by September 15th may request early admission to first grade by following the same procedures as are outlined immediately above in the section titled "Students who Completed Kindergarten in a Non-Public School or Public School Outside of the United States."
