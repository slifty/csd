Conduct on School Vehicles – Rules and Regulations

 

The document attached to this Administrative Regulation as Attachment 1 outlines the rules and regulations that students are expected to follow while on school vehicles. The Supervisor of Transportation shall ensure, through adequate training of transportation personnel responsible for enforcing District rules, that such transportation personnel are familiar not only with the applicable student codes of conduct but also with all applicable Board policies and regulations regarding interactions with students as listed in the cross references below. Students are reminded that all school rules remain in effect when traveling on school vehicles and at bus stops. Violations of such rules and/or the rules/regulations outlined in Attachment 1 may lead to school-based discipline in accordance with Board Policy and the applicable student handbook, which may include, but not be limited to, loss of privileges to ride on school vehicles.

 

When an infraction occurs, the school vehicle driver will fill out a School Bus Conduct report, listing the student’s name, school grade, bus #, date, whether the incident took place during the AM or PM run, and a description of the student’s conduct warranting the report. The bus driver contractor will then email a copy of the report to the school principal and/or designee and the District’s Transportation Office. The principal and/or designee and the Transportation Office will then start an investigation into the incident. An audio and/or video recording may be requested to complete the investigation.

 

Bus conduct reports should be submitted as soon as possible, so that the principal or designee and the Transportation Office are aware that an incident has occurred. If the incident happens on the PM run, the driver should at a minimum let dispatch know and submit a report no later than the conclusion of the driver’s AM run the following day.

 

A summary of disciplinary actions arising from conduct on school vehicles will be monitored and reported annually to the Board by the Superintendent or designee.

 

Selection of School Vehicle Stops

 

The Transportation Office is responsible for establishing school vehicle routes and the location of stops. At the beginning of each school year, all students will receive the route number and stop location in the Back to School mailing, along with a copy of the school bus rules and regulations.

 

Students may only get on/off the bus or other school vehicles at their assigned stop. All changes to stop assignments or locations must be approved in advance by the Transportation Office. Parents/guardians who feel a different stop is warranted for any reason shall contact the Transportation Office to request a change.

 

Hazardous Walking Conditions

 

If a parent/guardian believes that the walking conditions along their child’s route to and from school or to and from their child’s bus stop are hazardous, he/she may contact the Transportation Office in order to request a review of the walking area by the Transportation Supervisor. If after the review, the Transportation Office denies the request, the parent/guardian may request, through the District, that the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation review the matter to make a final determination as to whether an alleged hazardous walking route constitutes a hazardous route. If the route designated to be hazardous is to the student’s bus stop, an alternate bus stop will be designated.

 

If the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation designates a route as hazardous pursuant to prescribed standards, the Board will provide the transportation for students who would otherwise walk to and from school but need to traverse the hazardous area.

 

Emergency Drills

 

The Supervisor of Transportation shall ensure, through adequate instruction and a minimum of two (2) actual drills each year, that every student is familiar with school vehicle emergency procedures, equipment, and safe loading and unloading operations, in accordance with law and regulations.

 

In accordance with applicable law, emergency drills shall be conducted on school vehicles as follows:

 

  1. All schools shall conduct two (2) emergency evacuation drills on school vehicles during each school year, the first to be conducted during the first week of the first school term and the second during the month of March, and at such other times as the Supervisor of Transportation may require. Each drill shall include the practice and instruction concerning the location, use and operation of emergency exit doors and fire extinguishers and the proper evacuation of the vehicle in the event of fires and accidents.
  2. Bus operators shall be provided with proper training and instructions to enable them to carry out the provisions of law and may be required to attend classes and drills in connection therewith. Such training and instruction shall be provided by the bus operator’s employer.

Reporting Accidents

 

Any accident involving the operation of a school vehicle, with or without students on board, should be shall be reported immediately to the Transportation Supervisor by phone or text message.

 

The driver will follow the Bus Accident Protocol which is attached to this Administrative Regulation as Attachment 2. The driver will contact dispatch and either the driver or dispatch will call 911 to notify the police department.​​​​​​​

 

The driver shall remain at the site of the accident and wait for the police to arrive to fill out a report.​​​​​​​

 

The Supervisor of Transportation or designee may require the driver to be tested for drugs and alcohol in accordance with the school bus company accident policy and procedure.

 

School Vehicle Driver Handbook​​​​​​​

 

The transportation contractor should develop and maintain a driver’s handbook, which should contain instructions and directions for school vehicle drivers concerning transportation policies, regulations, rules, safety procedures, procedures for reporting accidents or other emergencies, and any other important instructions. This handbook should be updated as necessary.​​​​​​​
