Purpose

Petty cash funds may be used for designated purposes so long as such funds are subject to adequate controls and safeguards as established by the Superintendent or designee.
 

Authority

The Board authorizes the establishment of petty cash funds under the control of employees designated by the Superintendent or Director of Business Services.
 

Delegation of Responsibility

Employees authorized to oversee petty cash funds shall ensure that petty cash funds are spent only for designated purposes for which the fund was established.

The person responsible for the petty cash fund shall maintain a listing of the disbursement slips and receipts on an ongoing basis.

The Superintendent or designee shall develop and promulgate Administrative Regulations designating dollar limits and appropriate uses of petty cash funds.
