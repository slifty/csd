Guidelines

 

Nonschool Materials

 

N﻿onschool organizations, groups or individuals who wish to disseminate nonschool materials on school property or through District resources shall submit them to the building principal or designee. The building principal or designee shall review the proposal with the Superintendent or designee.

 

Requests for dissemination of nonschool materials through District social media channels shall be addressed through Board Policy on District social media.

Nonschool materials may not be disseminated during instructional time or school-sponsored activities, and may only be disseminated otherwise if they are directly related to District activities or have educational value to the school program.

 

If approval is granted by the building principal or designee, in consultation with the Superintendent or designee, the nonschool organization, group or individual shall comply with applicable Board Policy and any related Administrative Regulation, and the District's time, manner and place restrictions for dissemination of materials. Approval does not signify an endorsement by the District.

 

Nonschool Activities

 

Activities sponsored by nonschool organizations, groups or individuals shall not occur during instructional time or school-sponsored activities unless they have educational value to the school program, as determined by the Superintendent or designee in consultation with the building principal or designee. Where a nonschool entity is a for-profit entity that will benefit commercially from an activity during instructional time, the above listed factors must outweigh the commercial benefit to the nonschool entity, as determined by the Superintendent or designee in consultation with the building principal or designee.

 

Requests by nonschool organizations, groups or individuals to invite or promote student participation in nonschool activities shall comply with Board Policy and applicable Administrative Regulations regarding the dissemination of nonschool materials.

 

Scholarships/Awards

 

The District appreciates the generosity of organizations that offer scholarships or awards to deserving students; but, in accepting such offers, established criteria shall be observed.

 

No information, either academic or personal, shall be released from a student's record for the purpose of selecting a scholarship or award winner without the permission of the student who is eighteen (18), or the parents/guardians of a student who is younger, in accordance with the Board's Policy on student records.

 

The scholarship or award, and any pertinent restrictions, shall be approved by the Superintendent or designee.

 

All pertinent information regarding the scholarship or award shall be submitted for review by the Superintendent or designee prior to the date on which it is to be presented.
