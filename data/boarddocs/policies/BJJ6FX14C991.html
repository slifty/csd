Procedures:

 

While the Board does not condone the withdrawal from school prior to high school graduation, should a student seek to withdraw from school, the following procedures will be followed:

 

1﻿. Permission for a student of compulsory school age to withdraw from school shall only be granted upon written consent from the student’s parent/guardian and supporting justification.

 

2﻿. In order for a student's withdrawal from school to be approved, the student is required to meet with the Superintendent or Superintendent's designee to discuss the circumstances surrounding the student's withdrawal. Parent/guardians are encouraged, but not required, to attend this meeting.

 

3﻿.The student and their parent/guardian may receive counseling from the school regarding the possible ramifications of withdrawal from school.  Such counseling could include the following: (1) Information aimed at helping the student identify and achieve educational and life goals; (2) Information about tests for General Educational Development and other alternative educational opportunities; and (3) Information regarding the student's right to a public school education until graduation or the age of 21. 

 

4﻿. All District-owned supplies and equipment in the possession of the student shall be returned to the school prior to withdrawal from school.  Failure to return school property may delay the withdrawal process.
