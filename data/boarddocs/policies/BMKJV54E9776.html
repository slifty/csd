Guidelines

 

District employees shall adhere to the following guidelines﻿ regarding outside activities:

  1. Do not utilize school property for personal gain. Copyrights to materials or equipment developed, processed, or tested by District employees in the performance of District activities in fulfillment of the terms of their employment become and may be claimed by the District. (Does not apply to doctoral dissertations.)
  1. Do not use school property or working hours to solicit or accept customers for private enterprises, unless permitted by Board Policy. School property includes, but is not limited to, the District’s email system and an employee’s District-issued email address. 
  1. Do not engage in campaigning or other political activity on school property or at school-sponsored activities, unless permitted by Board Policy 321 (Political Activities).
  1. Do not use school time for outside activities when there is no valid work-related reason to be excused from assigned duties.
  1. Adhere to the standards of conduct outlined in Board Policy 320 (Freedom of Expression)﻿ and Board Policies 362/462/562 (Outside Employment and Outside Business Interests).
