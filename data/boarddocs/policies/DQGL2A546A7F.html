Purpose

The Board believes that the schools should help students learn to respect property and develop feelings of pride in community institutions.

Authority

 

The Board charges each District student with responsibility for the proper care of District property entrusted to the student's use.

 

Students who willfully cause damage to District property shall be subject to disciplinary measures in accordance with Board Policy and applicable law. 

 

Students and others who damage or deface District property may be prosecuted and punished under law. Students and/or their parents/guardians shall be held financially responsible for any such damage.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations to implement this Policy, consistent with the guidelines listed below. 

 

 

Guidelines

 

All students should be aware that all District property issued to them remains the property of Cheltenham School District.

 

Students are responsible for returning borrowed property to the District in satisfactory condition. If any such property is lost or damaged, it will be the responsibility of the student and/or the student's parents/guardians to pay for or replace those items.  After thirty (30) days, the District may refer delinquent obligations, fines, and/or fees to the local District Justice and/or to an outside agency for collection or other appropriate action.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall, on an as-needed basis, report incidences of vandalism to the Board which shall include the number and kind of incident, the cost of vandalism to the District, and such related facts and comments as the Superintendent or designee may wish to make.
