Purpose

The Board recognizes that a primary responsibility for all staff members is the protection of the health, safety and wellbeing of the students entrusted to the District’s care. It is therefore essential that all staff members understand the obligations that exist and the safeguards that must be observed in dealing with students involved in physical or verbal altercations

Every staff member has a responsibility to provide for the physical safety of students and an expectation that their individual safety is maintained.

 

Authority

The Board has the authority and responsibility to establish reasonable expectations for staff intervention if and when students are involved in fighting or other physical or verbal altercations.

The Board has the authority and the responsibility to create guidelines that define the extent, if any, to which a teacher or staff member should intervene to protect the health, safety or welfare of a student or students.

           

The Board acknowledges the rights of teachers and staff members to protect themselves from physical harm in the exercise of their duties.

 

 

 

Guidelines

The Superintendent shall promulgate Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy which shall provide direction to staff members for how to properly respond when the staff member observes or is made aware of a fight or physical or verbal altercation involving students.
