Price List

 

The Business Manager or designee shall periodically promulgate a price list for commercial advertising options to be posted with this regulation.

 

Prohibited Commercial Activities

 

Advertising may not do any of the following:

  • Be displayed inside classrooms or in other areas that may be disruptive to student learning or jeopardize the health, safety or welfare of any individual, as determined by the Superintendent or designee.  For purposes of this Administrative Regulation, the term classroom is not intended to include the school’s auditoriums, cafeterias, gymnasiums, or athletic fields/facilities.
  • Suggest that the product or service being advertised has been endorsed by the District;
  • Advocate or campaign for a particular candidate or political party;
  • Promote or encourage hostility toward any individual or group of individuals;
  • Promote or denigrate a particular religion or religious practice in a manner that suggests that the District is endorsing a particular set of religious beliefs;
  • Be derogatory towards or incite discrimination against any individual or group of individuals based upon race, color, age, creed, religion, gender, ancestry, national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, veteran’s status, handicap/disability of the group or person, or any other class protected by applicable law;
  • Promote or encourage hostility, disorder, violence, or disregard for legal obligations;
  • Be libelous, or interfere with or advocate interference with the rights of any individual or the operations of the District;
  • Promote gambling activities, the use of tobacco/nicotine products, smoking accessories, electronic smoking products or devices, alcoholic beverages, illegal substances or related paraphernalia; provided however, that establishments that serve alcohol may sponsor advertisements if they legally permit children under the age of 21 in their establishment;
  • Promote the use of firearms or other forms of weaponry;
  • Promote obscenity or pornography, or use sexual content, sexual overtones, or any material that is otherwise not suitable for children, such as adult publications or videos;
  • Advertise, promote or advocate for or against birth control products or programs, or abortion;
  • Promote, as a significant part of its business or other primary functions, other activities which are not in the best interest of the students, staff or schools, as determined by the Superintendent or designee, in accordance with established Board Policy and in consultation with legal counsel, as needed.

 

Exclusive Sponsorship Agreements

 

Exclusive sponsorship agreements are agreements that limit the District’s ability to sell advertisements to individuals or businesses that compete with the sponsor which is a party to the sponsorship agreement.

 

In addition to all of the limitations outlined above, the District will only consider entering into exclusive sponsorship agreements under the following conditions:

  • There is no other current or expected advertising presence of a competing sponsor.
  • The sponsor must purchase an advertising sign at all available venues; and at any venues determined to be “prime” venues by the Superintendent or designee, the sponsor must purchase the highest priced available advertisement in each such location.
  • It is the expectation that exclusivity rights will be sold on a three-year agreement basis, but if requested, the term may be negotiated for a shorter duration.
  • Restaurants will not be considered for exclusive sponsorship agreements.

Sponsors who want to renew their exclusive sponsorship agreements will need to provide the District or its agent with its intent to renew at least 90 days prior to end of the existing agreement.

 

Other Guidelines

 

All commercial advertising agreement must be approved by a committee established by the Superintendent to ensure that the agreement conforms to the mission, values and other established policies of the District.

 

Commercial advertisers shall bear full responsibility for all costs and expenses associated with the procurement, erection, maintenance and removal of all advertising/promotional materials. If any such materials become damaged or require repair for any reason, it is the sole and exclusive responsibility of the advertiser to repair or remove the advertising/promotional materials.  In the event the District becomes aware of a sign or other advertising/promotional materials requiring repair or removal, the District shall notify the responsible individual or organization and request repair/removal within seventy-two (72) hours of notification.  If the individual or organization fails to do as required, the District, at its sole discretion, may repair or remove the material and bill the sponsor for any costs incurred.

 

As a condition of the District permitting a commercial advertiser to advertise on District property, the commercial advertiser shall release, indemnify and hold the District harmless from any liability in any cause of action that may arise as the result of the erection, display, maintenance or removal of the promotional/advertising materials.

 

No student or staff information (e.g., names, addresses, telephone numbers, e-mail addresses, etc.), regardless of whether permitted under federal or state law, shall be made available to sponsors or advertisers for purposes of distribution or dissemination of advertising or promotional materials.  Students shall not be required to complete any survey or questionnaire that is designed to provide marketing information to a vendor or business about their interests and/or preferences for a particular vendor, business, or product.

 

All proposed advertisements are subject to all applicable approvals which may be required by any governmental authority or applicable law. 

 

As a condition of the District permitting commercial advertising and/or sponsorship on District property, the advertiser/sponsor represents that their advertising/sponsorship materials are not in violation of any trademark, patent, copyright, trade secret, or any other intellectual property right(s) of any person or entity.  The advertiser/sponsor further agrees to indemnify, defend and hold the District harmless in any litigation for misappropriation of trade secrets, unfair competition and trademark, patent, copyright infringement, or any other intellectual property claim which may arise out of sponsor’s advertisement or activities.

 

No advertiser or sponsor shall be permitted to use the District’s intellectual property (e.g., team names, slogans, logos, or designs constituting trademark or services marks whether or not registered) unless such use is approved in advance by the Superintendent or designee.
