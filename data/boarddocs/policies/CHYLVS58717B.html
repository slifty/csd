Visitors to District Schools During the School Day

Purpose

The Board welcomes and encourages visits to school by parents/guardians and other individuals with legitimate educational interests, as determined by the Superintendent or designee. To ensure order in the schools and to protect students, employees, and other members of the school community, it is necessary for the Board to establish procedures governing such visits.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent or designee and building principals or their designee(s) have the authority to prohibit the entry or seek the removal of any individual to a Cheltenham School District school or building who does not follow the guidelines stated below and in the accompanying Administrative Regulation, the directives of the Superintendent, building principal or their designee(s), other Board Policy, Administrative Regulation, or any applicable federal or state laws or regulations.

 

 

Guidelines

General Guidelines

 

Visitors with a legitimate educational interest should make arrangements and determine objectives for the visit with the building principal or designee in advance. No visitations will take place unless visitors and the District have established a visitation date and time in advance.

 

Upon arrival, visitors must register at the office/front desk where they will sign in and sign out, receive a visitor’s pass, and receive instructions. Visitors shall also follow all requirements set forth in the accompanying Administrative Regulation to present identification sufficient to enable the visitor’s identity to be crosschecked with a database identifying convicted sex offenders. Failure to provide photo identification may serve as a reason to deny a visitor access to the school or building.

 

Classroom Visits: Classroom and Child Observations Subject to Visitation

 

A parent/guardian, or a person with appropriate professional expertise designated by a parent/guardian, may visit a classroom or program site when:

 

  1. The District has specifically recommended that classroom or program for their child.

 

  1. The District has assigned the child to that classroom or program.
     
  2. The child is currently placed in that classroom or program.

 

Where a specified period of observation to exceed one (1) hour is requested by a parent/guardian, for the purpose of observation by the parent/guardian, or in the alternative, by a recognized professional on behalf of the parent/guardian, such a request must be made to and approved in advance by the building principal.

 

The total number of classroom visits shall be limited to two (2) per child per school year, unless otherwise approved by the building principal or designee or required by law.

 

Interested Educators/Professional Personnel

The top priority of the Board is to provide quality education for District students. Realizing the desirability of sharing educational experiences with others and the values that can accrue from such sharing, the Board authorizes visitations by professional persons not affiliated with Cheltenham School District under certain circumstances.

Such visitation shall be at the discretion of the building principal, with the approval of the Superintendent or designee.

 

Conditions of Classroom Visitations

In order to make the visit effective and maintain the integrity of the classroom instruction, the following conditions shall be observed for all classroom visits:

 

  1. Visitors shall consist of not more than two (2) people designated at any one (1) time without authorization of the building principal or designee in advance.
     
  1. Parents/guardians must identify in writing the name, address, telephone number, and professional credentials of, and must sign a release permitting the disclosure of personally identifiable information to, any appropriately qualified professional that they have designated to visit the classroom or program site of their child.

 

  1. The timing of classroom visits shall be determined by the building principal or designee, in coordination, as appropriate, with the Directors of Special Education and/or Student Services. Scheduling shall be dependent upon factors such as the type and intensity of planned instruction or activities, the susceptibility of individual students to the disruption known or likely to be caused by the visit, the stated purpose of the visit, the number of adults the visit will place in the room at the same time, and the total amount of traffic in the classroom at or near the time of the visit.
     
  2. A District staff member other than the teacher, assistant, or related service providers working with the child may accompany any visitor, at the discretion of the building principal or designee.
     
  3. Visitors shall refrain from disruptive conversation and from interaction with children or staff .
     
  4. The principal or designee may terminate a visit at any time if, in the judgment of that staff member, the visit is disrupting children, staff, or the learning process or becomes a threat to the health, safety, or welfare of children or staff.

 

  1. During the visit, all visitors shall remain in such locations within the classroom or program site as are designated by the accompanying District staff member or the staff member responsible for the classroom and shall follow the requirements of all applicable Board Policies and Administrative Regulations for the duration of their visit.
  2. Questions or concerns about a visit or about anything observed during a visit must be addressed to the accompanying District staff member, or by appointment, to the staff member responsible for the classroom, before or after the visit and outside the presence of children or working staff members.
     
  3. District staff shall not share with any visitor personally identifiable information concerning any students other than the student who is the subject of the observation.
     
  4. Visitors shall keep their observations of other children confidential. Visitors will refrain from discussing or sharing their observations of any children other than the subject of their observation to ensure the privacy and confidentiality of every individual.
     
  5. In response to unique circumstances, or to comply with the requirements of law, regulation, or court order, the District may waive any timeline or condition established in this Policy or its accompanying Administrative Regulations. No waiver given in accordance with this section shall be deemed to establish a revision or modification, or to establish a policy, practice, or custom contrary to the terms and conditions of this Policy or its accompanying Administrative Regulation.
