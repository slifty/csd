In order to achieve the goals of the accompanying Board Policy, the guidelines and procedures below shall be followed whenever feasible and if in compliance with the budgetary needs of the District:

  • Any leased or purchased office equipment shall meet the requirements for an EPA "Energy Star” rating.
  • Any new appliances or kitchen equipment leased or purchased shall meet the requirements for an EPA "Energy Star" rating.
  • All computers, copiers and other large equipment shall be programmed to utilize the energy power saver mode.
  • All non-essential equipment shall be shut off at the end of each school day.
  • Parking lot lights shall be shut off by 11:00 p.m. unless required for a special event(s) or for security reasons as authorized by the Superintendent or designee.
  • All computers shall be shut down each night unless they are essential to maintain network servers, building HVAC, safety systems, or security systems.
  • Doors shall be closed between heated/cooled areas and non-heated/cooled areas. Room windows shall be closed when unoccupied, or when air conditioning systems are operating.
  • During summer breaks, all appliances (such as mini-refrigerators and redundant copiers) deemed non-essential by the Director of Facilities and Maintenance or designee shall be shut off. The contents of walk-in refrigerators/freezers shall be combined into one central location so individual building refrigerator/freezer units can be shut off.
  • All exterior building lights shall be shut off during daylight hours.
  • All unnecessary interior lights shall be shut off when the space is unoccupied.
  • Energy saving bulbs (i.e. compact fluorescent bulbs) shall be installed to replace incandescent bulbs.
  • Domestic hot water production equipment is to be set no higher than 120 degrees Fahrenheit, or 140 degree Fahrenheit for cafeteria dishwasher use.
  • All vending machines must be equipped with energy saver controls.
  • HVAC control software run times shall be adjusted for holiday and other occupied/unoccupied schedule changes.
  • Building exhaust fans shall be shut off during unoccupied periods if not otherwise required for health or safety reasons.
  • Natural room lighting shall be used.
  • Domestic hot water circulating pumps shall be shut off during unoccupied periods.
  • Building heating and cooling space temperatures shall be set within the following ranges:

A) Heating Season - Occupied:

  1. Classrooms - 68 to 72 degrees (F)                 
  2. Offices - 68 to 72 degrees (F)
  3. Locker Rooms/Showers - 72 to 76 degrees (F)
  4. Halls & Stairs - 63 to 67 degrees (F)
  5. Auditoriums/H.S. Audion - 68 to 72 degrees (F)
  6. Gymnasiums - 68 to 72 degrees (F)  
  7. Swimming Pool (air) - 75 to 80 degrees (F)
  8. Swimming Pool (water) - 81 to 85 degrees (F)           

B) Heating Season - Unoccupied:

  1. All spaces – Unoccupied 55 to 60 degrees (F)

 

C) Air Conditioning - Occupied spaces during cooling season:

  1. All spaces - 74 to 78 degrees (F)

D) Air Conditioning - Unoccupied:

 

Units should be turned off during unoccupied periods unless the units must be kept operational to protect building air quality or for other health or safety reasons. The Director of Facilities and Maintenance or designee must grant approval for any units that are to be left operating for air quality control or for other health or safety reasons.

 

The Director of Facilities and Maintenance or designee shall research any new technologies for use of renewable energy sources, saving energy and other energy conservation and sustainability concepts, and present them for consideration to the Facilities Committee if determined to be feasible and cost-effective. Energy consumption in each building will be monitored and reported on a periodic basis to the Board by the Superintendent or designee.
