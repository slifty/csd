Purpose

 

The purpose of this Policy is to acknowledge the District's obligation to students experiencing homelessness, as outlined in federal and state law, and to detail how those obligations are met.

 

Authority

The Board recognizes its obligation to ensure that students experiencing homelessness have access to the same educational programs and services provided to other District students. The Board shall make reasonable efforts to identify children experiencing homelessness within the District, encourage their enrollment, and eliminate existing barriers to their attendance and education, in compliance with federal and state law and regulations.

The Board may waive policies, procedures, and Administrative Regulations that create barriers for enrollment, attendance, transportation, and success in school of students experiencing homelessness, based on the recommendation of the Superintendent or designee.
 

Definitions

 

Students experiencing homelessness﻿ are defined as school-aged individuals lacking a fixed, regular and nighttime residence, which include the following conditions:

  1. Sharing the housing of other persons due to loss of housing or economic hardship.
  2. Living in motels, hotels, trailer parks or camping grounds due to lack of alternative adequate accommodations.
  3. Living in emergency, transitional or domestic violence shelters.
  4. Abandoned in hospitals.
  5. Living in public or private places not designed for or ordinarily used as regular sleeping accommodations for human beings.
  6. Living in cars, parks, public spaces, abandoned buildings, substandard housing, transportation stations or similar settings.
  7. Living as migratory children in conditions described in previous examples.
  8. Living as unaccompanied homeless youth or run-away children, children who have been abandoned or forced out of homes by parents/guardians or caretakers, or separated from parents/guardians for any other reason.
  9. Living as school age parents/guardians in houses for school age parents/guardians if they have no other living accommodations.﻿

School of origin is defined as the school the student attended when permanently housed or the school in which the student was last enrolled.

Unaccompanied homeless youth are those experiencing homelessness while not in the physical custody of a parent guardian.

Delegation of Responsibility
 

The Board designates the Director of Student Services to serve as the District's liaison for students and families experiencing homelessness. The specific responsibilities of the Director of Student Services with respect to students and families experiencing homelessness shall be detailed in the accompanying Administrative Regulations developed by the Superintendent and shall be consistent with federal and state law, as well as guidance from the Pennsylvania Department of Education.
 

Guidelines
 

Students shall not be discriminated against, segregated or stigmatized based on their status as experiencing homelessness.

 

Information about a student's living situation for a student experiencing homelessness shall be treated as a student education record subject to the protections of the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act ("FERPA") and shall not be deemed directory information.﻿

Students experiencing homelessness﻿ shall be provided services comparable to those offered to other District students including, but not limited to, enrollment, as outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulations; transportation; school nutrition programs; career and technical education; preschool programs; and educational services for which students experiencing homelessness meet eligibility criteria. Such educational services include, but are not limited to, those provided under Title I or similar state or federal programs, programs for English Language Learners, programs for students with disabilities, and programs for gifted students.

The District's liaison shall participate in professional development programs and other technical assistance offered by the Coordinator of Pennsylvania's Education for Children and Youth Experiencing Homelessness Program and as outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulations.
