Purpose and Authority

The Board adopts this Policy to ensure that parents/guardians (and students who are 18 years of age or older) have the right, in accordance with applicable law and regulations, to have their children excused from specific instruction and/or state assessments that conflict with their religious beliefs.

 

Definitions

Specific instruction means identifiable elements of instruction by the teacher. It does not include required reading or academic standards.

 

State assessment means the Pennsylvania System of School Assessment (PSSA) or Keystone Exams. For purposes of this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation, locally approved and administered assessments established pursuant to 22 Pa. Code Section 4.24 are also treated﻿ as state assessments.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy. These regulations shall outline the procedure and related requirements for parents/guardians (and students, if applicable) to follow when they wish to have their child excused from specific instruction or state assessments due to such instruction or assessments conflicting with their religious beliefs.
