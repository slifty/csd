Assessment of Student Progress

 

The District's assessment system shall include a variety of assessment strategies, which may include any or all of the following, but shall not be limited to the following:

 

  • Written work by students;
  • Scientific experiments conducted by students;
  • Works of art or musical, theatrical or dance performances by students;
  • Other demonstrations, performances, products or projects by students related to specific academic standards;
  • Examinations developed by teachers to assess specific academic standards;
  • Nationally-available achievement tests;
  • Diagnostic assessments;
  • Evaluations of portfolios of student work related to achievement of academic standards;
  • Other measures as appropriate, which may include standardized tests.

 

Reporting of Student Progress

 

All appropriate staff members are expected to provide timely input and reporting on student progress data, as contemplated in this Administrative Regulation, the accompanying Board Policy and other reporting requirements established and promulgated by the administration.

 

Elementary Schools

 

  • The elementary school term shall be divided into four report periods (marking periods).
  • Parents/Guardians shall receive a written report of student progress at the conclusion of each marking period.
  • Individual parent-teacher conferences shall be held in conjunction with these written reports to discuss student progress.  Additional conferences shall be arranged at the request of the parent/guardian or the teacher.  All such conferences are to be scheduled in advance at a time mutually convenient to both parties.
  • Written interim reports of student progress shall be issued to parents/guardians between report periods to communicate potential academic failure or other area(s) or particular concern.

 

Middle School and High School

 

  • The school year shall be divided into four regular report periods (marking periods).
  • Written interim reports of student progress shall be issued between report periods to communicate potential academic failure or other area(s) of particular concern.
  • Parent-teacher conferences shall be arranged at the request of the teacher or the parent/guardian. These conferences shall be scheduled in advance at a time mutually agreeable to both parties.﻿
