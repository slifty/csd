Surveys Receiving Funding from the United States Department of Education

 

In accordance with the Protection of Pupil Rights Amendment (20 U.S.C. § 1232h; 34 CFR Part 98), the following procedures shall apply:

 

  1. The District shall make instructional materials available for inspection by parents if those materials will be used in connection with United States Department of Education-funded survey, analysis, or evaluation in which their children participate; and
  2. The District shall obtain written parental consent before any minor student is required to participate in any United States Department of Education-funded survey, analysis, or evaluation that reveals information concerning:
  1. Political affiliations;
  2. Mental and psychological problems potentially embarrassing to the student and his/her family;
  3. Sex behavior and attitudes;
  4. Illegal, anti-social, self-incriminating and demeaning behavior;
  5. Critical appraisals of other individuals with whom respondents have close family relationships;
  6. Legally recognized privileged or analogous relationships, such as those of lawyers, physicians, and ministers; or
  7. Income (other than that required by law to determine eligibility for participation in a program or for receiving financial assistance under such program).

In the case of surveys not funded by the United States Department of Education, parents/guardians will receive prior notice of the survey and their right to opt out of participation, as detailed below.

 

All Surveys Other than those Receiving Funding from United States Department of Education

 

A committee of central office and building administrators will review any potential surveys prior to their administration to students.

 

Prior to approving a survey to be administered to students, the committee will ensure the following: (1) the surveys is age- and content-appropriate; (2) the survey is aligned with the District’s mission and strategic plan; and (3) the survey does not require students to provide personally identifiable information beyond general demographic information such as age, gender, race and/or grade level.

 

If the committee approves a survey to be administered to student, parents/guardians will be notified of the intention to administer the survey at least two weeks prior to such administration.

 

Parents/guardians may contact the building principal or designated central office staff member to review the survey and/or obtain further explanation regarding how the survey data will be utilized prior to the administration of the survey.

 

Parents/guardians may submit a written request to the building principal to opt their child(ren)out of participation in the survey. Students will not be penalized or otherwise face any adverse
consequences for being opted out of any specific survey.
