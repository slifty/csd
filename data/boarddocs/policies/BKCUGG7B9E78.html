Guidelines

 

Fundraising will be governed with regard to the individuals or organization conducting the fundraising.

 

Student Organizations Fundraising on Behalf of Student Organizations

 

Student organizations may raise money through various fundraising projects for the benefit of these organizations.  These fundraisers may be limited in number at the principal's discretion.

 

In-School Fundraising by Individual Students and Student Organizations on Behalf of Outside Organizations

 

An individual student or a student organization may raise funds for a bona fide local, state, national, or international organization. These fundraisers may be limited in number at the principal’s discretion. Student participation in such fundraisers may also be limited at the discretion of the principal subject to notice to the Superintendent or designee.

 

Online Fundraising

 

Websites created for fundraising purposes will not be hosted on the District website. If Superintendent or designee approval is granted, the District’s web site may link to approved fundraising sites. The District reserves the right to remove the link to the fundraising site at any time with or without notice.  

 

Limitations on Fundraising

 

All fundraising done for the purpose of making a gift, grant or donation to the District, a District school, or a student organization must comply with Policy 702 (Gifts, Grants, Donations). Student organizations are encouraged to consult with the activity advisor or building principal prior to engaging in any such fundraising.

 

All fundraising activity pursuant to this Administrative Regulation and the accompanying Board Policy shall be in compliance with Board Policy and Administrative Regulation 254 (Student Wellness) to ensure compliance with the Healthy Hungry Kids Act.

 

Limitations on Staff Involvement in Student Fundraising of Any Kind

 

District employees shall not direct, coerce or otherwise exert undue influence over a student to participate, or not participate, in any fundraising activity, whether or not the fundraising is under the auspices of the District.

 

Students and families should feel encouraged to direct any concerns related to this Administrative Regulation or the accompanying Board Policy to the building principal or other building administrator without fear of reprisals or other adverse consequences.
