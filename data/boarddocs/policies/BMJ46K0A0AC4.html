Purpose

 

The purpose of this Policy is to provide a procedure for parents/guardians, students, volunteers, and community members to file formal, written complaints regarding the programs or operations of the District. ﻿

 

Authority

 

This Policy should not be construed to constitute an entitlement to meet with District staff and/or administration, nor shall it be interpreted to create rights to pursue ﻿complaints that do not affect the complainant's interests in the subject of the complaint.

 

﻿Delegation of Responsibility

 

﻿The Superintendent shall develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy, consistent with the general guidelines listed below, detailing﻿ the procedures to be followed when a complaint is received by the District.

 

Guidelines

 

Complaints concerning the District’s programs or operations should initially be directed to the staff member(s) or the administrator(s) immediately in charge of the area in which the complaint arises. Attempts to resolve the complaint shall begin with informal, direct discussions among the affected parties. Only when these informal discussion fail to resolve the complaint shall the more formal procedures, as outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulation, be utilized.

 

Anonymous complaints will not be recognized as an official complaint, as contemplated by this Policy, and will therefore typically will not be given a response under this Policy.

 

The Board requires that all complaints made pursuant to this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation comply with the provisions of Board Policy and Administrative Regulation 922 (Civility).  Complaints that do not comply with such provisions will typically not be given a response.

 

Since the District requires its employees to make mandatory reports to law enforcement and other agencies for suspected child abuse, possible violation of criminal law and educator misconduct, among other required reports, complaints regarding the District’s referral of such matters to governmental agencies shall not be subject to the procedures required by this Policy.

 

Special procedures provided by law or other Board﻿ Policy for handling complaints in certain areas (i.e. discrimination, unlawful harassment) shall supersede the provisions of this Policy and its accompanying Administrative Regulation. Therefore, upon receipt of a complaint brought under this Board Policy, the Superintendent or designee has the right to determine whether the complaint should be handled in accordance with the provisions of this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation, or instead whether the complaint shall be brought under another applicable Board Policy.

 

Retaliation against an individual who files a good faith complaint in accordance with the procedures outlined in this Board Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation, or against an individual who participates in or cooperates with any investigation in response to such a complaint, is prohibited.
