Purpose

The Board recognizes that students have the right to request redress of complaints, as further defined below. Accordingly, individual and group complaints shall be recognized, and appropriate appeal procedures shall be provided.
 

Definition

For purposes of this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation, a complaint shall be one that arises from actions that directly and materially affect the student's participation in an approved educational program or activity and arises from an alleged violation or misapplication of Board Policy or school rules.
 

Authority

The Board and District employees will recognize the complaints of students contemplated by this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation provided that such complaints are submitted in accordance with this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation.
 

Guidelines

This Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation applies to complaints about school programs and activities including extracurricular activities, regardless of whether such programs or activities take place on or away from school property.

 

Complaints of misconduct by a student against another student shall be handled in the same manner as other student disciplinary investigations, and the procedures set forth in the accompanying Administrative Regulations shall not apply to such complaints.

 

Special procedures provide by law or other Board Policy for handling complaints in certain areas such as harassment and discrimination shall supersede the provisions of this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation. Therefore, upon receipt of a complaint pursuant to this Policy, the Superintendent or designee may determine that the complaint should be handled in accordance with the provisions of another Board Policy or legally-required procedure.

 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee will prepare written procedures implementing this Policy in accordance with applicable law.
