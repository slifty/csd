Purpose

 

The Board shall supply each staff member and student with the supplies and equipment that are deemed necessary for implementation of the approved educational program and students' participation in the course of study. Examples could include, but are not limited to: (1) paper for classroom writing assignments; (2) paint for the art program; and (3) basketballs for the physical education program.

 

Guidelines

Where individualized and non-reusable materials, clothing or equipment is required for reasons of safety or health, students may be requested to provide their own materials, clothing or equipment which shall meet standards set by the school. Such standards shall be reasonably related to the considerations of safety, health, and protection of property.

Where the foregoing requirements impose a financial hardship on a student, the District will assume the cost or make alternative arrangements for the student on a case-by-case basis in accordance with established procedures.

 

S﻿tudents may be required to provide certain supplies for participation in extracurricular and co-curricular activities.

 

Delegation of Responsibility﻿

 

The Superintendent or designee shall ensure that no student is denied access and/or participation in educational programs of the District for financial reasons, and shall do so in a way that protects the privacy of each student.
