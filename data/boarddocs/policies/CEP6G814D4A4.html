Purpose

 

The Board recognizes that individuals, businesses, and community organizations may wish to contribute funds, supplies, and/or equipment to support or enhance District programs and activities.

 

Authority

 

The Board or its designee has the authority to accept donations to the District or to any D﻿istrict school.

 

The Board or its designee reserves the right to refuse to accept any donation that is not consistent with the District's mission or goals or when accepting such donation would adversely affect the D﻿istrict.

 

Any donation accepted by the Board or its designee shall become District property, may not be returned without Board approval, and is subject to the same controls and regulations as is other D﻿istrict property.

 

The Board shall be responsible for the maintenance of any donation it accepts, unless otherwise stipulated. As instructional equipment becomes more complicated and diverse, it is imperative that attention be given to technological and curricular compatibility and to potential maintenance costs over time.

 

The Board shall make every effort to honor the intent of the donor in the use of a donation, but it reserves the right to utilize any donation as determined to be in the District's ﻿best interests.

 

In no case shall acceptance of a donation be considered an endorsement by the Board of a commercial product, business enterprise, or institution of learning.

 

All donations shall be recorded in the appropriate inventory listing and property records.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee is authorized to accept donations to the District valued at $250 or less on behalf of the Board and shall notify the Board of the acceptance of such donations. The donor shall be officially thanked in the Board’s name. Donations valued at more than $250 shall require Board approval or, in the case of a donation that is accepted prior to Board approval for urgent or compelling reasons, ratification at the next Board meeting. In instances where the Superintendent or designee doubts the appropriateness or usefulness of an offered donation that the Superintendent would otherwise be able to accept without Board approval, the matter may be referred to the Board.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall, as appropriate:

 

  1. Counsel potential donors on appropriateness of donations.
     
  2. Encourage individuals and organizations considering a donation to consult with the building principal or Superintendent or designee before doing so.
     
  3. Acknowledge the receipt and value of any donation accepted by the D﻿istrict.
     
  4. Arrange for appropriate means of﻿ recognizing or memorializing donations accepted by the District.
