Purpose

 

The Cheltenham Board of School Directors is committed to accurate, transparent, clear, accessible, consistent, timely and comprehensive communication with its stakeholders through various channels of communication. The strength and success of the District rely on the informed support and engagement of the entire school community.

 

Authority

 

As appropriate under the circumstances, the District will communicate clearly, timely and consistently with the public; welcome public opinion and comments; keep the public informed about Board business, the District, and the schools; and share information about student, staff and stakeholder activities, programs, and accomplishments.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop A﻿dministrative Regulations establishing general communication standards and guidelines which help accomplish the above purpose and in accordance with the District's legal obligations.

 

The Board designates the Superintendent or designee to oversee and manage all District-owned social media accounts (including those created for an individual school) and the District's website. This includes serving as the primary contact person for such accounts and the District website.
