Purpose

Adequate maintenance of buildings, grounds, and property is essential to efficient operation and management of the District.
 

Authority

The Board directs a continuous program of inspection and maintenance of all school buildings, equipment, and grounds. Wherever possible, maintenance shall be preventive. 

 

The Board directs that a planned program of preventive and routine maintenance to maximize performance and increase longevity of buildings, equipment, and grounds shall be developed and implemented by the Director of Facilities and Maintenance. When appropriate, technology should be leveraged in ways to drive efficiencies and innovation in the program of preventive and routine maintenance.

 

Delegation of Responsibility
The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy and shall ensure that the Board is kept apprised of current and future maintenance and improvement initiatives. The Superintendent or designee shall also make recommendations to the Board when additional staff or resources are needed to adequately support the District’s custodial, grounds and/or maintenance initiatives. Additionally, the Superintendent or designee shall prepare a five-year facilities plan and update it annually. Updates to the five-year facilities plan shall be shared with the Board.
