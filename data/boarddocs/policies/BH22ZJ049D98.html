Purpose

 

The Board shall provide a comprehensive instructional program to enable District students to achieve educational objectives and attain academic standards required for student achievement.

 

Definitions

 

Planned instruction – a course or a series of planned courses that are designed to teach specific skills, knowledge, and behaviors in a systematic and cumulative manner. Planned instruction shall consist of at least the following: (1) Objectives to be achieved by all students; (2) Content, including materials, activities and instructional time; (3) Relationship between objectives of a planned course and established academic standards; and (4) Procedure for measurement of attainment of objectives and academic standards.﻿

 

Guidelines

 

No planned instruction shall be taught in the District unless it has been adopted by the Board. The Board reserves the right to determine which units of the instructional program constitute planned instruction and are thereby subject to the adoption procedures of the Board.

 

A course guide shall be prepared for all planned instruction adopted by the Board in order to direct and assist the professional staff toward the attainment of academic standards.  Each course guide serves as a basic framework for the planned instruction. Each teacher shall use the course guide to meet the needs of students.  Copies of all current guides shall be kept on file in the Office of Education and made available to parents/guardians.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall be responsible for the preparation of course guides, and shall develop a plan for such preparation which includes: the participation of appropriate staff members and resource personnel; continuing research in instructional methods, materials, and activities; and systematic review of all guides to insure their continuing usefulness in achieving established goals.

 

The Superintendent or designee is responsible for the continuous evaluation of the effectiveness of the District's planned instruction/courses, and shall recommend to the Board such new and altered planned instruction/courses deemed to be in the best interest of the District. The Superintendent or designee is encouraged to invite the participation of appropriate staff members in the formulation of recommendations.
