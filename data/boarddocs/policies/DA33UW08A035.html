Purpose

The Board shall provide, pursuant to rules of the State Board of Education, and where all required criteria contained in this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulations are met, individual instruction to students confined to home or hospital for physical disability, illness, or injury, or when such confinement is recommended for psychological or psychiatric reasons, and the student cannot attend school as a result.

Authority

 

The Board reserves the right to limit homebound instruction where not required by law and/or not reimbursable by the Department of Education.

 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent or designee shall promulgate Administrative Regulations implementing this Board Policy in accordance with law.
