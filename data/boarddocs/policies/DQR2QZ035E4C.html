General Expectations/Rules

 

Each student in the District has the responsibility for the proper care, custody, and control of District property entrusted to them by the District.

 

Each student shall exhibit the proper respect for all District property and shall not deface, mar, vandalize, or otherwise damage any such property.

 

Individuals who violate the provisions contained in this Administrative Regulation and/or the accompanying Board Policy shall be subject to disciplinary action in accordance with Board Policy and Administrative Regulations, school rules and/or the Student Handbook/Code of Conduct. The District may commence proceedings with local law enforcement at the discretion of the Superintendent or designee.

 

 

 

Financial Responsibility for Lost/Damaged District Property

 

Students and their parents/guardians will be held responsible for all loss of and damage to District property, excluding normal wear and tear. Students and their parents/guardians will be assessed the costs to repair or replace lost or damaged property, as determined by the appropriate administrator after consulting with the Director of Business Services or designee.

 

All fines collected from students or their parents/guardians at the school building level shall promptly be forwarded to the attention of the Director of Business Services for deposit into the District’s General Fund.

 

Failure to pay outstanding fines prior to the last student day of the school year may result in any or all of the following:

 

  1. Loss of privilege to attend designated extracurricular activities, including, but not limited to proms, dances, athletics and/or school field trips;
  2. Loss of privilege to participate in graduation ceremonies;
  3. Alternative arrangements for the issuance of report cards, transcripts, or class schedules;
  4. Commencement of proceedings with law enforcement and/or the local Magisterial District Judge;
  5. Other disciplinary measures deemed appropriate under the circumstances in accordance with Board Policy, school rules, or the Student Handbook.

 

Enforcement / Reporting

 

Each building principal is responsible for enforcement of this Administrative Regulation and the accompanying Board Policy within their assigned building.

 

Any vandalism to District property shall promptly be reported to the building principal or Director of Business Services. Building principals who receive reports of vandalism shall promptly forward such reports to the Director of Business Services or designee.

 

Any commencement of proceedings with local law enforcement or the local Magisterial District Judge as contemplated in this Administrative Regulation shall promptly be reported to the Superintendent.
