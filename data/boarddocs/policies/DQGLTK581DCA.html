Purpose

 

When District property such as textbooks, instructional materials, equipment, or supplies become obsolete or unnecessary for educational or operational purposes, the D﻿istrict shall sell or dispose of the such items in both an environmentally responsible and secure manner, in accordance with Board Policy and applicable law.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Board delegates to the Superintendent or designee the responsibility to ensure that any confidential, proprietary, or other personally identifiable information is removed from District property prior to disposition and to dispose of such property in accordance with the guidelines of this P﻿olicy.

 

Guidelines

 

The building principal or department supervisor where any obsolete or unnecessary property is located shall inform the Superintendent or designee. The Superintendent  or designee shall first determine whether such property can be used elsewhere in the District.

 

If the ﻿obsolete or unnecessary property is unable to be used elsewhere in the District and the property is considered to have resale value and can be sold at a profit, as reasonably determined by the Director of Business Services or designee, it may be disposed of by public sale, salvage scrap, negotiated sale, pre-priced sale, or through online electronic bidding. The property may also be traded in for new property.

 

If the obsolete or unnecessary property does not have resale value, or cannot be sold at a profit, ﻿as reasonably determined by the Director of Business Services or designee, it ﻿may be donated to nonprofit agencies or properly discarded.

 

The disposal of any equipment or other property must be done in a manner consistent with environmental rules and regulations and other applicable law. If the property contains hazardous waste materials, it may require special handling in disposal.

 

All personally identifying, proprietary, and confidential information and licensed software shall be removed from the property before any disposal. The Technology Department shall ensure that any electronic files on any technology devices containing personally identifying, proprietary, or confidential information are erased or otherwise permanently inaccessible from such devices.

 

Real Estate

 

The disposal of all real estate shall require approval of the Board and is regulated by the Pennsylvania Public School Code, as well as all other laws and regulations governing the sale of land or buildings.

 

Recordkeeping

 

The Director of Business Services or designee shall be responsible for maintaining records of all obsolete and unnecessary property disposed of during each fiscal year. This summary shall include quantity, description of property, method of disposition, and value received. Such records shall be maintained in accordance with Board Policy.


Records of fixed assets and electronic equipment shall be removed from D﻿istrict inventories upon disposal.
