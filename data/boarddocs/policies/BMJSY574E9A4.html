Purpose

 

The Board recognizes that District employees have the right to private lives and association with others outside of school and work.  The Board, however, has a responsibility to evaluate employees in terms of their effectiveness in discharging assigned duties and responsibilities.

 

W﻿hen nonschool activities adversely impact an employee's effectiveness within the District, the Board reserves the right to evaluate the effect of such activities upon the employee’s ability to satisfactorily carry out their work-related responsibilities and take appropriate action to protect the District's interests.

 

The Board does not endorse, support or assume liability for any District employee who conducts nonschool, outside activities in which District students or employees participate.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Board directs the Superintendent or designee to promulgate Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy which provide guidance to employees in how to avoid situations in which their personal interests, activities, and associations could conflict with the interests of the District including disciplinary action to the extent appropriate.
