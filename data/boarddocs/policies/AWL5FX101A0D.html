  1. Purpose

    Class size in Cheltenham School District ranges from the one-to-one tutorials or counseling sessions to large group instruction of several hundred students. The nature of the subject matter, facilities, and equipment available for instruction, the factors of safety and supervision are all variables relating to class size.

     
  2. Guidelines

    Elementary

    At the elementary level, the policy of the Board has been an average of twenty-five (25) students to one regular classroom teacher per school.

    Secondary

    At the secondary level, the number of classes taught per week is the criteria used to determine the number of teachers.

    Reports of these numbers are sent to the Superintendent by high school administrators for examination.

    In all courses, class size shall be determined by analysis of a series of factors including but not limited to, total course enrollment, nature of the program, safety considerations, facilities, instructional needs of the students, design of the master schedule, and other relevant factors.

    The Superintendent may recommend changes in the numbers of professional staff according to the numbers and needs of students. The Board shall authorize the final number of professional staff members to be employed each year.

    Insufficient Enrollment

    Prior to the end of April each year, the administration shall present to the Board for review a list of all courses with an enrollment of fewer than fifteen (15) students, except special education.

    The administration shall also provide projected course enrollments, recommendations for deleting any courses from the curriculum or continuing them despite low enrollment, and a complete rationale for these recommendations.

    All decisions shall be subject to the provisions of Section 1604 of the Pennsylvania School Code and Chapter 5 of the Regulations of the Pennsylvania Department of Education.

    Courses shall be dropped only by majority vote of the Board.
