Overtime

 

In accordance w﻿ith the Fair Labor Standards Act, overtime shall be paid to non-exempt (including hourly classified) employees as follows:

 

  1. No ﻿overtime shall be scheduled or worked without the prior approval of the immediate supervisor or the Superintendent's designee. Employees who work overtime without proper approval may be disciplined, up to and including termination of employment for repeated offenses.

 

  1. Overtime will be paid at the rate of time and one-half the regular rate of pay for time worked in excess of forty (40) hours per week.

 

  1. Non-exempt (including hourly classified) employees who work hours in excess of their regularly scheduled hours during a given workweek will be compensated at their regular hourly rate for all hours worked up to forty (40) total hours during a given workweek.

 

  1. For purposes of computing overtime, credit shall be given only for hours actually worked and as provided by law. Time off for holidays, sick leave (including workers compensation), personal leave, vacation, jury duty, bereavement, religious leave, or any other type of paid leave will not be included when computing overtime.﻿

 

Additional Employee Responsibilities

 

  • Employees are expected to take their scheduled meal and other breaks during their shifts, and may not work through such breaks, without prior approval of their immediate supervisor, except in emergency circumstances.

 

﻿Additional Supervisor Responsibilities

 

  • It is not permissible to routinely allow employees to work through a lunch break and earn overtime. 

 

  • Supervisors who approve overtime pay shall be prepared to articulate the reason(s) why such overtime pay was determined to be necessary under the circumstances.﻿﻿
