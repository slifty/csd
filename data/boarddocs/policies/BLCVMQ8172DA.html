All Cheltenham High School students are required to carry their current student photo identification card during the school day, at school-sponsored events, and while traveling on vehicles owned, operated, leased, or under the control of the District.

 

Student identification cards are provided by the District and remain District property at all times.  In the event that a student's photo identification card is stolen or misplaced, the student shall immediately inform their building principal or designee.  Students may be required to pay a replacement fee if their student photo identification card must be reproduced for any reason.

 

When a student withdraws from the District during the school year, their student photo identification card must be returned along with all other property owned by the District in the student's possession.

 

 

Students may not loan their student photo identification cards to other students or allow other students to utilize their student photo identification cards for any reason. Furthermore, students may not damage, duplicate, or alter their student photo identification card in any way.

 

Violations of this Administrative Regulation or the accompanying Board Policy, including refusal on the part of a student to carry their student photo identification card and present it upon request of a District employee will be considered a violation of school rules and Board Policy 218 (Student Discipline) Such violations will be handled in accordance with established Board Policy.
