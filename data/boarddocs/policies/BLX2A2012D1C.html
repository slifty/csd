Guidelines Criteria for Selection of Textbooks for Board Consideration

 

When making a recommendation to the Board regarding the adoption of textbooks, the Superintendent or designee(s) shall consider, among other factors deemed relevant by the Superintendent or designee(s), the following:

  • Suitability for student maturity level;
  • Relationship to the curriculum and a continuous, multi-grade program;
  • Freedom from bias;
  • Culturally relevant;﻿
  • Cost; and
  • Appearance and durability.

 

Complaint Procedure

 

Written c﻿omplaints concerning a textbook utilized in the District's instructional program shall be forwarded to the Assistant Superintendent or designee. The complaint shall be as objective and specific as possible. The Assistant Superintendent or designee will generally respond to the complaint within 15 business days of receipt of the written complaint. The individual filing the complaint may be asked to meet or speak with the Assistant Superintendent or designee is order to resolve the issue/concern in a mutually agreeable manner.
