Purpose

The purpose of this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation is to outline the processes and procedures that the District will follow with respect to access to students and student records in cases of divorce, separation, adoption and similar circumstances.
 

 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop procedures for District employees to follow with respect to access to students and student records in cases of divorce, separation, adoption and similar circumstances, in accordance with applicable law. ﻿
