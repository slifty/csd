Purpose

The Board is committed to (1) reducing the District's energy consumption and environmental footprint throughout its buildings and grounds and (2) using renewable energy sources while maintaining a comfortable learning and work environment for students and staff and also meeting the necessary financial and budgetary goals of the District. The Board is further committed to implementing and supporting curricular initiatives and professional development that equip students, staff, and other members of the school community with the knowledge, skills, and passion to take action in furthering environmentally sustainable practices and habits at school, work, and beyond. This may take the form of periodic renewal and sustainability audits and other initiatives consistent with the District's current and future plans for sustainability.

 

The Board establishes a goal of adopting a sustainability plan on or before June 30, 2022.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The implementation of this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation is a shared responsibility of all District employees, students, volunteers/visitors, and those who use District facilities. The accompanying Administrative Regulation shall address, at a minimum, waste and food, energy and water consumption.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall be responsible for ensuring that effective sustainability education and professional development is provided to District students and all staff at appropriate intervals.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall implement procedures that enable students and staff to provide meaningful input on the District's sustainability efforts and hear about the impact of such efforts. When appropriate, students and staff shall be afforded opportunities to assume leadership roles in advancing the District's sustainability efforts.

 

Building principals shall provide assistance, as necessary, in implementing this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation at the individual building levels.

 

All District employees are responsible for implementing energy management regulations and practices during the period of time while they are in District facilities or on District grounds.

 

Maintenance/Custodial staff are responsible for implementing energy management regulations and practices for common areas such as halls, cafeterias, restrooms, gyms, locker rooms, and other school property, and ensuring that all non-essential energy usage is curtailed at building closing. Maintenance staff shall also be responsible to ensure that energy-saving systems are operative.
