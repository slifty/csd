Purpose﻿ 

 

It is the Board's intent to effect the prompt payment of bills, but at the same time ensure that due care has been taken in the review of such bills.

 

Authority

 

Each bill or obligation of the District must be properly itemized, verified and approved by the Board before payment may be made.

 

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Treasurer or designee may pay out funds on orders prepared and signed/approved by the Board Secretary or designee prior to the Board having approved such payments for amounts due under any contracts previously approved by the Board where prompt payment will result in a discount or other advantage to the District.

Delegation of Responsibility

 

It shall be the responsibility of the Business Manager or designee, upon receipt of an invoice, to verify that the purchase invoice is in order, the goods were received in acceptable condition or services were satisfactorily rendered, funds were budgeted by the Board for the good or service, funds are available to cover the payment, and the invoice is for the amount contracted.

 

Should the invoice vary from the acknowledged purchase order, the Business Manager or designee shall document on the invoice the reason for such variance, and, if necessary in the case of an overage, request that the Board approve a budget transfer to cover the overage.

 

﻿Guidelines

 

All claims for payment shall be submitted to the Board and recorded in the minutes of the Board meeting.

 

The Treasurer or Board designated agent is authorized to make payments on proper orders approved by the Board and signed by the Board President or Vice President, Treasurer, and Board Secretary. Payments may be made via check or an electronic transfer of funds.

 

No checks shall be made out to cash.

 

Sales Tax

 

The D﻿istrict is exempt from sales tax on the purchase of tangible, personal property or services that are sold or used by the D﻿istrict. 

 

The District shall control use of its sales tax exemption number issued by the Department of Revenue, in compliance with established regulations. Only those individuals authorized in advance by the Business Manager or designee may use the District's sales tax exemption number. Individuals authorized to use the District's tax exemption number may only use the exemption number for purposes authorized by the Business Manager or designee. The exemption number shall be used only when buying property or services for District use.﻿ The District shall obtain a sales tax license number or pay applicable sales tax for school organizations who purchase items to be resold.
