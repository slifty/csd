Purpose

 

The Board regards the use of bicycles and motor vehicles for travel to and from school by students, staff and others as an assumption of risk and responsibility by parents/guardians and students.

 

Authority

 

The Board will permit the use of bicycles by students, staff and others in accordance with school rules and those rules outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulation.

 

The Board will permit the use of motor vehicles by students for purposes of traveling to and from school provided that such students have applied for and received a Cheltenham High School student parking permit. Student drivers are subject to the driving and parking rules and regulations contained in the Cheltenham High School Student Handbook, as well as those contained in the accompanying Administrative Regulation. Students who violate these rules and regulations are subject to disciplinary consequences, including, but not limited to, potential loss of the driving/parking privileges at school.

 

All motor vehicles parked on school property are subject to search by school administration, to the extent permitted by law and as set forth in the accompanying A﻿dministrative R﻿egulation, at any time when it is determined there is reasonable suspicion that the vehicle may items which constitute a direct and immediate threat to the health, safety and welfare to the school community.

 

The Board will not be responsible for bicycles or motor vehicles which are lost, stolen, or damaged, or for injuries arising out of their use.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop and publish rules and regulations for the operation and parking of bicycles and motor vehicles by students, staff and others who use bicycles and motor vehicles for travel to and from school.
