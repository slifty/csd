  1. Purpose

    In an era where data-gathering activities are increasingly prevalent to assess student achievement, evaluate curriculum, programs and services, and obtain feedback, this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation detail the procedures in place to ensure the privacy rights of students and their families are protected whenever surveys are administered to students.
  1. Delegation of Responsibility

    The Superintendent or designee shall be responsible for developing an Administrative Regulation outlining the procedures to be followed to ensure that student/family privacy is protected when surveys are administered to students.
  1. Guidelines

            The Superintendent or designee shall ensure that the administration of surveys to students and the use of personal information obtained in connection with such surveys is in compliance with the Protection of 

            Pupil Rights Amendment (PPRA), the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), and any other applicable law or regulation concerning the administration of surveys to students and the use of any     

            personal information obtained in connection with such surveys.

 

            No outside agencies, organizations or individuals may administer surveys to District students without receiving prior permission of the Superintendent or designee. Prior to the Superintendent or designee     

            granting permission for an outside agency, organization or individual to administer a survey to District students, the Superintendent or designee must ensure that adequate safeguards are in effect to protect the     

            privacy rights of students and their families.
