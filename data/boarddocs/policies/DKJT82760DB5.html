In the event a designated administrator must take disciplinary action against an employee whose behavior is in violation of the accompanying Board Policy or otherwise incompetent, immoral, negligent, cruel, or in persistent or willful violation of school laws the following procedures shall be followed, to the extent practicable under the circumstances:

 

  1. Document the rule, law, or Policy item which is being ignored or violated.

 

  1. Document the infraction with specifics as to the activity observed, date, time and identity of witnesses.

 

  1. Document management’s responses to infractions, including specific and explicit written directions to the individual with the date, time, response of individual, agreed upon timeline for correction, if appropriate, and offer of assistance.

 

Disciplinary action will occur in all incidents where there exists just cause. Designated administrators are therefore responsible for:

 

  1. Forewarning of possible disciplinary consequences;

  1. O﻿btaining substantial evidence of the infraction; and

 

  1. D﻿etermining, with the Superintendent or designee the appropriate degree of discipline related to the seriousness of the offense and the record of the involved employee’s service.﻿
