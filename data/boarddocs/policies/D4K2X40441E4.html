Purpose

The Board recognizes that exclusion from school, whether by suspension or expulsion, is the most severe sanction that can be imposed on a student, and one that cannot be imposed without due process.
 

Authority

The Board shall define and publish the types of offenses that would lead to exclusion from school.  Exclusions affecting students with disabilities shall also be governed by Board Policy and applicable state and federal law and regulations.

 

Every principal or designee in charge of a District school may temporarily suspend any student on account of disobedience or misconduct. Any principal or designee suspending any student shall promptly notify the Superintendent or designee.

 

The Board may, after a proper hearing, suspend such child for such time as it may determine, or may permanently expel them. Such hearings may be delegated to a duly-authorized committee of the Board, or to a duly-qualified hearing examiner, who need not be a member of the Board, but whose adjudication must be approved by the Board.

 


 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations to implement this Policy in accordance with applicable state and federal law and regulations and other Board Policies.
