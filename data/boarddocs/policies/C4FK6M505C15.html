General Philosophy

 

Under the direction of the Director of Communications, guided by the Superintendent, the Communications Department shall strive to develop and maintain a positive relationship with the community by using various forms of media to share information with stakeholders and encourage two-way communication.

 

The Director of Communications shall be responsible for:

  1. Serving as the District's liaison to and point of contact with external media representatives;
  2. Planning, supporting, and executing appropriate communications and a public information program designed to keep the community informed of the planning, policies, programs, services, and operations of the District; and
  3. Overseeing the publicity of District programs, events, or initiatives as needed.

Examples of Communications Channels

  • District Website (www.cheltenham.org) 
  • District and School Social Media Accounts (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram)
  • Weekly News Share Publication
  • School Messenger Robo Calls
  • District and School-Based E-Blasts
  • District Television Station (Comcast Channel 42 and Verizon Channel 1960)
  • District streaming and video teleconferencing

General Communications Guidelines

 

District employees and students are required to direct any media inquiries or requests for statements or information from media representatives to the Director of Communications.  Unless, due to subject matter, another individual is designated by the Superintendent, the Director of Communications is the District's official spokesperson.

 

Questions, comments or suggestions regarding this Administrative Regulation or the accompanying Board Policy should be directed to the Director of Communications.
