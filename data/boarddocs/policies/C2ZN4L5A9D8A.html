A copy of the current Memorandum of Understanding between the District and the Cheltenham Township Police is attached hereto.

 

The provisions outlined in the attached MOU are to be followed not only in school, but also on school vehicles, school trips, and school-sponsored activities.  Staff members do not need to and should not wait for administrator approval to report incidents to the police in the event of an emergency. However, the staff member’s administrative supervisor should be notified of a call made to the police as soon as possible following the call. 

 

Questions regarding implementation of this Administrative Regulation, including the attached MOU, and the accompanying Board Policy should be directed to the building principal, District Security Manager, or Assistant Superintendent.

 

Individuals with questions about the Safe Schools Act legal requirements regarding the Memorandum of Understanding with law enforcement and the mandatory reporting of certain offenses to law enforcement are encouraged to review Section 1303-A of the Public School Code, which can be found by visiting https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/uconsCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&yr=1949&sessInd=0&smthLwInd=0&act=14&chpt=13A&sctn=3&subsctn=0. Information regarding specific criminal offenses that require law enforcement notification can be found in the Pennsylvania Crimes Code, which can be viewed by visiting https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&ttl=18.
