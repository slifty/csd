Guidelines

 

Cheltenham School District values the purpose of homework to support student learning within the classroom. Meaningful homework, which is review and/or practice, is differentiated for the learner and provides all students the opportunity to achieve success. Students should receive specific criteria for the successful completion of the assigned task. Homework is defined as any assignment required to be completed outside of the classroom period. School grade level teams/departments will work together to communicate homework expectations at each school. These expectations will be provided to parents/guardians and students in writing. The following guidelines have been established to ensure our students are able to balance school responsibilities along with home and extracurricular activities. 

 

Grades K-2

Students are encouraged to read daily for 15 minutes or longer, as time allows. When homework is assigned, it will not exceed 15 minutes per day.

 

Grades 3-4

Students are encouraged to read daily for 20 minutes or longer, as time allows. When homework is assigned, it will not exceed 30 minutes per day. 

 

Grades 5-6

Students are encouraged to read daily for 20 minutes or longer, as time allows. When homework is assigned, it will not exceed 45 minutes per day. 

Grades 7-8

Students are encouraged to read daily for 20 minutes or longer, as time allows. When homework is assigned, it will not exceed 75 minutes per day. Teams will work together to ensure there are no conflicts with scheduled exams. 

 

Grades 9-12

Students are encouraged to read daily for 20 minutes or longer, as time allows. When homework is assigned, it will not exceed 120 minutes total per day across all courses except for Advanced Placement courses. Advanced Placement courses mirror coursework at the collegiate level. Advanced Placement courses are prescribed by College Board and may require additional time for completion of course objectives. 

 

Homework shall have due dates and times that correspond with the regular school day and class hours. Homework assignments are to be provided/posted before the end of class. Homework should not be assigned or given due dates over a weekend, Thanksgiving, Winter or Spring breaks, or during observance of religious holidays. When students are taking state or national assessments, no homework should be assigned to be due on the test administration dates. 

 

Long Term Assignments/Projects

Students may receive assignments/projects that require more than 5 days to complete. These assignments cannot have due dates immediately following a weekend or the District’s return from a scheduled holiday or break.
