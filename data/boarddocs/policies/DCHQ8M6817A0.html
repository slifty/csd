Authority
Electronic devices are permitted in all of the buildings owned by the District, on school grounds, in school vehicles, and/or while participating in school-sponsored activities on or off school premises. The District administration shall have the right to regulate the use of such electronic devices in accordance with applicable law.

Delegation of Responsibility
The Superintendent or designee shall promulgate Administrative Regulations detailing, at a minimum, rules and conditions upon which students may possess and use electronic devices.
