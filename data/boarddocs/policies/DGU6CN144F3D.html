Purpose

Field trips, when used for teaching and learning integral to the curriculum, are an educationally sound and important component of the instructional program.

Definition

For purposes of this Policy, a field trip shall be defined as any trip of students away from school premises that is an integral part of approved planned instruction, is conducted as a first-­hand educational experience not available in the classroom, and is supervised by a District employee.

Delegation of Responsibility

Field trips are to be conducted according to the guidelines established by the Superintendent, as set forth in the accompanying Administrative Regulations, and consistent with the guidelines established by the Board, as set forth below.  Such Administrative Regulations shall include provisions to ensure the participation of students with disabilities in accordance with applicable law and the student's IEP or Section 504 Plan.  

The Board delegates to the Superintendent or designee the right to cancel, postpone or terminate any field trip in the event of war, acts of terrorism, natural disasters or other circumstances which might endanger the students or staff.  The Superintendent shall inform the Board in the event of any such field trip cancellation.

Guidelines

Although no specific number of field trips is allotted to any teacher, class or school, trips will be limited by budgetary guidelines. Absent exigent and unforeseen circumstances, all field trips require prior approval by the Superintendent or designee. The Board does not endorse, support nor assume responsibility in any way for any District staff member who takes students on field trips not approved by the Superintendent or designee.

District employees, chaperones, and students shall adhere to and are subject to all school rules, Board Policies, and Administrative Regulations during the course of the field trip, as if they were on school property.
