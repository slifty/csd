Purpose

 

The Board directs that District students shall attend the school associated with the attendance area where they reside, unless an exception has been granted or an alternative assignment approved by the Superintendent or designee in accordance with this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulations.

 

Authority

 

The Board is authorized to establish school attendance areas in accordance with law.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall periodically review existing attendance areas and recommend any proposed changes to the Board that may be justified based upon the educational, fiscal, and logistical goals of the District.

 

The Superintendent or designee may grant exceptions to this policy or assign a student to a school other than the one designated for the attendance area where the student resides when such exception or alternative assignment is consistent with the student's educational program and the financial and logistical goals of the District. Requests for attendance zone exceptions shall be submitted to the District Registrar. Such requests will not be granted if honoring such a request would require the addition of a class section or otherwise result in a particular class section exceeding the class size guidelines established in Board Policy.﻿

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy, consistent with the general guidelines listed below.

 

Guidelines

 

In assigning students to schools within the District, no unlawful discrimination shall occur.

 

Twins and higher order multiples shall be assigned to class sections in accordance with applicable law.

 

Students moving from one school attendance area to another school attendance area within the District during the school year are permitted to complete the school year in the school associated with their former attendance area. In such cases, the student's parent/guardian shall be responsible for the student's transportation to/from school, unless otherwise required by law.

 

Homeless students shall be assigned to school in accordance with Board Policy and applicable law.
