Purpose

 

District-owned social media accounts are intended to be used as a way to strategically and carefully communicate with interested stakeholders and members of the public regarding the District and District-sponsored programs, activities, and events.

 

The purpose of this Policy is to establish the process and standards for approval and operation of District-owned social media accounts and to identify the differences between personally owned social media accounts and those maintained by the District.

 

Definitions

 

Social media - a category of Internet-based resources that integrate user-generated content and user participation to share information, ideas, personal messages and other content, including photos and videos. Social media includes social networks, which are online platforms where users can create profiles, share information and personal messages, and connect with others.

 

District-owned social media account - a social media account, regardless of platform, that is approved by the Superintendent or designee and operated or overseen by a designated District employee(s), and is designed to further the educational mission of the District by providing information to the school community and general public. Personal social media account - a social media account, regardless of platform, that is attributed to and operated by a District employee, individual school director, or student for personal use and is not approved by the Superintendent or designee as an official communications channel of the District.

 

Designated public forum - created when a District-owned social media account is intentionally opened for use by the public as a place for expressive activity where members of the public may communicate, post or comment on information, subject to viewpoint neutral rules. In terms of social media, this would include the ability of public users to comment on or reply to social media posts, pictures, or videos.

 

Nonpublic forum – created when a District-owned social media account enables members of the public to read and receive district information, but the District has not designated opportunity for expressive activity by the public, and no commenting or posting of information by members of the public is permitted. In terms of social media, the ability to comment, post or reply is disabled on the District’s account for public users.

 

Authority

 

The Board establishes that District-owned social media accounts may operate as designated public forums, where the public may comment and interact with information posted by the District, subject to any established rules.

 

The Board establishes that District-owned social media accounts may also operate as nonpublic forums. To the extent that a District-owned social media account will be operated as a nonpublic forum, the Board directs school staff to disable functions allowing public users to comment or post information on such accounts.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall approve all social media accounts created and/or maintained as District-owned accounts, including social media accounts for individual schools within the District.

 

The Board designates the Superintendent or designee to oversee and manage all District-owned social media accounts and serve as the primary contact person for District-owned social media accounts.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall be responsible for developing and implementing Administrative Regulations, consistent with the general guidelines listed below, which shall, at a minium, outline the rules and regulations regarding the operation of District-owned social media accounts and requirements of District employees with respect to use of personal social media accounts.

 

The Superintendent or designee will cooperate, in accordance with law, with social media sites, internet service providers, local, state and federal officials in investigations or with other legal requests, whether criminal or civil in nature, related to the use of social media.

 

Guidelines

 

All District-owned social media accounts shall display the official name of the District or the individual District school. Individuals may not use the name of the District or its logo or mascot on sites not owned by the District or in forums/discussion boards to express or imply the official position of the District without the prior written permission of the Superintendent or designee. Individuals are not permitted to create social media accounts of any nature which imply ownership, control or an official account of the District or a District school, or allege to represent the District or school’s position on any issue without the prior written permission of the Superintendent or designee.

 

All provisions of the District's Acceptable Use of Technology Policy shall apply in the event an individual engages in social media activity via the District's network.

 

Employees who participate in social media activities by maintaining their own sites or participating on sites outside of the District’s network should exercise caution to communicate that they are not acting in a District capacity or expressing the views of the District. Such employees should read and comply with the social media site's Terms of Service.

 

District-owned social media accounts are intended to connect to the public. Accordingly, District employees should not use District-owned social media accounts to conduct on-line conversations about individual employee or student matters which are more appropriately held in private as set forth in more detail in the accompanying Administrative Regulation.
