Access to Recess Guidelines

Purpose

 

The Board is committed to providing a school environment that promotes opportunities for regular physical activity as part of the total learning experience.  Such opportunities support students’ health, well¬being, and ability to learn.  

 

Guidelines


At the elementary school level, time shall be allotted for students to participate in supervised recess.  


Recess shall not be taken away from students as a form of punishment.  However, removal from recess may be an appropriate consequence for student misconduct during recess, including, but not limited to misconduct which threatens the health, safety or welfare of the student or others. Removal may also be appropriate for students in the event that weather or other conditions create a threat to the health, safety or welfare of the student or others.

 

Delegation of Responsibility


The Superintendent shall be responsible for promulgating Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy which shall establish guidelines to be followed for determining whether students will go outside or remain indoors for recess due to weather and/or other safety related conditions. 

 
The Superintendent or designee shall have the authority to make or require exceptions to the criteria outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulations when doing so promotes the health, safety, or welfare of students, as determined by the Superintendent or designee.
