Purpose

Punctual and reliable attendance and the completion of assigned duties by administrative, professional and support employees is essential for the operation of the D﻿istrict. Positive outcomes derived from attendance include, but are not limited to, enhanced learning, positive morale, efficient and consistent operations, and lower administrative costs.

 

 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

It shall be the responsibility of the Superintendent or designee to impose employee discipline in accordance with the applicable collective bargaining agreement and applicable law when a District employee fails to meet attendance requirements.
