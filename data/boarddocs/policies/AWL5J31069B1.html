Purpose

 

The Board recognizes that each student's mode of dress and appearance is a manifestation of personal style and individual preference. The Board will not interfere with the right of students and their parents/guardians to make decisions regarding their dress and/or appearance, except when their choices disrupt the educational program or jeopardize the health and safety of others. 

 

Authority

 

The Board has the authority to impose limitations on students' dress in school to the extent that such limitations do not violate the students’ right to freedom of expression. These limitations apply to regular school days and any school-sponsored activities. Students may be required to wear certain types of clothing while participating in physical education classes, technical education classes, extracurricular activities, or other situations where special attire may be required to ensure the health or safety of the student.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Board authorizes the Superintendent or designee to monitor student dress and appearance and enforce school rules and regulations prohibiting student dress or appearance practices which: 1. Present a hazard to the health or safety of the student or to others. 2. Materially interfere with school work, create disorder, or disrupt the educational program. 3. Cause excessive wear or damage to school property. 4. Prevent the student from achieving educational objectives because of blocked vision or restricted movement. The Superintendent or designee shall ensure that all rules and regulations implementing this Policy impose only the minimum necessary restrictions on the exercise of the student's style and individuality.

 

Guidelines

Each school’s dress code shall be gender-neutral. Schools cannot enforce specific attire based on gender. Students have the right to dress in accordance with their gender identity within the constraints of the dress codes adopted by the school. Exceptions to the dress code may be made by the Superintendent or designee for medical or religious reasons when required by law.
