Purpose

 

To ensure the appropriateness, consistency, and continuity of instruction, the Board requires all professional staff members to appropriately plan and prepare to deliver the classroom instruction they have been assigned to deliver.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop and implement Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy, which may include, but need not be limited to, requiring professional staff to develop lesson plans to reflect their preparation to deliver effective classroom instruction.
