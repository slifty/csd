Definition

 

For purposes of this Administrative Regulation and the accompanying Board Policy, personnel file includes the following information, if retained by the District: (1) application for employment; (2) wage or salary information; (3) notices of commendations, warning or discipline; (4) authorization for a deduction or withholding of pay; (5) fringe benefit information; (6) leave records; (7) employment history with the employer, including salary information, job title, dates of changes, retirement record, attendance records and performance evaluations. Personnel files do not include: (1) records of an employee relating to the investigation of a possible criminal offense; (2) letters of reference; (3) documents which are being developed or prepared for use in civil, criminal or grievance procedures; (4) medical records; (5) materials which are used by the employer to plan for future operations; or (6) information available to the employee under the Fair Credit Reporting Act.

 

Maintenance of Personnel Files

 

Information to be placed in an employee's personnel file includes information that pertains to the professional role of the employee and is submitted by duly authorized school administrative personnel, the Board, or the Department of Education; diplomas and transcripts issued by academic institutions; and criminal history and child abuse certifications.

 

Medical records shall be kept in a separate file from the employee’s personnel file.

 

Personnel files shall be available to the Board only as required in the performance of its designated functions.

 ​​​​​

Personnel files shall be maintained and destroyed in accordance with the District's standard record retention policies and procedures.

 

Employee Access

 

An employee (or their designated agent) has the right to inspect their personnel file, as defined above, and in accordance with and subject to the below guidelines.

 

Absent reasonable cause for more frequent inspection, inspection shall be limited to once per calendar year.

 

Employees who wish to inspect their personnel file shall submit a request in writing to the Director of Human Resources. To assist the District in providing the correct records to meet the employee's request, the employee shall indicate in their written request either the purpose for which the inspection is requested, or the particular parts of their personnel file to which they wish to inspect or have inspected by their agent.

 

Upon a proper written request, the Director of Human Resources shall schedule a mutually convenient time during normal business hours for the employee or their designated agent to inspect their personnel file. Such inspection will take place in the presence of the Director of Human Resources or designee. No alterations or additions to any documents contained in the personnel file are permitted, and the employee inspecting their personnel file (or their designated agent) may not remove any materials from their file. The employee or their designated agent is permitted to take their own notes while inspecting their personnel file, and may make copies of any records at a cost of 25 cents per page for each page in excess of 25 pages.

 

Appeals

 

Employees wishing to appeal material in their personnel file may submit a request in writing to the Director of Human Resources. The request shall include, at a minimum, the employee's name, date, material to be appealed, and the reason/basis for the appeal.

 

The Director of Human Resources or designee shall hear the appeal and make a determination. Such determination may be appealed to the Superintendent.

 

An employee who is not satisfied with an evaluation or observation report has the right to prepare a formal statement and have that statement attached to all copies of the evaluation or observation report in question.
