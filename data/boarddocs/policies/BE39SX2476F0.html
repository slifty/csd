Regulation of Off-Campus/After Hours Speech

Off-campus or after hours expression is governed by the accompanying Board Policy if the student expression involved constitutes unprotected expression as stated in the Policy, and further provided the off-campus or after hours expression:

 

(1) does or is likely to materially or substantially interfere with the educational process, including school activities, school work, or discipline and            order on school property or at school functions;

(2) threatens material harm to the school or community;

(3) encourages unlawful activity; or

(4) interferes with another's rights.

 

Examples of off-campus and/or after hours speech, expression or other behavior that may subject a student to school-based discipline include, but are not limited to:

  • Posting a threat on social media to inflict harm on the school or another individual at school, even if the post is made off-campus or during non-school hours;
  • Arranging a drug deal in school, even if the deal is executed off-campus or during non-school hours;
  • Arranging to fight or engage in an altercation with another student or individual in school, even if the fight or altercation takes place after school or away from school property.

 

Distribution of Nonschool Materials

 

Students who wish to distribute nonschool materials on school property are required to submit them for review to the building principal or designee at least two (2) school days in advance of planned distribution. The building principal shall forward a copy of the proposed materials to the Superintendent or designee for review.

 

Materials that are critical of the District shall not be censored or restricted solely on the basis of their viewpoint, and the review for unprotected expression shall be reasonable and not calculated to delay distribution.

 

If the nonschool materials contain unprotected expression as stated in the accompanying Board Policy, the building principal or designee shall notify the student(s) that they may not distribute the materials because the materials constitute a violation of Board Policy. Where the reviewer cannot show that the materials contain unprotected expression, such material may be distributed on school property, provided the student(s) distributing the materials comply with established time, place and manner regulations regarding distribution of nonschool materials.

 

An appeal of the reviewer's decision may be made to the Superintendent and then to the Board, in accordance with Board Policy.

 

The distribution of nonschool materials may only take place at designated places, times, and in designated manners, in order to ensure that such distribution does not interfere with the orderly operation of the schools.

 

Students who distribute nonschool materials in compliance with this provision may still be ordered to desist such distribution if the materials are later found to contain unprotected expression.

 

Students who distribute printed materials shall be responsible for clearing any litter that results from their activity and shall distribute materials at a time when they do not miss instructional time.

 

Posting of Nonschool Materials

 

If a school building has an area where individuals are allowed to post nonschool materials, students may post such items as well, if the materials do not constitute unprotected expression and the items are submitted for prior review in the same manner as if the students were going to distribute them. Such materials shall be officially dated, and the District may remove the materials within ten (10) days of the posting or at other reasonable, specified times.
