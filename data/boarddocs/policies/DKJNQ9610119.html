Authority

 

The Board has the authority to specify reasonable conditions under which personal necessity leave may be granted, the type of situations in which such leave will be permitted and the total number of days that may be used by an employee in any school year for such leave.

 

Guidelines

Personal Leave

Personal leave for administrative employees beyond that outlined in this Policy, if any, shall be in accordance with the applicable administrative compensation plan or any individual employment contract.

Personal leave for professional employees beyond that outlined in this Policy, if any, shall be in accordance with the applicable collective bargaining agreement between the District and the Cheltenham Education Association.

Personal leave for classified employees beyond that outlined in this Policy, if any, shall be in accordance with the applicable collective bargaining agreement between the District and the Business Employees' Council and/or any applicable Board-approved employee handbook or Board resolution. 

Bereavement Leave

District employees shall be entitled to five (5) days of paid leave as a result of the death of an immediate family member. Immediate family members include father, mother, brother, sister, son, daughter, husband, wife, parent-in-law, son-in-law, daughter-in-law, near relative who resides in the same household, or any person with whom the employee has made their home.

District employees shall be entitled to one (1) day of paid leave, on the day of the funeral, as a result of the death of a near relative. Near relatives include a first cousin, grandfather, grandmother, grandchild, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, brother-in-law, or sister-in-law.

Note: To the extent that the provisions of this Board Policy are in conflict with the provisions of any applicable administrative compensation plan, employment contract, collective bargaining agreement, Board-approved employee handbook, or Board resolution, the terms of the applicable administrative compensation plan, employment contract, collective bargaining agreement, Board-approved employee handbook, or Board resolution shall control.
