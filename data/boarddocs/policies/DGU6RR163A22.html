Purpose
The Board recognizes the educational value of and supports student participation in school-sponsored trips (excluding field trips), which may from time to time include educational visits to foreign countries and schools. However, the safety and welfare of the students, staff members, and any chaperones are a top priority of the District. The purpose of this Policy is to govern student travel other than field trips, as that term is defined in Board Policy 121 (Field Trips).

 

Delegation of Responsibility
All trips governed by this Policy require prior written approval by the Superintendent or designee. The Superintendent or designee shall be provided with all logistical details regarding transportation, accommodations, cost, fundraising required of students, provisions for student supervision and safety, and the educational value of the trip in order for the Superintendent or designee to make a decision regarding approval of the trip.

 

Guidelines
In the case of school-sponsored trips, visits or exchanges governed by this Policy, the Board delegates to the Superintendent or designee the right to cancel, postpone, modify or terminate any trip in the event of war, acts of terrorism, natural disasters, or other circumstances which lead the Superintendent or designee to reasonably believe that not doing so might endanger the students or staff or not be in the best interests of the District. Any school-sponsored trip, visit or exchange that is cancelled, postponed, modified, or terminated shall be communicated to the Board.As voluntary participants in school-sponsored trips governed by this Policy, students are expected to comply with all rules set forth in advance of any trip, as well as all rules set forth in Board Policy, the Student Handbook, or any applicable Code of Conduct. Student conduct in violation of these provisions will be subject to discipline in accordance with Board Policy, the Student Handbook, and applicable law. Participation in school-sponsored trips governed by this Policy is a privilege, not a right, and may be revoked or denied to any student who violates Board Policy, the Student Handbook, or any applicable Code of Conduct.In addition to approval by the Superintendent or designee, all overnight trips or trips outside of the United States must receive authorization by the Board prior to departure.The school principal will make any recommendations regarding the cancellation, postponement, modification or termination of a school-sponsored trip to the Superintendent or designee prior to the date of trip departure. This recommendation will include information obtained from appropriate agencies and individuals and the basis for the principal’s recommendation.
