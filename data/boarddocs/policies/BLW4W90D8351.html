Exemption from﻿ Specific Instruction

 

Parents/guardians of District students (or students, if they are at least 18 years of age) may review information about the District curriculum, including academic standards to be achieved, instructional materials, and assessment techniques in accordance with Board Policy 105.1.

 

P﻿arents/guardians (or, students, if applicable) may request that their child be excused from specific instruction conflicting with their religious beliefs by making a written request to the Assistant Superintendent or designee. The request must clearly identify the specific instruction from which the student is to be excused and state that the specific instruction for which an excusal is sought is in conflict with their religious beliefs.

 

It shall not be the responsibility of the D﻿istrict or any of its employees to ensure that a student exercises their right to be excused in accordance with a parental/guardian (or student, if applicable) request. I﻿t shall be the responsibility of the student to request permission to leave class when the specific instruction objected to is presented.

 

Students who are exempted from specific instruction pursuant to this Administrative Regulation and the accompanying Board Policy will be assigned alternate activities, as determined by the principal or designee, in their sole discretion, and in a way that does not require the provision of extra District resources.

 

The building principal or designee shall determine where a student who has been exempted from specific instruction shall report during the time the student is excused. All students excused from specific instruction shall still be required to achieve the academic standards established by the District as necessary for graduation.

 

Exemption from State Assessments

 

Parents/guardians of District students (and students, if they are at least 18 years of age) may review state assessments at least two weeks prior to the school’s administration of such assessment (or a shorter period of time, only if the state assessment is not yet available at the school building), to determine whether it conflicts with their religious beliefs. The assessment must be reviewed on District property, at a location designated by the District, and D﻿istrict personnel must be present at the time of review. Arrangements to review state assessments may be made by contacting the Assistant Superintendent or designee. Parents/guardians (or students, if applicable) will be required to sign a confidentiality agreement as a condition of reviewing a state assessment. Photocopying, duplicating, or writing down the content of any state assessment or the accompanying test directions or instructions during such review is prohibited.

 

If, upon inspection, a parent/guardian (or student who is at least 18 years of age) finds a state assessment to be in conflict with their religious beliefs and wishes that their child be excused from the assessment, the right of the parent/guardian (or student, if applicable) will not be denied upon written request to the Superintendent or designee that states the objection.

 

Requests for a student to be excused from participation in a state assessment based on a religious objection will not be accepted from parents/guardians (or students, if applicable) who have not reviewed the specific assessment for which excusal is sought in person, in advance. Students who are exempt from participation in a state assessment are still required to attend school on the day(s) when such assessment is administered. The building principal or designee shall determine where a student who has been exempted from a state assessment shall report during the time the student is excused and the assignments that will be required of the student during such period.
