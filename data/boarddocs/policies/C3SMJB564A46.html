Guidelines

 

Students are reminded that school rules and all applicable Board Policies and Administrative Regulations are in effect, and students are subject to discipline: (1) while at school; (2) at school-sponsored events, trips, or activities, whether on or away from school property; (3) while traveling to/from school, school-sponsored events, trips, or activities on a vehicle owned, leased, or under the control of the District; and (4) as otherwise permitted by law. 

 

Elementary Schools

 

Guidelines detailing expected behavior and the consequences for failing to act in accordance with such guidelines are set forth in the document attached hereto as Attachment A. 

 

Secondary Schools

 

Guidelines detailing expected behavior and the consequences for failing to act in accordance with such guidelines are set forth in the document attached hereto as Attachment B. 

 

The documents attached hereto as Attachment A and Attachment B do not necessarily include, and should not be read or understood to include, every possible behavior that might be exhibited in violation of Board Policy, Administrative Regulations or rules promulgated at the school building level, but rather are written to contemplate the more common and serious types of behavior that may be experienced in a school environment. 

 

Discipline or other measures imposed for infractions of Board Policy, Administrative Regulations, or school rules not explicitly defined in the accompanying Attachment A or Attachment B shall be at the discretion of the Superintendent or designee in accordance with applicable law. 

 

Discipline of Students with Disabilities

 

Federal law details specific procedures and limitations that must be followed when imposing discipline on students with disabilities.  These procedures are detailed at length in Administrative Regulation 233 (Suspension and Expulsion).  Questions regarding discipline of students with disabilities should be directed to the Director of Special Education.
