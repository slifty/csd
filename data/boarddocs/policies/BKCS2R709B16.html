Purpose

Prudent trusteeship of District resources dictates that individuals responsible for the safekeeping of District funds be bonded.
 

Authority

 

The Board directs that the District be indemnified against loss of money by bonding each individual required to be bonded by Board Policy or applicable law. The Board shall bear the cost of bonds for designated individuals. ﻿

 

The amount of each bond shall be commensurate with the financial responsibility of the designated individual's positions. Enumeration and valuation on such bonds shall be determined annually.

 

All District employees not required by law to be bonded shall be covered under a blanket bond paid for by the District.
