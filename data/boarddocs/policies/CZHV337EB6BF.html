Purpose

The Board shall annually, by a majority vote of the entire Board, designate one or more banks or bank and trust companies as depository for the safeguarding of District funds.

 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Board directs the Director of Business Services or designee to periodically obtain quotations for banking services prior to its designation of depositories.

 

Authority
Each designated depository shall furnish proper security for such deposits in the amount designated by the Board and in accordance with law. 

 

Each designated depository shall be advised not to cash checks payable to the District, but to deposit said checks to the District account(s).

 

Each depository shall be required to report monthly to the Treasurer or Board monthly on the status of such funds in the matter required by law.
