Background

 

As indicated in the Board’s Equity Statement, which can be viewed in its entirety in the accompanying Board Policy, the Board has articulated that the success of the Cheltenham community is dependent upon effectively addressing equity challenges.  The Board committed to dismantling institutional barriers by adopting policies and practices and strategically allocating resources that support access and opportunity for each and every student.  To this end, the Superintendent or designee was directed to develop and disseminate Administrative Regulations implementing the accompanying Board Policy addressing the topics listed below.

  1. The equitable administration of school-based discipline at all District schools based on a model of restorative practices rather than punitive measures, when appropriate.
  1. The maintenance of high academic standards and expectations for every student, and the provision of multiple pathways to success in order to support the achievement of every student based on their individual needs.
  1. Ensuring equitable access to academic programs, rigorous coursework options, and athletic, extracurricular, and co-curricular offerings to every student, especially the most historically underperforming and challenged learners such as students of color, students with disabilities, students with limited English language proficiency, and economically disadvantaged students.
  1. The provision of resources, materials, and assessments that reflect the diversity of the District’s students and which are geared toward the understanding and appreciation of individual cultural uniqueness and differences.
  1. The promotion and maintenance of welcoming and inclusive school environments.
  1. Meaningfully engaging and empowering students, their families, and members of the Cheltenham Township community to be partners in the District’s efforts to provide an equitable experience for every student. This shall include, among other methods, periodically soliciting feedback from: (1) a cross section of District students regarding their experiences, sense of inclusion, and how their needs are being met; and (2) other members of the Cheltenham Township community.
  1. Recruiting, employing, supporting, and retaining a workforce that is culturally responsive and reflective of the diversity of the Cheltenham community.
  1. The provision of meaningful professional development for District employees, Board members, and, where possible, independent contractors, to strengthen their knowledge and skills in areas such as cultural proficiency, equity, inclusion, implicit and explicit bias awareness, and social justice. Such professional development sessions should offer opportunities for self-reflection and the receipt of feedback on how to improve upon carrying out the Board’s equity statement and supporting the success of every student.
  1. Ongoing review, evaluation, and modification of policies and practices that perpetuate inequitable outcomes for students based on an annual review of District qualitative and quantitative data.
  1. Periodic reporting to the Board and community stakeholders on the success of the District’s equity initiatives, progress towards achieving the goals of this Policy, and emerging needs.

 

Administrative Regulations

  1. School-Based Discipline

The equitable administration of school-based discipline focused on restorative practices rather than punitive measures, when appropriate, is a key component of the District’s equity work.  To achieve this goal, the District has done, and will continue to support, the following:

    • Proactive implementation of positive behavioral interventions and supports (PBIS), which is designed to model positive behavior and behavior strategies for students, with the goal of preventing inappropriate behavior, as opposed to punishing for such behavior when it occurs;
    • Periodic and ongoing review of Board Policy and procedures related to school-based discipline to ensure alignment with the District’s equity goals;
    • Implementation of alternatives to exclusionary discipline, when appropriate;
    • Utilization of school-based discipline data to proactively address behavioral challenges;
    • Implementation of professional development on the appropriate utilization of restorative practices;
    • Coordination between and with administrators, school building officials, and school safety officers to ensure the consistent application of safety-related policies and procedures; and
    • Implementation and maintenance of a youth court at Cheltenham High School.
  1. Academic Standards / Pathways to Success

The District is committed to the maintenance and implementation of high academic standards and expectations for all students with fidelity and in an equitable manner.  The Office of Education will work on an ongoing basis to ensure that the curriculum that is written, adopted, and implemented is consistent with the high academic standards that have been established for all students.  The following steps shall be taken to accomplish these objectives:

  •  Administering annual benchmark exams to collect and monitor formative data on student achievement;
  • Utilizing the Danielson Framework for Teaching as a guide to observe and evaluate Planning and Preparation, Classrooom Environment, Instruction, and Professional Responsibilities for teachers and administrators;
  • Conducting regular walkthroughs of classrooms and buildings to assess the implementation of the Danielson Framework;
  • Collecting and revisiting student achievement data through LinkIt! and building based data protocols;
  • Establishing a curriculum cycle for each content area and providing an annual update through the Educational Affairs Committee;
  • Conducting a multi-tier review by teachers and administrators that demonstrate all curriculum documents prior to adoption are aligned to PA state standards; and
  • Conducting a multi tier review by teachers and administrators that demonstrate all curriculum documents reflect diversity materials and instructional practices.
  1. Access to Academic Programs / Coursework & Athletic/Extracurricular/Co-Curricular Offerings

The District will work to ensure that course barriers (i.e. prerequisites) that prevent students from participation in higher-level courses are eliminated where possible.  The following steps shall be taken to accomplish this objective:

  • Regularly reviewing course descriptions;
  • Maintaining updated and easily accessible course catalogs;
  • Continuing to implement the current mathematics course sequence that provides all students access to courses that are prerequisites to higher-level courses;
  • Maintaining collaborative efforts to consult with community and university partners to prepare all students for participation and sustainability in higher-level courses;
  • Supporting coursework updates that minimize tracking not substantiated by data;
  • Providing ongoing professional development opportunities for teachers, counselors, and administrators;
  • Creating and following through with communication opportunities to ensure that all students, parents, and guardians are aware of the District's offerings; and
  • Promoting teacher, counselor, and administrator collaboration to identify and support students who qualify but do not elect to participation in higher-level course.

The District will continue to adopt and implement curriculum on a District-wide basis to ensure all students have access to grade-level material of their local and statewide peers that is consistent with applicable state standards.  The following steps shall be taken to accomplish this objective:

  • Establishing a curriculum cycle for each content area and provide an annual update through the Educational Affairs Committee;
  • Conducting a multi-tier review by teachers and administrators that demonstrate all curriculum documents prior to adoption are aligned to PA state standards; and
  • Conducting a multi-tier review by teachers and administrators that demonstrate all curriculum documents reflect diversity materials and instructional practices.
  1. Diversity of Resources, Materials, and Assessments

The Office of Education will identify and recommend programs, materials, and resources that are aligned with Pennsylvania’s academic standards and reflect the District’s commitment to representation and equity in all classrooms. 

 

The Office of Education will ensure that a process is maintained for reviewing and approving new resources and materials before, during, and after their use to ensure their alignment and implementation in accordance with the accompanying Board Policy. 

 

The Office of Education will continue the ongoing review of curriculum documents and supplemental materials to ensure consistency with the District’s goals and objectives.

  1. Welcoming and Inclusive School Environments

To promote and maintain welcoming and inclusive school environments the following steps will be taken:

    • Family ambassadors will be appointed to assist with creating an environment where families new to the District feel respected, valued, and supported by all school personnel;
    • Physical displays at schools will feature a diverse representation of the student and staff population;
    • The District website will be developed and updated to represent and highlight the District’s diverse student and population;
    • District orientation sessions will specifically highlight the District’s efforts designed to ensure an inclusive school environment;
    • Efforts will be made when developing the school calendar to acknowledge and honor holidays and celebrations of various cultures and diverse populations; and
    • Translators and/or translation services will be available upon request for linguistically diverse families who would benefit from such services to be able to fully participate in school affairs.
  1. Engaging and Empowering Students, Families, and Community Stakeholders

The District strives to meaningfully engage students, their families, and interested stakeholders in the District’s efforts to provide an equitable experience for every student.  The following strategies will be utilized to encourage such engagement:

    • Inviting participation in surveys and focus groups on various topics, with subsequent data review, and recommended actions steps that are supported by the data;
    • Encouraging students to participate in District governance matters where appropriate (i.e. attendance at Board and Committee meetings, student representatives to the School Board);

 

Efforts shall be made to ensure that when feedback is sought from stakeholders on matters of importance, efforts be implemented to ensure that a diversity of opinions and perspectives are sought and considered.

  1. Recruitment and Retention of Diverse, Culturally Responsive Workforce

The Human Resources Department implements strategies to recruit, hire, and retain the best talent, reflecting the diversity of the Cheltenham community as follows:

 

Recruitment

  • Attending diversity career fairs (i.e. DVCEE Annual Educators of Color Recruitment Fair, MCIU Diversity Job Fair);
  • Working with organizations that support diversity in education (i.e. The Fellowship Black Male Educators Consortium);
  • Networking with local colleges and historically black colleges and universities (HBCU’s);
  • Partnering with universities and colleges with “grow your own” programs for students and support staff (i.e. Arcadia dual enrollment program and Aspiring to Educate (A2E)/Temple University); and
  • Maintaining membership with the Montgomery County Recruitment Outreach (MCRO) through the MCIU.  (MCRO is a recruitment consortium of Human Resources Directors from school districts in Montgomery County who are dedicated to the recruitment and employment of diverse skilled and talented educators.)

Hiring

  • Reviewing candidates to ensure commitment to concepts of Board Policy 829 (Equity);
  • Constantly reviewing hiring processes and procedures to ensure alignment with concepts of Board Policy 829 (Equity); and
  • Including cultural proficiency questions are embedded in interview process for all positions; and
  • Ensuring that new staff induction programs include sessions on cultural proficiency and equity.

Retention

  • Sponsoring meet and greets during the school year to support teachers of color; and
  • Implementing and maintaining a mentorship program for new teachers of color.
  1. Professional Development

Consistent with the accompanying Board Policy, professional development sessions delivered to staff and other interested stakeholders will offer opportunities for self-reflection and the receipt of feedback on how to improve upon carrying out the Board’s equity statement and supporting the success of every student.

 

Professional development offerings may be identified in collaboration with any educational consultants retained to assist the District with carrying out its equity work. 

 

Efforts will be made to ensure that professional development sessions can be accessed synchronously and asynchronously. 

 

Individuals with ideas on professional development opportunities that should be considered to address District needs are encouraged to provide such suggestions to the Assistant Superintendent or designee. 

  1. Ongoing Evaluation of Policies and Practices

Administration will work, in conjunction with the Board and the Policy Committee, to engage in an ongoing and continuous review of Board Policies and related procedures to ensure consistency with the District’s equity goals.  An equity audit may also be conducted, with prior approval of the Board, to assist with the evaluation of the District's progress towards meeting its goals related to equity and identifying new goals based on current data. 

  1. Internal and External Reporting

Reporting on the District’s progress in achieving its equity goals and emerging needs may be communicated in a number of informal and formal ways.  This could include, but is not necessarily limited to, presentations at Board and Committee meetings, information communicated in Board updates and/or community correspondences, and via strategic planning updates and progress reports.
