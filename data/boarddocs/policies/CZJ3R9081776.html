Purpose

 

The Board shall establish local graduation requirements and acknowledge each student's successful completion of the instructional program by awarding diplomas at a graduation ceremony. 

 

Authority

 

In addition to the local graduation requirements established by this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulations, the Board shall adopt the graduation requirements students must achieve in accordance with state law.

 

The Board shall award a high school diploma to every student enrolled in the District who meets the requirements of graduation established by the Board.  A student who has completed the requirements for graduation shall not be denied a diploma as a disciplinary measure, but the student may be denied participation in the graduation ceremony when their personal conduct so warrants, as determined by the Superintendent or designee.

 

The Board shall permit a student with a disability, whose Individualized Education Program (IEP) prescribes continued educational services, to participate in commencement ceremonies with their graduating class and receive a certificate of attendance, provided that the student has attended four (4) years of high school.  The Board shall issue a high school diploma to each student with a disability who completes the graduation requirements established by the Board or the goals established in the student's IEP, as determined by the student's IEP team.

 

A student may qualify for graduation by attending a District school part-time when lawfully employed part-time or when officially enrolled part-time in a postsecondary institution.

  

The fourth year of high school shall not be required for graduation if a student has completed all requirements for graduation and attends a postsecondary institution as a full-time student.

 

Guidelines

 

Beginning with the class of 2020, For the class of 2024, the Board requires that each student earn 27 credits in order to be eligible for graduation.  Beginning with the class of 2025, the Board requires that each student earn 28 credits in order to be eligible for graduation.  

 

Supports shall be provided to students experiencing educational instability to ensure that they graduate in a timely manner, in accordance with law and Board Policy. A graduation plan shall be developed to facilitate this process for students in grades nine (9) through twelve (12) who are experiencing educational instability.

 

Supports shall be provided to facilitate the on-time graduation of children of active duty military families in accordance with the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children, state law and Board Policy.

 

Pursuant to Pennsylvania law, which authorizes local school districts to establish programs for awarding high school diplomas to eligible veterans, the District hereby establishes such a program to award diplomas to eligible veterans. This program shall extend to veterans to the extent authorized by state law as presently enacted and as amended in the future. The Administrative Regulations accompanying this Policy prescribes the procedures for identifying veterans eligible to obtain a high school diploma and for the awarding of the high school diploma. The Policy also provides for the application process for said diploma and specifies the credentials required to receive the high school diploma.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall be responsible for:

 

1. The publication and distribution of graduation requirements to students and parents/guardians.

 

2. Counseling students regarding graduation requirements and providing timely warnings to students who are in danger of not fulfilling such requirements.

 

3. Assessing and accurately recording student attainment of academic standards and progress towards satisfying graduation requirements.

 

4. Development of a list of individuals who qualify for the award of a diploma.

 

5. Planning and executing graduation ceremonies that appropriately recognize this important achievement.
