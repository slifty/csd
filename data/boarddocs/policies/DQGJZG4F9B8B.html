Purpose
Unless otherwise required by law, the Board shall assume no responsibility for making its regular educational program available to District residents who are high school graduates or after the school term in which a student reaches the age of twenty-one (21) years.  The Board shall not be responsible for the continuing education of such residents in any other school district.
