As noted in the above chart, in the calculation of grade point average (GPA), Advanced Placement (AP) and Honors (H) courses will be assigned a maximum additional weight, as follows:

 

Advanced Placement (AP): 6 quality points maximum.

Honors (H): 5 quality points maximum.

College and Career Preparation (CCP): 4 quality points maximum.

 

Unweighted GPA

 

The Unweighted GPA is calculated by multiplying the number of credits earned for the course (“Earned Credit” (EC)) by the Quality Points (QP) for each course. The sum of the total points earned is divided by the sum of earned credit to determine Unweighted GPA.

 

Weighted GPA

 

The Weighted GPA is calculated by multiplying the Earned Credit (EC) by the Quality Points (QP) and adding the assigned Weight (W) for each course. The sum of the total points earned is divided by the sum of earned credit to determine the Weighted GPA.

GUIDELINES APPLICABLE TO ALL CLASSES

 

Medical and or Incomplete

 

A Medical or Incomplete in a course does not impact the GPA calculation. Once the course is completed, the grade earned is calculated into the GPA.

 

Credits Transferred from Other Institutions i.e. 2 - Year or 4 - Year Institutions

 

Students who obtain credits at an outside institution will have the course reflected on their transcript as Dual Enrollment with the name of the institution, name of the course, and a grade of Pass/Fail. These credits do not impact GPA calculation.

 

Students Transferring from Another High School

 

A student who transfers to Cheltenham High School from a high school within the United States, the student's final grades are included in the GPA calculation upon verification of attendance and documentation of the grades.

 

A student who transfers to Cheltenham High School from a high school outside of the United States, the student's final grades are not included in the GPA calculation but students receive credit for completed courses upon verification of attendance and documentation of the grades. The courses are listed on the final transcript with a grade of Pass/Fail.
