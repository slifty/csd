Purpose / Authority

 

The Board requires District employees, independent contractors and volunteers to comply with the identification and reporting requirements for suspected child abuse, as well as the training requirements for recognition and reporting of child abuse in order to comply with the Child Protective Services Law and the Public School Code.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy which provide clear direction regarding the identification and reporting of suspected child abuse, as required by law.
