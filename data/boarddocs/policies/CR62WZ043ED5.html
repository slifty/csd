Purpose

 

Meaningful and appropriately assigned homework contributes to student growth. Therefore, the quantity and quality of homework assignments are essential to their effectiveness. 

 

The purpose of homework assignments is to enrich classroom instruction and to provide additional opportunities for extra practice outside of school hours. 

 

Meaningful homework assignments provide opportunities for students to:

 

  1. Review, reinforce, and extend what they have learned in class;

 

  1. Receive feedback from teachers;

 

  1. Develop self-directed time management, study skills, and independent learning;

 

  1. Prepare for assessments;

 

  1. Experience differentiation or appropriate accommodations according to their needs; and

 

  1. Practice skills and habits that support post-secondary success


Guidelines

 

Some guiding principles for this phase of instruction are recommended.

 

  1. Homework will reflect the curriculum and course of study for each school.  

 

  1. Homework will not be utilized for punitive measures or as a means to modify student behavior.

 

  1. Students must complete homework. Parents/guardians and family members or other caregivers should not work on students’ homework assignments.

 

Students’ responsibilities include:

 

  1. Accessing assignments (print and/or electronically) and requesting clarity when necessary.

 

  1. Gathering and taking home all resources required to complete homework.

 

  1. Creating and maintaining a homework schedule and routine.

 

  1. Putting forth their best effort on all assignments, and ensuring that their completed homework is their own work product.
  2. Submitting homework by the due date. Advise family members and teachers in advance of any concerns with meeting deadlines.

 

Parent/Guardian / Family / Caregiver responsibilities include:

 

  1. Communicating with students and teachers about homework.

 

  1. Fostering a positive environment to support homework by being optimistic about homework and the learning process. 

 

  1. Supporting expectations for student effort and quality work with regular supervision.

 

  1. Identifying a workspace for students.

 

Teachers’ responsibilities include:
 

  1. Establishing and communicating the purpose of assignments for students and families.

 

  1. Creating a system for homework distribution, collection, and feedback from students and families.

 

  1. Posting all assignments and reviewing completed homework, including providing feedback, promptly.

 

  1. Modeling expectations and final products as appropriate.

 

  1. Providing timely communication about homework concerns to students and families.

 

  1. Ensuring that students have access to all resources required to complete assignments.

 

Principals’ responsibilities include:

 

  1. Confirming that homework is aligned with the District’s Policy.

 

  1. Ongoing communication with instructional staff.

 

  1. Supporting and observing homework implementation.

 

  1. Reinforcing expectations for healthy school and life balance for students. 

 

  1. Nurturing the school and family partnership.
