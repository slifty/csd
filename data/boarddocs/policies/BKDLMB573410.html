Guidelines

 

Public performances by students are subject to the following regulations and limitations. Questions regarding this Administrative Regulation should be directed to the building principal or the Assistant Superintendent.

 

Approval Process

 

Public performances shall be pre-approved by the building principal, who shall be responsible for ensuring that the appropriate number of chaperones have been identified to accompany and supervise students at such performance, and also the Assistant Superintendent or designee.

 

Parental permission is required before students may participate in any public performance.

 

Other Guidelines

 

No student, group of students, student organization, or District employee or contractor may receive compensation for a public performance of students in their capacity as representatives of the District. Nothing in this paragraph shall preclude the reimbursement of properly documented travel expenses incurred in connection with a public performance of students in accordance with Board Policy. Additionally, nothing in this paragraph shall preclude compensation made pursuant to an extra duty contract where supervision of, attendance at or participation in public performances of students is a component of the extra duty responsibility associated with the underlying extra duty contract. Additional exceptions to this restriction require the prior written approval of the Superintendent or designee.

 

Students shall be protected and guarded against exploitation. As such, no student may be compelled to participate in a public performance. Similarly, no student may be penalized or retaliated against in any way for failure to participate in a public performance. However, when public performances are scheduled as a regular part of a planned course of instruction taken for credit, students shall be informed in advance of their obligation to participate, and they will be excused from participation only in accordance with the rules and procedures governing school attendance.

 

Class time shall not be utilized for practicing or preparing for a public performance unless such performance contributes to the educational program or mission of the District, as determined by the building principal.

 

Dismissal from school during school hours for participation in public performances may be limited at the discretion of the building principal.

 

Students participating in school-sponsored public performances shall be allowed to make up missed classwork, assignments, and tests without penalty, so long as the student’s participation in the public performance and absence from school was pre-approved by the building principal.

 

The frequency of public performances by students may be limited at the discretion of the building principal, based upon the best interests of the District and its students and staff. The building principal is also authorized to cancel student participation in a public performance if necessary to protect the health, safety or welfare of District students or staff, or if doing so is determined to be in the best interests of the District.

 

Students are reminded that all school rules and applicable Board Policies and Administrative Regulations remain in effect while students are away from school property in connection with a public performance/event.

 

When students participate in competitive events, the District shall pay the expenses of the selected students and appropriate staff at the first level of outside-the-District participation. At higher levels of participation, the District shall pay staff expenses and substitute costs (providing appropriate staff are willing to sponsor and chaperone the students involved), but shall not pay for student costs.
