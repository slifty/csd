Student Teachers and Other Interns

Purpose

 

The Board recognizes its responsibility to support teacher training and the contributions that student teachers/interns can make to the District’s schools.

 

Authority

 

The Board encourages and authorizes the Superintendent or designee to arrange for the training and supervision of a reasonable number of student teachers/interns in the District’s schools.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Board directs the Superintendent or designee to establish procedures to implement this Policy and ensure that no District student is subjected to an excessive number of student teachers/interns.

 

Guidelines

 

The Board requires that student teachers/interns shall only be accepted from accredited institutions with which the District has a written cooperative agreement.
