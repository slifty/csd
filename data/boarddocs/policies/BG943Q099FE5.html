Purpose

 

The purpose of this Policy is to permit commercial advertisements on District property on a limited basis in accordance with the best interests of the District.

 

The Board also recognizes that in certain instances, and within certain limitations, it may be in the best interests of the District to enter into relationships with corporations, businesses, and community-based organizations for exclusive advertising rights.

 

This Policy does not, nor does it seek to, create a forum for all types of advertising or to provide a public forum for purposes of communication.  Rather, recognizing the special nature and function of the public-school setting, and only to the extent that it does not interfere with the same, the intent of this Policy is to make use of a limited portion of the District’s property to generate financial support from commercial sponsors for District programs and activities.

 

Definitions

 

Commercial advertising - promotion of products, services and other promotional opportunities.

 

Sponsorship - any payment of money or other economic benefit to the District in exchange for recognition.

 

Sponsorship agreement - a Board-approved agreement between the District and a private entity to provide private financial support for District programs and activities in exchange for advertising, signage, announcements and/or other promotional opportunities at District activities.

 

Guidelines

 

Limitations

 

Only commercial advertising, as defined herein, whether by for-profit or not-for-profit entities, is permitted. Noncommercial advertising is not permitted so that the District’s position of neutrality on political, religious and other public issues can be maintained.

 

In the absence of express written agreements with other organizations to the contrary, such as the Cheltenham School District Foundation or the Alumni Association of Cheltenham High School, all revenues generated from commercial advertising, sponsorship arrangements, and exclusivity agreements shall be used for the sole benefit of the District, shall be deposited into the District's general fund, and shall not be tendered with restrictions.

 

﻿Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Business Manager or designee shall review all proposed agreements to ensure compliance with this Policy and the additional guidelines outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulation.
