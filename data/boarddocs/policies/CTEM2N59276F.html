Purpose

The District's libraries function as an integral part of the total educational program and experience for students. A goal of the school libraries is to facilitate and expedite the attainment of an educational program and academic standards of high quality for each student. 

 

To attain this goal, the following objective gives purpose and direction to the library program: To provide an educationally functional and effective library program that fosters creativity, provides opportunity for personalization, and develops global, 21st century skills that lead to college and career readiness.
 

Guidelines

Objectives

Every school library has the primary purpose of contributing to the achievement of the objectives formulated by the District, of which it is an integral part. The general objectives of dynamic school library service are common to all schools. Each school librarian shall strive to:
 

  1. Participate effectively in the school program as it strives to create an instructional environment that allows personalization, engagement, and inquiry through active, technology-rich instructional strategies.
     
  2. Provide students with the library materials and services most appropriate and most meaningful for their growth and development as individuals.
     
  3. Stimulate and guide students in all phases of media access to essential learning and 21st century skills.
     
  4. Provide an opportunity through library experiences to develop and encourage student passion and interest.
     
  5. Help students become skillful users of libraries and associated media.
     
  6. Introduce students to community libraries as early as possible and cooperate with public libraries in their efforts to encourage continuing education and cultural growth.
     
  7. Serve on selection committees and District-wide curriculum committees in selection and use of all types of library materials that contribute to the learning program.
     
  8. Participate with teachers and administrators in programs for continuing professional and cultural growth of the school staff.
     
  9. Collaborate with school and public librarians and community leaders in developing an overall library program.

 

Criteria For Selection of Library Materials
The following criteria shall apply in the selection of library materials:

 

  1. Educational significance and relevance to the curriculum.

  2. Need and value to the collection.

  3. Reputation and significance of author or producer.

  4. Clarity, adequacy and scope of content.

  5. Validity, accuracy, objectivity, currentness, and appropriateness of content.

  6. Organization and presentation of content.

  7. High degree of readability and/or comprehensibility.

  8. High degree of potential user appeal.

  9. High artistic quality and/or literary style.

  10. Quality format.

  11. Value commensurate with cost.

  12. Culturally and socially appropriate content.

 

  1. Consistency with the Board's Equity Statement and the goals of the District's Equity Policy (Policy 829), especially with respect to the provision of resources and materials that reflect the diversity of the District’s students and which are geared toward the understanding and appreciation of individual cultural uniqueness and differences.

 

  1. Consistency with the requirements and philosophy outlined in this Policy.

 

Questioned Materials
The District recognizes the right of individuals to openly criticize and evaluate library materials that exists in the District's libraries.

All questions or concerns relating to such library materials should be submitted in writing to the Assistant Superintendent or designee.  The question or concern shall be as objective and specific as possible. The Assistant Superintendent or designee will generally respond within 15 business days. The individual submitting the question or concern may be asked to meet or speak with the Assistant Superintendent or designee is order to resolve the question/concern in a mutually agreeable manner.

The American Library Association affirms that all libraries are forums for information and ideas, and that the following basic policies should guide their services.

I. Books and other library resources should be provided for the interest, information, and enlightenment of all people of the community the library serves. Materials should not be excluded because of the origin, background, or views of those contributing to their creation.

II. Libraries should provide materials and information presenting all points of view on current and historical issues. Materials should not be proscribed or removed because of partisan or doctrinal disapproval.

III. Libraries should challenge censorship in the fulfillment of their responsibility to provide information and enlightenment.

IV. Libraries should cooperate with all persons and groups concerned with resisting abridgment of free expression and free access to ideas.

V. A person’s right to use a library should not be denied or abridged because of origin, age, background, or views.

VI. Libraries which make exhibit spaces and meeting rooms available to the public they serve should make such facilities available on an equitable basis, regardless of the beliefs or affiliations of individuals or groups requesting their use.

VII. All people, regardless of origin, age, background, or views, possess a right to privacy and confidentiality in their library use. Libraries should advocate for, educate about, and protect people’s privacy, safeguarding all library use data, including personally identifiable information.

In addition to the above, it is the belief of the Board that all people, regardless of their actual or perceived race, color, age, creed, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, ancestry, national origin/ethnicity, veteran status, marital status, handicap/disability, or membership in any other protected class, possess a right to privacy and confidentiality in their library use.
