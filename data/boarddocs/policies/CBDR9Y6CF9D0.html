The following guidelines are provided for the assignment and supervision of student teachers/interns:

 

  1. All requests from institutions for placement of student teachers/interns and participants in field experience or similar programs shall be directed to the Superintendent or designee. The Superintendent may remove or reassign a student teacher/intern from their placement if doing so is determined to be in the best interests of the District and/or District students.
  2. Requests will be considered and assignments made by the Superintendent or designee.
  3. Assignments shall be based on the availability of interested and qualified staff and contingent upon the approval of the principal.
  4. Student teachers/interns shall be placed with experienced teachers of demonstrated competence who have acquired tenure in Pennsylvania and have at least three (3) years of successful teaching experience, one of which has been in the Cheltenham School District.
  5. The assignment of student teachers/interns will ensure that no student or teacher has an excessive number of student teachers/interns.
  6. The principal and cooperating teacher shall meet with candidates for student teaching/interns prior to final acceptance and placement.
  7. No teacher shall be obliged to accept a student teacher/intern against their wishes.
  8. Final responsibility for all classroom instruction and management rests with the classroom teacher. The classroom teacher shall be responsible for determining final student grades. Student teachers/interns may not be left unsupervised for extended periods of time. The classroom teacher is to be present in the classroom for at least part of each period the student teacher is teaching.
  9. Student teachers/interns shall report to the principal.
  10. Student teachers/interns shall observe and abide by all applicable Board Policies, Administrative Regulations and school rules, and attend all meetings and activities which are required of the classroom teacher, unless alternative arrangements have been made with the principal or designee.
  11. Student teachers/interns shall comply with required health examinations.
  12. Student teachers/interns shall not be accepted into District schools until they have complied with the mandatory background check requirements for criminal history (both state and federal) and child abuse, and the District has evaluated the results of those screening processes.
  13. Student teachers/interns shall report, on the designated form, PDE – 6004 (Act 24), arrests and convictions as required by law. Failure to accurately report such arrests and convictions may, depending on the nature of the offense, subject the individual to criminal prosecution.

Arrest or Conviction

While serving in District schools, student teachers and interns shall use the designated form to report to the Superintendent or designee, within seventy-two (72) hours of the occurrence, an arrest or conviction required to be reported by law.

While serving in District schools, a student teacher or intern shall be required to report to the Superintendent or designee, in writing, within seventy-two (72) hours of notification, that they have been listed as a perpetrator in the statewide database, in accordance with the child protective services law.

A student teacher or intern shall be required to submit a current criminal history background check report if the Superintendent or designee has a reasonable belief that the student teacher or intern was arrested or has been convicted of an offense required to be reported by law, and the student teacher or intern has not notified the Superintendent or designee.

Failure to accurately report such arrests and convictions may subject the student teacher or intern to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal from the program, denial of future employment and criminal prosecution.
