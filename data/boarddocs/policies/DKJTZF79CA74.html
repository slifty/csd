Guidelines

 

The Superintendent delegates to the Director of Facilities and Maintenance the responsibility to efficiently and adequately manage, audit and maintain District buildings, grounds, and property. This responsibility shall include, but not be limited to, routine repairs and maintenance, equipment replacement, facilities auditing, and long-range planning for facility modernization, conditioning and upgrading.

 

The Director of Facilities and Maintenance shall develop and implement a planned program of preventive and routine maintenance, leveraging technology where possible and appropriate, to maximize performance and increase longevity of buildings, grounds, and property/equipment.

 

Building principals, in conjunction with the building-level Facilities and Maintenance Department staff, shall conduct a physical inspection of the building on a regular basis. Any concerns shall be reported in writing to the Director of Facilities and Maintenance or designee.

 

On an as-needed basis, the Director of Facilities and Maintenance shall prepare and provide or present to the Board information regarding the following items:

 

  • Updates to the five-year facilities plan;

 

  • A written description of existing facilities, including the current condition of the interior, exterior, and major systems, as well as a prioritized list of future maintenance, repairs, or upgrades;

 

  • Status of current maintenance/repair projects and initiatives;

 

  • Methods by which technology is being used in the Facilities and Maintenance Department to drive innovation, costs savings, and other staff/resource efficiencies. (i.e. School Dude Maintenance Software)

 

  • Recommendations regarding future maintenance needs/projects and/or utilization of staff or other resources in the Facilities and Maintenance Department; and

 

  • Other information requested by the Superintendent or Board.

 

The Director of Facilities and Maintenance shall ensure that any recommendations regarding additional or the changed utilization of staff or other resources that will have a budgetary impact are brought to the attention of the Board as soon as is practicable in order to ensure appropriate inclusion in the budget development process.
