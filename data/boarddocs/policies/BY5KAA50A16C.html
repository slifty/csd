Complaint Resolution Procedures

  1. The student or group of students is/are encouraged to first make the complaint known to the staff member most closely involved or, if none is identifiable, a counselor or building administrator.  The identified staff member, counselor, or building administrator shall make a good faith effort to informally resolve the complaint. If the student or group of students does/do not feel comfortable raising the complaint with the staff member most closely involved, they may skip this initial step and proceed to steps listed below.
  2. For complaints that remain unresolved, the student or groups of students shall prepare a written statement of their complaint which shall set forth, at a minimum, the following: (1) Specific nature of the complaint and a brief statement of relevant facts; (2) Manner and extent to which the student believes they have been adversely affected; (3) Relief sought; (4) Reasons why the student feels entitled to the relief sought; and (5) Previous steps taken/efforts made to resolve the complaint.
  3. The written complaint should then be submitted to the building administrator or designee, and then successively (if unresolved) to the Assistant Superintendent or appropriate central office administrator, the Superintendent, and ultimately to the Board, if appropriate, as determined by the Superintendent or designee.  At each level, the complaint shall be considered, and a written response shall be forthcoming, generally within fifteen (15) business days, unless the student(s) is/are advised that a longer period of time is needed in order to appropriately respond.  At each level the student shall be afforded the opportunity to be heard personally by the school authority, with the assistance of the student’s parent/guardian, if desired.​​​​​​ 

 

Confidentiality

 

In all phases of the complaint resolution process, reasonable efforts shall be made to maintain the confidentiality and protect the privacy of all parties. However, even if one or more of the parties have requested confidentiality, the request may not be able to be honored if the investigation of the complaint cannot reasonably be conducted without the identity of the parties being known.

 


Retaliation Prohibited

 

Retaliation against an individual who files a good faith complaint or who participates in or cooperates with an investigation is prohibited.

 

 


Intentionally False Accusations Prohibited

 

False complaints or false accusations contained in complaints can have serious effects on innocent persons. Therefore, it is prohibited to intentionally falsely accuse an individual of misconduct. Such intentionally false accusations will result in disciplinary action.
