Purpose

The Board takes seriously all concerns and complaints by employees. The purpose of this Policy is to provide a complaint procedure for those employees who are either not members of collective bargaining units or are members of collective bargaining units but have complaints that cannot be addressed through the collective bargaining agreement. If a specific complaint procedure is provided within any other Board Policy, the specific procedure in the applical Board Policy will be followed with respect to such a complaint. If a specific complaint procedure is not provided, the purpose of this Policy is to provide a procedure for general use.
 

Definition

A complaint refers to a claim by an employee based upon an event or condition under which that employee works, which the employee reasonably and in good faith believes is caused by a misinterpretation or inequitable application of Board Policy or administrative decisions not specifically covered by other Board Policies or the collective bargaining agreement, if any, which applies to that employee."
 

Guidelines

 

Complaint Procedure

An alleged complaint should be resolved informally, if at all possible, at the lowest administrative level, including by bringing such complaint to the employee’s direct/immediate supervisor. Available to employees, however, are direct avenues of appeal to higher administrative authority. To provide assured, unobstructed communication with those who have the power to respond to an alleged complaint and to contribute to constructive personnel relationships and ultimately, to a better school system, the Board delegates to the Superintendent the responsibility to promulgate specific procedures for handling complaints brought pursuant to this Policy which includes a mechanism for appeal to the Board.
