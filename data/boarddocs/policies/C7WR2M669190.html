Purpose 

The purpose of this Policy is to ensure that all crowdfunding campaigns conducted by District employees, relating to their roles as District employees, are conducted with prior written approval of the administration and in accordance with established procedures.

 

Definitions

Crowdfunding - the practice of funding a project or venture by raising monetary contributions from a large number of people, typically via the internet.

Campaign - a fundraising effort that is designed to raise funds to meet an advertised goal or need.
 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent shall promulgate Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy in accordance with the guidelines established below.
 

Guidelines


District employees, including coaches and activity sponsors, may not engage in crowdfunding campaigns related to their roles or duties as a District employee or otherwise benefiting the District, including District programs, initiatives, mission, students, or staff without prior written authorization from a designated administrator.

This Policy does not govern employee participation in purely private crowdfunding campaigns or initiatives that in no way involve the District, including its programs, initiatives, mission, students, or staff.

Approved crowdfunding campaigns must operate in compliance with all laws and all other Board Policies and Administrative Regulations.
