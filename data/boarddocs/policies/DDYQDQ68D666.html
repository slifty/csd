Guidelines

 

Postsecondary education institutions and military recruiters shall have access to secondary students’ names, addresses and telephone numbers, unless the parent/guardian (or a student who has reached eighteen (18) years of age) requests that such information not be released without prior written parental/guardian (or student, for a student who has reached eighteen (18) years of age) consent.

 

The building principal or designee shall annually notify parents/guardians, and students who have reached eighteen (18) years of age, of the right to request that student information not be released to representatives of postsecondary institutions and/or military recruiters without prior written consent. The letter attached as Attachment A may be utilized to notify parents/guardians/students as contemplated herein. 

 

The building principal or designee shall, upon request, provide a list of graduating seniors to military recruiters by the first day of the academic year of graduation. The names of students who have opted out of this disclosure shall not be included on this list.

 

It shall be within the discretion of the building principal or designee to determine under what conditions and when access to secondary students will be provided to representatives of postsecondary institutions, military recruiters and prospective employers.

 

Military recruiters and all other members of the active and retired Armed Forces, including the National Guard and Reserves, shall be permitted to wear their official military uniforms while on District property.
