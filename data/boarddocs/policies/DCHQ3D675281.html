Purpose

In order to promote cultural awareness and understanding and to provide diverse experiences to District students, the District may accept foreign exchange students for enrollment at Cheltenham High School and/or participate in international exchange study programs on a limited basis in accordance with this Policy.
 

Authority

The Board may accept foreign exchange students who meet the established guidelines for admission to District schools.

 

Students on J-1 Visa

The J-1 Visa is one of two types of visas that eligible high school students can obtain in order to participate in a student exchange program﻿. The J-1 visa program works by authorizing a private, non-profit organization to issue the required forms that enable students to obtain a J-1 visa.

 

Exchange students on a J-1 Visa shall not be required to pay tuition. However, the district will not fund any additional tuition expenses for exchange students residing in the District who attend outside placements such as charter schools to the extent it is not required to do so by law. The Board reserves the right to limit the number of foreign exchange students admitted to the District.

Students on F-1 Visa

The F-1 Visa is one of two types of visas that eligible high school students can obtain in order to participate in a student exchange program﻿. The F-1 visa program works by authorizing either schools (whether public or private) to issue the required forms that enable students to obtain a the F-1 visa (the I-20 Form).

 

The District is not registered to issue I-20 Forms.  As such, the Board cannot accept students on an F-1 visa, unless the enrollment can be processed without the issuance of an I-20 form and the student otherwise meets all requirements for enrollment in the District.
 

Delegation of Responsibility
 

The Superintendent designee shall be responsible for determining the visa status and eligibility of foreign exchange students applying for admission to District schools. The Superintendent or designee shall further be responsible for making the final decision regarding the admission of foreign exchange students to District schools, subject to the Board's authority to limit the number of such students admitted to District schools in any particular year.

 

All potential organizations or individuals applying for admission shall forward the request to the Superintendent or designee by July 1 preceding the school year of attendance.

 

The Superintendent shall develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy which, at a minimum, outline the criteria that foreign exchange students must meet in order be considered for admission to District schools.
