Purpose

 

District employees set an example in dress and appearance for students to follow. Employees' dress and appearance should reflect their professional status and encourage respect for authority in order to have a positive influence on the District's programs and operations.

 

Authority

 

The Board has the authority to specify reasonable dress and appearance guidelines, in accordance with law, for all District employees to prevent an adverse impact on the educational programs and District operations.

 

Guidelines

 

While engaged in work-related duties, employees shall present themselves in a physically clean, neat, well-groomed, and professional manner.

 

E﻿mployees should dress in a manner that is appropriate for their work assignment and that is conducive to the educational environment.

 

Designated employees may be required to wear a work uniform and/or utilize safety gear when performing assigned duties.

 

The Board recognizes that work assignments and activities vary between employees and from day to day, requiring individuals responsible for enforcing this Policy to use common sense and good judgment as to what is appropriate attire under the circumstances.

 

The requirements of this Policy, as well as those outlined in the accompanying Administrative Guidelines, apply to non-District employed aides and other professionals while performing services to the District.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall implement Administrative Regulations consistent with applicable law and in accordance with this Policy.

 

If an employee feels that an exception to this Policy would enable them to carry out assigned duties more effectively, a request should be made in accordance with the procedure outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulation.
