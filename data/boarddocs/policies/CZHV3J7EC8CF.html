Introduction

 

A petty cash fund shall be formally established through the Business Office to enable purchase of incidental goods and services by authorized personnel. A fund, with a beginning balance of $300 will be maintained at the Administration Building for all petty cash needs. The accounts payable clerk will maintain the petty cash fund.

 

  1. Petty Cash Accounts
     
    1. The individual charged with the control of a petty cash fund is responsible to see that the fund at all times equals the original amount, which may be represented either by cash, petty cash vouchers, or invoices which add to that amount.

      Petty cash funds are audited periodically to ensure appropriate use of funds and to verify balances. Any audit discrepancies or disputes regarding the appropriateness of the use of petty cash for a particular purpose or transaction shall be resolved by the Director of Business Services.
       
    2. Petty cash purchases must be pre-approved by the administrator responsible for the purchase. If an emergency situation arises and an item must be purchased without a petty cash advance, it is up to the discretion of the Director of Business Services or designee to approve reimbursement of the cash spent.
       
    3. Replenishment of the petty cash fund shall be periodically requested through the Business Office on a form listing all individual receipts, the appropriate account number to charge and the amount. This is then totaled, the cash remaining in the petty cash is noted for verification of the initial cash balance, and the form is signed by the person responsible for the petty cash before submitting to the Director of Business Services or designee for replenishment.
       
    4. All petty cash funds must be kept in a secured place in the District Administration Building with restricted access to the funds.
       
    5. Departments are encouraged to use purchase orders whenever possible, and should not rely on petty cash reimbursement as a routine alternative to making purchases with purchase orders.
       
    6. All petty cash accounts must be brought to the initial established value by June 30 of each year, therefore reimbursement for any receipts still outstanding must be requested by June 30.
       
    7. Petty cash is not to be co-mingled with other money, such as school sales, etc.
       
    8. Petty cash is not additional budget money, but rather is money that is advanced, and when reimbursed, purchases are charged against petty cash.
       
  2. Limits on Spending
     
    1. Petty cash purchases pre-approved by the administrator responsible for the purchase may not exceed thirty five ($35) dollars. Petty cash purchases that exceed thirty five ($35) dollars, but are equal to or less than fifty ($50) dollars may be made with the prior approval of the Director of Business Services. Incremental purchasing (splitting one large purchase into several small purchases) is not allowed.
       
  3. Tax Liability
    1. Cheltenham School District is a tax-exempt entity. Tax paid by individuals using petty cash will not be reimbursed.

 

  1. Unallowable Expenses from the Petty Cash Fund
     
    1. Petty cash funds may not be used for check cashing or IOU substitutions.
       
    2. Unallowable expenses include the following:
       
      1. Alcoholic beverages.
         
      2. Political contributions.
         
      3. Contributions to not-for-profit organizations.
         
      4. Personal expenses of any kind.
         
      5. Personal gifts (except recognition activities, including purchase of awards to recognize retirement, service, or meritorious performance by employees and students).
         
      6. Activities whose primary purpose is entertainment.
         
      7. Activities which have been or may be reimbursed from an external source.
         
      8. Flowers for employees, students, Board members, or their families on such occasions as birth, death, or serious illness.
         
      9. Requests inadequately documented or not approved.
         
    3. Networking hardware/software cannot be purchased with petty cash.
       
    4. Promotional advertising cannot be paid out of the petty cash fund.
       
    5. Rental or leases of equipment or buildings cannot be purchased with petty cash.
       
    6. Purchases not allowed under petty cash purchases should be requisitioned using the District’s purchasing procedures.
       
    7. Petty cash is not to be used for travel reimbursements.
       
  2. Policy Violations
     
    1. Any employee who violates this Administrative Regulation or the accompanying Board Policy shall be subject to discipline, up to and including possible termination from employment.
