Application

 

Requests for sabbatical leaves, which include the required documentation, shall be made to the Superintendent or designee on or before April 1 for the following school year and October 1 for the following semester. (Exceptions to the prior application requirement will be made for sabbatical leave for restoration of health only.)

 

Each application for sabbatical leave for professional development shall be reviewed by the Superintendent or designee and acted upon by the Board.

 

The written request for sabbatical leave for professional development must state the dates of the requested leave.

 

Commitment of Employee

 

A sabbatical leave for health or professional development may be taken for the period approved. Employees who are granted sabbatical leaves shall not be engaged in gainful employment during the period of leave, except as permitted by a leave of absence for classroom occupational exchange or in the case of a de minimis employment impact as determined by the District (which may include compensable graduate school-related employment, including but not limited to graduate student assistantships, teacher assistantships, and fellowships). A sabbatical leave and/or leave of absence granted to regular employees shall also operate as a leave of absence from all other school activities, including those performed on a volunteer basis.

 

No leave of absence shall be granted to an employee unless they agree to return to employment with the District for a period of not less than one (1) school term following the sabbatical. If the employee fails to return to service, unless prevented by illness or physical disability, the employee shall forfeit all benefits and compensation for the period of the leave.

In such case, the employee shall forfeit all benefits to which said employee would have been entitled for the balance of the sabbatical leave and/or shall reimburse the District for all salary and benefits received by the employee during the sabbatical leave.

 

Commitment of Employer

 

Time on sabbatical leave for health or professional development leave shall be counted as time on the job as provided by the Public School Code. Employer is under no obligation to accommodate or facilitate any component of the employee’s professional development plan or related coursework which may include, but is not limited to, access to school students, staff, or D﻿istrict resources.

 

Compensation

 

During the period of sabbatical leave for health or professional development, an employee shall be compensated at the minimum amount specified by provisions of the Public School Code in effect at the time the sabbatical leave commences. While on leave, the employee shall also be entitled to insurance benefits provided other professional employees as though the employee were actually in regular daily attendance in the position from which the sabbatical leave was taken. Accordingly, the amount of the employee’s contribution to medical coverage shall be deducted from the compensation payable to the employee while on sabbatical leave.

 

Sabbatical Leave for Restoration of Health

 

Sabbatical leave for restoration of health falls into two categories: 1) those that can be anticipated and 2) those that are on an emergency basis. Request for the first type, which coincide with the semester calendar, must adhere to the deadlines above and require a certificate from the employee’s physician. The medical certification should state that the medical condition for which sabbatical leave is sought will be ameliorated through a period of rest and/or treatment for a semester or a year.

 

Requests for an emergency restoration of health sabbatical will be recommended to the Board as soon as feasible, after receipt of the request and a medical certification from the employee’s physician (as described above). The medical certification shall be completed by a licensed and authorized physician in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. The medical certification may not be provided by a nurse practitioner, chiropractor, or physician not having the area of required specialization in order to make the diagnosis. The District reserves the right to request supplemental medical information from the physician in order to assess the need for a sabbatical leave for restoration of health. In addition, the District reserves the right to have an independent medical examination in accordance with the provisions of the Public School Code of 1949.

 

At both the approximate midpoint of the leave and at least thirty (30) calendar days prior to the conclusion of the leave, a physician’s statement from a physician licensed in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania shall be submitted to the Superintendent or designee, indicating the extent to which the purpose of the leave has been achieved and evaluating the health status of the employee relative to their ability to return to employment. A fitness for duty certification may be required by the District.

 

Leave of Absence for Professional Development

 

All requests for sabbatical leave for professional development are subject to review and authorization by the Board. The Board has the sole authority to enforce the policy establishing the conditions for approval of such sabbaticals.

 

In reviewing the request on behalf of the Board for a sabbatical leave for the purposes of professional development, the Superintendent or designee shall evaluate the request based upon the following information, as well as all of the other requirements contained in this A﻿dministrative Regulation and the accompanying Board P﻿olicy, to be provided by the staff member who is making the request:

  • A rationale supporting the course of studies chosen for the sabbatical leave for professional development.
  • Evidence that the proposed course of studies is applicable to teaching in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania and is of sufficient depth and rigor to justify the leave for professional development.
  • A rationale supporting the leave for professional development as a better fit for the proposed course of studies and related activities when compared with full-time employment and course work:
    • The purpose of the leave for professional development should be to provide the opportunity to attend a program or series of courses that could not otherwise be completed while maintaining a full-time workload. For example, attendance at classes, interacting with other students, making use of a college library or information services facility and working with an instruction adviser are all activities that frequently require physical availability during what would otherwise be working hours. In addition, a leave for professional development could include an internship or practicum, or provide the opportunity to work on the data gathering or research involved in the development of research such as a professional publication or dissertation; and
  • The Superintendent’s or designee's evaluation of how the course of studies undertaken will help to support the needs and current priorities of the District.

A leave for professional development shall be granted only to staff members participating in a preapproved regional or nationally credited program from an organization given credence by the District administration in their sole and exclusive discretion and in the case of a required certification of academic program, recognized as an approved certification program by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania for: (1) the purpose of retaining a Pennsylvania professional certificate or Pennsylvania commission; (2) further preparation and improvement in their area(s) of certification that will have direct benefit to the District as determined by the Superintendent or designee; (3) additional certification recommended by the Superintendent or designee that will benefit students within the District; (4) attaining other appropriate and identifiable educational positions within the District as determined by the Superintendent or designee within their discretion; or (5) improving professional competency in the employee’s area of certification or directed area of certification. In approving a plan of professional development, the Superintendent or designee will take into account their independent assessment of the rigor of the proposed program and the perceived assessment of the quality of the program offered by the accredited institution within the Superintendent's or designee’s sole discretion.

 

The minimum requirements for leave for a half school term shall consist of any of the following or a combination thereof: nine (9) graduate credits; twelve (12) undergraduate credits; or one hundred eighty (180) hours of professional development activities. The minimum requirements for leave for a full school term shall consist of the following or a combination thereof: eighteen (18) graduate credits; twenty-four (24) undergraduate credits; or three hundred sixty (360) hours of professional development activities.

 

Requests for professional development leave shall be submitted to the Superintendent or designee on the approved form together with a detailed plan. The detailed plan shall include, but not be limited to, the course syllabi of the intended program and any relevant information regarding the proposed program requested by the Superintendent or designee. As a general principle, random courses will not be approved for a sabbatical leave. The applicant must demonstrate that they have applied to an approved program of studies leading to a master’s or doctoral degree. Participation in a Pennsylvania Department of Education - authorized certification program (as distinguished from receiving a certificate) that will result in the employee receiving a new certification in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania as recommended by the District administration would also be considered within the discretion of the Superintendent or designee.

 

Applicants who propose to take graduate or undergraduate credits shall submit notification of acceptance and enrollment by any of the aforesaid accredited institutions of higher learning for study in the courses preapproved by the Superintendent or designee. Upon completion of the sabbatical, the employee shall provide to the Superintendent or designee satisfactory evidence that the employee’s approved plan for professional development was fully complied with during the leave of absence, no later than one (1) month after return. If the employee fails to do so, unless prevented by illness or physical disability during a leave for professional development, the employee shall forfeit all benefits to which the employee would have been entitled to under the provisions of the School Code for the period of absence. Failure to receive passing grades shall be considered evidence that the employee did not fully comply with the employee’s approved plan for professional development.

 

The Superintendent or designee reserves the right to request additional information about the program or any modifications to the program while the employee is in the process of participating in the program.

 

Applicants who propose to undertake professional development activities without receiving credits shall submit to the Superintendent or designee on behalf of the Board a detailed written plan listing the specific activities and goals for the sabbatical. The Superintendent or designee shall have exclusive discretion to determine whether or not the proposed activities in the detailed written plan suffice for a sabbatical leave approval, which will include the rigor and the potential value of the program for the employee and the District in its implementation. As a general principle, the proposed professional development activities must have a demonstrable and substantive impact on the improvement of student outcomes, as well as the improvement of the skill sets of the employee that would add value to the employee’s current assignment and the curriculum of the District as determined by the Superintendent or designee. If approved, the employee shall submit monthly status reports to the Superintendent or designee substantiating compliance with the approved plan. Upon return from professional development leave, the employee shall submit to the Superintendent or designee within the first month a formal report describing the educational activities pursued and their benefits and relevancy. If the employee does not submit the report by the end of the month, a written reminder will be delivered to the employee. If the report is not submitted within ten (10) calendar days after the date of the reminder, the employee shall forfeit all benefits and compensation for the period of the leave.

 

The employee shall file a final written report with the Superintendent or designee concerning the professional benefits derived by the employee and the benefit to be shared with the others in the District. The final report shall be filed within sixty (60) calendar days after return to service.

 

If the employee fails to provide satisfactory evidence that they fully complied with their approved professional development plan, unless prevented by illness or disability, it shall result in the cancellation of such leave and a forfeiture of salary and benefits received during such leave. In such case, the employee shall forfeit all benefits to which said employee would otherwise be entitled during the period of absence for professional development. The employee shall reimburse the District for all salary and benefits received by the employee during the leave of absence.
