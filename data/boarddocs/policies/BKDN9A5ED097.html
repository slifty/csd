Guidelines

 

No student will be denied access to or participation in the educational, curricular programs of the District due to an inability to pay for any individualized instructional supplies that may be required at student or parent/guardian expense.


From time to time, where individualized and/or non-reusable materials, clothing or equipment are required for reasons of safety or health in the instructional program, the District may request that students provide their own materials.  In cases where such costs impose a financial hardship on a student or their family, the parent/guardian should alert their building principal of the hardship.

 

When it is determined that a financial harship does exist, the principal or designee shall explore all possible resources for providing the necessary item(s) at a reduced or no cost.  Possible resources include, but are not limited to, funds established for such purposes by student, parent/guardian, and/or staff organizations or student activity funds.  In the event that no available resources can be identified, the principal shall contact the Superintendent or designee, who shall consult with the Business Manager or designee in order to arrange for the provision of the necessary materials, clothing or equipment.
