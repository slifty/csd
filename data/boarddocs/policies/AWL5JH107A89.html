Purpose

 

The Board acknowledges the importance of offering students the opportunity to participate in self government within the schools.

 

Authority

 

The purposes and activities of Student Council shall pertain to the general welfare, concerns, spirit, and social activities of the student body.  Students shall have the right to organize, conduct meetings, elect officers and representatives, and petition the Board. The Board will recognize the Student Council as the primary voice of the student body, although the Board will, from time to time, recognize other student groups as voices of the student body. The charter, constitution or bylaws of the organization for student government shall be duly adopted by the members of the student body it represents and approved by the Superintendent or designee.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall appoint a qualified member of the faculty to serve as advisor to student government activities. Any payment of stipend to this advisor would require Board approval.

 

The Superintendent or designee is authorized to establish rules and regulations, consistent with the below guidelines, which:

 

  1. Invite the participation of students in their formulation.

 

  1. Assure that all students have equal access to the student government and an equal opportunity to vote and hold office.

 

  1. Require fiscal accountability and adherence to Board Policy for all financial aspects of student government activities.

 

  1. Prohibit retaliation or other disciplinary action against students or faculty advisors for participation in authorized Student Council activities provided such activities are not in violation of Board Policy, Administrative Regulations, school rules and regulations.

Guidelines

 

​​​​​​

The building principal shall encourage and maintain a structure for student government within his/her school building.

 

The building principal shall develop a structure that enables representatives of the school's student government to meet with him/her on a regular basis to discuss school issues and concerns.

 

The Superintendent or designee may, at their sole discretion, develop forums for elementary, middle school and high school representatives to meet on a regular basis to discuss issues and concerns.
