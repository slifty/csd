General Expense Reimbursement Guidelines & Procedures

 

In all instances of travel and job related expense reimbursement, full itemization of expenditures is required, along with an indication of the reason for the proposed expenditure.


Claims for mileage reimbursement must be supported by an itemized listing of miles traveled.  The itemized list shall indicate the date of travel, the starting and ending locations, and the authorized purpose of the travel. 


Mileage reimbursement will be at the current per mile rate set by the IRS based on the actual driving distance by the most direct route.  Mileage reimbursement is in lieu of all actual automobile expenses such as fuel, towing charges, repairs, replacements, tires, depreciation, insurance, etc. 

 

In addition to the standard mileage reimbursement, necessary and reasonable charges for automobile-related expenses in connection with authorized District travel such as parking fees and toll charges are reimbursable if supported by an itemized receipt. 

 

Traffic and parking ticket expenses will not be reimbursed. 

 

In order for expenses to be eligible for reimbursement, the request for reimbursement must meet both of the following criteria:

  1. The request must be submitted to the Business Office within 60 days of the date on which the expense was incurred; and
  2. Requests for reimbursement of any expenses incurred during any fiscal year ending June 30 must be submitted to the Business Office by July 31 of the following fiscal year.  

The District will only reimburse expenses related to official District business, and will not reimburse personal expenses.  No reimbursement will be made for alcoholic beverages.


The Business Manager has the final authority to disallow expenses which, in the Business Manager's opinion, are unreasonable or unnecessary. 

 

Additional Requirements - Attendance at Conferences

 

When any District employee wishes to attend a conference outside the District, they must obtain prior permission from their immediate supervisor and Superintendent or designee. The employee wishing to attend the conference shall also prepare an itemized estimate of the costs that will be incurred in connection with attendance at such conference, including, but not necessarily limited to, program/admission fees, meals, lodging, transportation, etc.  This estimate shall be submitted to the Business Manager or designee in advance of the conference.

 

Board approval is required in advance of any employee attending a conference where the estimated expenses to be reimbursed by the District exceeds $300, unless provided for otherwise by an applicable collective bargaining agreement or employment contract. Where more than one (1) representative of the District travels to and attends the same conference, it is expected that those attendees cooperate with one another to reduce travel expenses, where possible.

 

Meals, including tax and gratuities, while attending conferences shall be reimbursed at an amount that does not exceed the IRS per diem rates applicable to the specific meal (i.e. breakfast, lunch, dinner) in the state or location where the conference is taking place.

 

Actual and necessary expenses incurred in connection with attendance at conferences outside the District shall be reimbursed only if attendance at the conference was authorized in advance in accordance with this Administrative Regulation.
