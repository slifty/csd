Authority

 

In accordance with Section 511 of the Public School Code, the Board is authorized to appropriate District funds for accident insurance related to (1) student participation in athletic events or games; (2) the practice or preparation for such athletic events or games; and (3) the transportation to/from such athletic events or games, including practices.

 

Guidelines

 

The Board will maintain insurance coverage by a qualified insurer, at no cost to the student or their parent/guardian, for certain student injuries resulting from accidents sustained in the course of participation in District-supervised and sponsored sports activities.  Such insurance is not maintained and should not be understood to cover injuries or accidents other than those directly related to a student's participation in District-supervised and sponsored sports activities, as outlined in more detail in the accompanying Administrative Regulation.

 

The Superintendent or designee may provide information to parents/guardians on how to purchase insurance coverage, at no cost to the District, for injury or other loss resulting from accidents sustained by students in intramural sports programs, in the program of physical education and/or in any activity in school, on school grounds, on a school bus, or other District-owned vehicle, at an activity sponsored by the District, while traveling between home and school other than on a school bus or other District-owned vehicle, and in any activity round-the-clock.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall prepare specifications and secure proposals from qualified insurance carriers for presentation to the Board for consideration. The Superintendent or designee shall also notify all students and parents/guardians of students who may be eligible for insurance.

 

When the District assumes the cost for student insurance, the Superintendent or designee will ascertain that all eligible students are properly insured.
