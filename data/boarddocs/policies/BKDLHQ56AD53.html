Purpose

The Board recognizes the value of students sharing their talents and skills with the community through participation in public performances/events.

Authority

 

The Board endorses public performances by students when (1) they constitute a learning experience that contributes to the educational program; (2) they do not interfere with other student obligations; and (3) the circumstances of the performance/event do not pose a threat to the health, safety or wellbeing of the students involved.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

All requests for public performances by student groups require the approval of the Superintendent or designee.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations﻿ implementing this Policy. Such Administration Regulations shall address the extent, if any, to which the District will fund the costs associated with public performances/events.
