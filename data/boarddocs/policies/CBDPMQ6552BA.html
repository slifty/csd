Purpose

The purpose of this P﻿olicy is to ensure compliance with applicable law regarding the payment of overtime and to ensure the consistent treatment of all employees subject to such Policy and law.

 

 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent or designee shall develop and implement procedures to be followed regarding the approval and payment of overtime pay, in accordance with applicable law and any applicable collective bargaining agreement or Board resolution.
