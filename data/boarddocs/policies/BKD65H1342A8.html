Purpose

 

The Board recognizes its responsibility to the taxpayers of the District to ensure that public funds are utilized in furtherance of the District's educational program, mission, and strategic plan in a way that creates ﻿value to the taxpayers.

 

Authority

 

The Board has the authority and responsibility to prepare and adopt the budget, approve bids, levy taxes, approve expenditures, and incur debt in accordance with applicable law.

 

The District shall submit an annual financial report to the Secretary of Education by the designated deadline, in accordance with law and the reporting standards established by the Pennsylvania Department of Education.[3]

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Board requires the Superintendent or designee to:

  1. Establish and implement sound accounting procedures based upon recommendations of the District's auditor and the state and federal governments.
     
  2. Institute effective business practices.
     
  3. Ensure that all District financial records are retained in accordance with applicable law and Board Policy.[6]

The Superintendent or designee shall review the financial operations on an ongoing basis and report to the Board any recommended improvements to ensure continued implementation of sound fiscal practices.
