Guidelines

 

The following prohibitions and restrictions apply to all campaigning on District property, except that District students may engage in campaigning to the extent permitted by other policies, regulations, and rules governing student behavior:

  1. Campaigning on District property and use of District resources for campaigning is prohibited, except as expressly authorized by Board Policy.
  1. Employees are not permitted to engage in campaigning, or use District resources for campaigning, at school-sponsored events or activities, except as expressly authorized by Board Policy.
  1. Nothing in this Policy shall prevent employees or others from engaging in conduct or speech otherwise protected by contract, statute, or the United States or Pennsylvania Constitution.
  1. On Election Day (primary and general), employees, on their own time, and others may engage in campaigning and other political activities at officially designated polling locations on District property.
  1. Employees who hold or are running for public office (elected or appointed) are not entitled to time off from their District duties for reasons incident to such offices, except as such time may qualify under the District’s established leave policies or the applicable collective bargaining or other employment agreement.
  1. This Policy does not apply to the conduct of employee representative elections or where the discussion of politics or political issues is in furtherance of the District-approved curriculum. Staff members may not use their positions and/or classroom time as a forum for the propagation of non-curriculum-related or private opinions, or to lobby for personal causes or economic gains.
  1. No campaigning may take place on District property that results in access to/from District property being blocked.
  1. The public comment period of Board or Committee meetings may not be used for the purposes of campaigning.

Q﻿uestions regarding permitted conduct within the context of the Policy should be directed to the Superintendent or designee.

 

Violation of this Policy by District employees shall constitute cause for disciplinary action.
