 Course Requirements

 

Cheltenham High School operates under a single diploma plan and all students who meet the general requirements for graduation receive the same diploma. Every student who graduates from Cheltenham High School must have completed the required courses as outlined below.

 

There are specific guidelines for obtaining a high school diploma in less than four years. Students who wish to exercise this option should see their school counselor.

 

 

Requirements for Cheltenham High School Classes Graduating in 2024 and Beyond

 

Class of 2024 and Beyond

English

4 credits

Math

4 credits

Science

4 credits (Biology Required)

Social Studies

4 credits (Including Government)

World Language

1 credit

Physical Education

1 credit

Health

0.5 credit

Elective Credits

8.5 credits (Class of 2024)

9.5 credits (Class of 2025 and Beyond)

Total Credits

27 credits (Class of 2024)

28 credits (Class of 2025 and Beyond)

 

The principal of Cheltenham High School shall have discretion to determine which courses meet the requirements listed above.

 

Courses from the requirements listed above must address the state mandated 2 years in arts and/or humanities, which could include World Languages, Visual and Performing Arts, and/or courses above and beyond those required in English and Social Studies.

 

Additionally, to be eligible for graduation a student must demonstrate proficiency in core academic subjects according to state requirements outlined in the Pennsylvania School Code, Section 4.24 to the extent required by law. This may include performance at established proficiency levels on state-approved standardized tests. Course credit shall not be granted through the successful performance on state assessment without first having completed the course.

 

Annually, the Board shall approve the graduating class before diplomas are awarded. Following approval of candidates for graduation by official action of the Board, those students so qualifying will have the privilege of receiving appropriate recognition at graduation exercises as conducted by the school district on a date established each year.
