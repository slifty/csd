Purpose

The Board recognizes that duly approved student organizations may wish to raise and disburse funds to be used to support their activities. The Board is responsible for adopting and enforcing reasonable rules and regulations regarding the management, supervision, control, and limitations of student activities, including raising and disbursing funds.
 

Definition

For purposes of this Policy, student activity funds shall include the funds of Board-approved student groups. Student activity funds shall be raised by students and expended for purposes related to the activity, with student participation in the decision-making process regarding these areas.
 

Authority

Student activity funds are not part of District funds but must be approved by the Board. The Board adopts this Policy to ensure proper supervision of student activity funds under the District's responsibility.

 

This Policy does not apply to organizations such as parent teacher organizations, booster clubs, and similar clubs organized and governed by parents/guardians or any persons other than District students.

 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent or designee is responsible for developing Administrative Regulations governing student activity funds.
