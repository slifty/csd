Purpose

The purpose of this Policy is to promote a school environment that is a safe and secure for all students, staff, visitors, and other members of the school community.

 

Definitions

District property means on or in District property, at school-sponsored activities, or in public conveyances providing transportation to or from a school or school-sponsored activity.

Weapon is defined consistently with state law and includes, but is not limited to, any knife, cutting instrument, cutting tool, nunchuck stick, firearm, shotgun, or rifle. The term also includes any other tool, instrument or implement capable of inflicting serious bodily injury. The term does not include any item approved by the Superintendent or designee for possession in conjunction with a lawful, supervised activity or course.

Possession means having a weapon on one’s person or in an area within one’s control.
 

Guidelines
 

The use, possession, or transfer of weapons by employees on District property is prohibited unless the weapon has been approved by the Superintendent or designee for possession in conjunction with a lawful, supervised activity or use. Under no circumstances will a loaded firearm be permitted on District property for any reason contemplated in this Policy.
 

Appropriate disciplinary and/or legal action will be taken against any employee who possesses a weapon or who assists possession in any way.

 

Employees who are found in possession of a weapon in violation of this Policy will be reported to law enforcement.
 

Any weapons possessed in violation of this Policy are subject to seizure and/or forfeiture.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent or designee shall, in accordance with applicable law, ensure notification of an incident involving possession of a weapon on school property, at a school-sponsored activity or on a conveyance providing transportation to or from a school or school-sponsored activity, to employees and parents/guardians of students.  The specific notification requirements are outlined in more detail in Board Policy 218.2 (Weapons).  

The Superintendent or designee will report as required to the Pennsylvania Department of Education all incidents relating to acts of violence or unapproved possession of a weapon by any person on District property.
