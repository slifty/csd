Authority

 

The Board shall establish age requirements for the admission of beginning students, as defined below, which are consistent with statute and sound educational practice and ensure the equitable treatment of all eligible children.

 

Guidelines

 

First Grade

 

Beginners are students entering the lowest grade above the kindergarten level.

 

A beginner who attains the age of six (6) years before September 15 is eligible for admission to first grade.

 

T﻿he placement of students transferring into the Cheltenham School District and requests for early admission to first grade will be handled in accordance with the provisions outlined in the accompanying Administrative Regulation.

 

Kindergarten

 

A child who attains the age of five (5) years by September 1 is eligible for admission to kindergarten. There is no early admission to kindergarten.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy.
