Purpose

Student photo identification cards are utilized by Cheltenham School District for security, identification, and other purposes in District buildings ​​​​​
 on all District property, and on vehicles owned, operated, leased, or under the control of the District for all grades designated in the accompanying administrative regulation.

 

To assist in maintaining a safe and orderly school environment, all students are required to carry their student photo identification card during the school day, at all school-sponsored events, and while traveling on vehicles owned, operated, leased, or under the control of the District.

 

Authority

The Board establishes that the use of student photo identification cards shall be required for students in grades as determined by the Superintendent or designee.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop and implement Administrative Regulations detailing expectations of students with respect to student photo identification cards, including a procedure by which students shall report and request replacement of any lost or stolen student photo identification cards.
