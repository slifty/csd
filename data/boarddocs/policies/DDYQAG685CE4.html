Authority

 

In accordance with law, the Board shall permit disclosure of required student information about secondary students to representatives of postsecondary education institutions and to representatives of the Armed Forces of the United States.

 

In accordance with law, the same access to secondary students shall be granted to postsecondary education representatives, military recruiters and prospective employers.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy that establish procedures for annually notifying parents/guardians/students, as appropriate, of this Policy. The notice shall:

 

  1. Advise that the District routinely discloses names, addresses and telephone numbers of students to military recruiters and representatives of postsecondary education institutions to the extent required by law, subject to a parent/guardian/student’s request not to disclose such information without written consent;
     
  2. Explain the parent/guardian/student’s right to request that the student information detailed above not be disclosed without prior written consent; and
     
  3. Establish a procedure for how the parents/guardians/students may opt out of the disclosure of such student information, and the method and timeline for doing so.﻿
