Authority

 

Payment of the actual and necessary expenses, including travel expenses, of any District employee that are incurred in the course of performing assigned duties for the District shall be reimbursed in accordance with this Policy and the accompanying Administrative Regulation.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Business Manager or designee shall be responsible for establishing reimbursement procedures, guidelines, and limitations.  No expenses shall be reimbursed unless properly itemized and supported by a receipt or similar documentation evidencing proof of purchase. Unauthorized expenses will not be reimbursed. 
 

The Business Manager or designee shall be responsible for reviewing requests for reimbursement of expenses, approving or denying such requests, and processing the reimbursement of approved expenses.   

 

Guidelines

 

Employees are expected to exercise the same care when incurring travel and other expenses on behalf of the District that a prudent person would exercise if traveling on personal business expending personal funds. 

 

Employees using their personal vehicle for authorized District business (other than their normal commute to/from work) shall be reimbursed at the applicable rate determined allowable by the United States Internal Revenue Service for the use of private vehicles in the performance of work assignments, unless otherwise specified in any applicable collective bargaining agreement or individual contract. 

 

To the extent that the provisions of this Board Policy or the accompanying Administrative Regulation conflict with any provision of any applicable collective bargaining agreement or individual employment contract, the terms of the applicable collective bargaining agreement or individual contract will control.
