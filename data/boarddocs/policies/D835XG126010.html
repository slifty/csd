Purpose

 

The Board recognizes that the preparation of an annual school calendar is necessary for the efficient operation of the District and effective communication with students, staff, parents/guardians and the school community.

 

Authority

 

The Board shall determine annually the days and the hours when the schools shall be in session for instructional purposes in accordance with state law and regulations.  This may include, as appropriate, activities qualifying as instructional days or hours under the direction of certified school employees for fulfilling the minimum number of required days or hours of instruction under law, regulations and state guidance.  

 

The school calendar shall consist of a minimum of (1) 180 student days; or (2) 900 hours of instruction at the elementary level and 990 hours of instruction at the secondary level, unless otherwise approved by the Pennsylvania Department of Education.

 

The Board reserves the right to alter the school calendar when it is in the best interests of the District, including in cases of emergency, in accordance with applicable law and regulations.

 

Guidelines

 

The Administrative Regulation accompanying this Policy shall contain guidelines for the development of the school calendar. This Administrative Regulation may only be amended or repealed by a majority of the Board.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

In order to facilitate the Board’s timely adoption of the school calendar, the Superintendent or designee shall be responsible for preparing an annual school calendar for Board consideration no later than October 31, unless another date is agreed to in advance.
