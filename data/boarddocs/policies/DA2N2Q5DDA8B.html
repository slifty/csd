Policy Notification

 

The principal or designee shall annually provide a copy of the District's hazing Policy and Administrative Regulations to each student activity or organization within the District.

 

These Administrative Regulations and the accompanying Board Policy will be posted on the District's publicly accessible website so that students, parents/guardians, coaches, activity sponsors, volunteers and District employees are aware of their content.

 

The principal or designee shall also ensure that students are informed of the content of the District's hazing Policy and Administrative Regulations. In addition to posting these Administrative Regulations and the accompanying Board Policy on the District's publicly accessible website, the means of notification may include, but are not be limited to, the following: distribution and publication in student handbooks, presentation at school assemblies, and inclusion in coaches’ handbooks, activity sponsor handbooks, student codes of conduct, faculty handbooks, and parent/guardian handbooks. Coaches and activity sponsors shall be familiar with the District’s Policy and Administrative Regulations regarding hazing and inform students of their requirements at the start of the activity/season and on an as-needed basis during the activity/season.

 

Reporting & Investigation Procedures

 

Students who believe they or others have been subjected to hazing are encouraged to promptly report such incidents to their principal, assistant principal, athletic director or school counselor, coach/activity sponsor, or other trusted school official. In the event a report is received by an individual other than the building principal, the building principal shall be notified so that a proper investigation may begin.

 

Parents/guardians, coaches, activity sponsors, volunteers, District employees, representatives, agents, and contractors shall be alert to incidents of hazing and potential hazing and shall promptly report such conduct to their immediate supervisor or the appropriate building principal. In the event a report is received by an individual other than the building principal, the building principal shall be notified so that a proper investigation may begin.

 

Complaints of hazing by a student against another student shall be handled in the same manner as other student disciplinary investigations.  The person accepting the complaint shall handle the report objectively, neutrally and professionally, setting aside personal biases that might favor or disfavor the student filing the complaint or those accused of a violation of these Administrative Regulations or the accompanying Board Policy.

 

Every report of alleged hazing that could be interpreted at the outset to fall within the provisions of Board Policies and/or Administrative Regulations addressing potential violations of laws against discrimination or harassment, including, but not limited to claims of Title IX discrimination and sex-based harassment, shall be handled as a joint, concurrent investigation into all allegations and coordinated with the full participation of the Title IX Coordinator in accordance with applicable legal requirements.  Information regarding the contact information for the District's Title IX Coordinator can be found online at: https://www.cheltenham.org/district-info/title-ix. 

 

 

If, in the course of a hazing investigation, potential issues of Title IX discrimination or sex-based harassment are identified, the Title IX Coordinator shall be promptly notified. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in these Administrative Regulations or the accompanying Board Policy, any complaints or allegations raised under these Administrative Regulations or the accompanying Board Policy that could be construed as an allegation or complaint of Title IX discrimination or sex-based harassment must be referred to the Title IX Coordinator to ensure it is addressed in accordance with the District's Title IX obligations.

 

Upon receipt of a complaint of hazing, the building principal or designee shall determine what, if any, interim measures should be put in place to protect students from further hazing or similar conduct.

 

Those receiving the initial report and conducting or overseeing the investigation will assess whether the complaint, if proven, would constitute hazing, aggravated hazing or organizational hazing and shall report it to law enforcement consistent with established procedures outlined in the Memorandum of Understanding with law enforcement.

 

Consequences for Violations

 

Students

 

If the investigation results in a substantiated finding of hazing, the investigator shall recommend appropriate disciplinary action up to and including expulsion and criminal prosecution, as circumstances warrant, in accordance with Board Policy, Administrative Regulations, school rules (i.e., Code of Conduct, Student Handbook), and applicable law. The student may also be subject to disciplinary action by the coach or activity sponsor, up to and including removal from the activity or organization. Whether a student qualified for and received safe harbor under a criminal investigation shall be considered in assigning discipline.

 

Non-Student Violators/Organizational Hazing

 

Employees who fail to report known instances of hazing shall be subject to appropriate discipline, in accordance with applicable law and any applicable employee handbook or collective bargaining agreement.

 

If the investigation results in a substantiated finding that a coach, activity sponsor, or volunteer affiliated with the student activity or organization engaged in, condoned or ignored any violation of these Administrative Regulations or the accompanying Board Policy, they shall be disciplined and/or prosecuted in accordance with Board Policy and applicable laws and regulations. Discipline could include, but is not limited to, dismissal from the position as coach, activity sponsor, or volunteer, and/or dismissal from employment.

 

If an organization is found to have engaged in organizational hazing, it shall be subject to the imposition of fines and other appropriate penalties. Penalties may include rescission of permission for that organization to operate on school property or to otherwise operate under the sanction or recognition of the District.

 

Safe Harbor

 

An individual needing medical attention or seeking medical attention for another shall not be subject to criminal prosecution if they comply with the requirements under law, which are outlined below, subject to the limitations set forth in law

 

Immunity for Individuals Seeking Medical Attention for Another

 

An individual shall not be prosecuted for an offense under the Timothy J. Piazza Antihazing Law if the individual can establish all of the following:

 

  1. A law enforcement officer first became aware of the individual's violation of the Timothy J. Piazza Antihazing Law because the individual placed a 911 call or contacted campus security, police or emergency services, based on a reasonable belief that another individual was in need of immediate medical attention to prevent death or serious bodily injury.
  2. The individual reasonably believed the individual was the first individual to make a 911 call or contact campus security, police or emergency services and report that an individual needed immediate medical attention to prevent death or serious bodily injury.
  3. The individual provided the individual's own name to the 911 operator or equivalent campus security officer, police or emergency services personnel.
  4. The individual remained with the individual needing medical assistance until a campus security officer, police or emergency services personnel arrived and the need for the individual's presence had ended.

 

Immunity for Individuals Needing Medical Attention

 

An individual needing medical attention shall be immune from prosecution for an offense under the Timothy J. Piazza Antihazing Law or Section 6308(a) (relating to purchase, consumption, possession or transportation of liquor or malt or brewed beverages) only if another individual against whom probable cause exists to charge an offense under the Timothy J. Piazza Antihazing Law reported the incident and remained with the individual needing medical attention and the other individual qualifies for a safe harbor.
