  1. Purpose

    The Board believes that the goals and objectives of the district are best achieved by a diversity of learning experiences, some of which are more appropriately conducted outside the regular classroom curricular program of the school and some of which may combine educational experiences both inside and outside the classroom.

    All learning experiences offered by the schools of this district— curricular, extracurricular, and cocurricular should be planned and integrated toward the attainment of the district's objectives.

    This policy does not apply to interscholastic, intramural, or club sports.

     
  2. Definition

    “Extracurricular Activities” means "those programs that are sponsored or approved by the Board but are not explicitly connected to academic learning." Such activities shall normally be:
     
    1. Conducted wholly or partly outside the regular school day.
       
    2. Marked by student participation in the processes of initiation, planning, organizing, and execution.
       
    3. Equally available to all students who voluntarily elect to participate, except that where eligibility requirements are necessary or desirable, the Board shall be so informed and must approve the establishment of eligibility standards before they may be operable.
       
    4. Conducted in a manner that ensures the inclusion of all students in accordance, when applicable, with their individualized educational plans, including providing accommodations where appropriate.

“Cocurricular activities” refers to activities, programs, and learning experiences that complement, in some way, what students are learning in school—i.e., experiences that are connected to or mirror the academic curriculum.

"Cocurricular activities" frequently involve participation in a class offered for credit toward graduation from high school or for which a student is awarded a grade. Such activities shall ordinarily be:

  1. Conducted partly outside and partly inside the regular school day.
     
  2. Available to all students who voluntarily elect to participate except for those who fail to meet the eligibility requirements.
     
  3. Conducted in a manner that ensures the inclusion of all students in accordance, when applicable, with their individualized educational plans, including providing accommodations where appropriate.
     
  1. Authority

    Eligibility for Participation

    Participation in co-curricular activities and extracurricular activities is limited to students who are enrolled in the district on a full-time basis unless participation is mandated by law as set forth in the accompanying administrative regulation.

    In order to ensure the effective implementation of this requirement, the Superintendent or designee shall, by administrative regulation, establish eligibility criteria for legal participation for all non-district students, including but not limited to, charter school students, private school students, and home-schooled students.

  1. Delegation of Responsibility

The Board shall make school facilities, supplies and equipment available and assign staff members for the support of a program of extracurricular and co-curricular activities. Any extracurricular or co-curricular activity shall be considered to be under the sponsorship of this Board when it has been approved by the Board on recommendation of the Superintendent.

 

The Board shall maintain the program of extracurricular and co-curricular activities at no cost to participating students, except that:
 

Pol. 110

  1. The Board's responsibility for provision of supplies shall carry the same exemptions as listed in the Board's policy on regular school supplies.
     
  2. Students may be required to assume all or part of the costs for travel and attendance at extracurricular and co-curricular events and trips, with consideration from students who are economically disadvantaged.
     
  3. Where eligibility requirements are necessary or desirable, the Board shall be informed and must approve the establishment of eligibility standards before they become operable. The Board will receive a periodic report evaluating compliance with such standards.
     
  1. Guidelines

    The Superintendent or designee shall develop and disseminate procedures to implement the extracurricular and co-curricular activities program.

    Guidelines shall ensure that the program of extracurricular and cocurricular activities, among other things:
  1. Assesses the needs and interests of and is responsive to district students.
     
  2. Involves students in the planning of co-curricular activities.
     
  3. Is responsive to the articulated needs of students.
     
  4. Ensures the provision of competent guidance and supervision of staff.
     
  5. Guards against the exploitation of students.
     
  6. Provides for a variety of experiences and a diversity of organizational models.
     
  7. Provides for the continuing evaluation of the co-curricular program.
     
  8. Ensures that all extracurricular and cocurricular activities are open to all students and that all students are fully informed of the opportunities open to them.
     
  9. Ensures provision of competent guidance and supervision by staff.
     
  10. Provides support procedures, for a student identified as having difficulty, to help the student regain academic standing and regain or retain eligibility.
     
  11. Ensures that students meet designated minimum academic eligibility standards to the extent that they participate in extracurricular activities in which students participate for at least an average of 15 hours per week for a minimum of nine weeks.

    The Superintendent shall prepare and present to the Board for its consideration, these standards of eligibility to be met by all students participating in extracurricular and cocurricular activities. The Superintendent shall secure Board approval before making any changes to the eligibility standards once approved by the Board. The eligibility standards shall be contained in the Administrative Regulation of this policy and shall include an appropriate appeal procedure for those students who may be declared ineligible.
     
  12. Establishes legal qualifications for participation by non-district students.
     
  13. Establishes criteria for starting and discontinuing activities.

Student Clubs

The schools may encourage students to broaden their knowledge and citizenship by permitting the formation of clubs or other groups organized to promote or pursue specialized activities outside the classroom.

Membership shall be open to all interested and/or eligible students. Approval of the building principal shall be obtained.

A member of the faculty shall attend the meeting or activity as an official advisor.

Such clubs cannot be associated with any political or religious organization or any organization which denies membership on the basis of sex, unless appropriate; race; color; creed; religion; or political belief.

Clubs shall not be formed which would tend to have divisive effects upon the general goals of public education.

Application for use of school facilities for student-initiated clubs shall be made according to procedures developed by the Superintendent. Approval will be granted and appropriate notification made when the application is complete and the proposed use is in compliance with Board policies.

The district retains the authority to maintain order and discipline on school premises in order to protect the well-being of students and employees and to ensure that student attendance at such meetings is voluntary.
