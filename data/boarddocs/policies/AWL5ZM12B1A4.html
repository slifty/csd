Purpose

 

The Board recognizes its responsibility under law to adequately insure the real and/or personal property of the District.

 

Authority

 

The Board has the authority and responsibility to secure adequate insurance coverage to protect the District's interests in its buildings and properties. Such coverage shall insure for actual cost value and/or replacement cost.

In securing insurance, the Board shall be guided by the service of an insurance agent or carrier, scope of coverage provided by the policy, price of desired coverage, assurance of coverage, and other factors deemed relevant by the Board.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Board, or its designee, shall appoint an insurance advisor, who may be the Agent of Record.

The Superintendent or designee shall be responsible for periodically reviewing the District's insurance portfolio and recommending any changes in coverage(s) to the Board.
