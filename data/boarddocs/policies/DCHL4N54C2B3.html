Purpose

This Policy governs separation from the District due to employee resignation.
 

Definition

A resignation is a voluntary termination of employment with the District initiated by an employee and includes resignations in lieu of termination, negotiated resignations, resignations for purposes of retirement, and all other forms of voluntary resignation.
 

Delegation of Authority

The Board authorizes the Superintendent or designee to accept all resignations of District employees (except the Superintendent) on behalf of the Board without further Board action.  The Superintendent or designee shall report all employee resignations and their acceptance as an informational item at the next regular Board meeting.  A resignation accepted by the Superintendent or designee is irrevocable absent Board action to the contrary.

The Superintendent shall submit their resignation to the Board President. The Board shall take official action on acceptance of the resignation in accordance with the terms specified in the Superintendent's written contract.  

 

The Administrative Regulations accompanying this Policy shall outline the procedures regarding both resignation and also final pay calculation for employees who have voluntarily resigned. These procedures shall ensure that individual resignations will be reviewed and handled in a manner that produces the lowest potential for compromise of confidential data.

 

Guidelines

This Policy does not relieve the requirement of Board approval for a negotiated resignation that otherwise requires Board approval pursuant to law.
