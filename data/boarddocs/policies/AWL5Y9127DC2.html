Title
Use of Equipment and Facilities by Staff

Status

Authority

 

District-owned equipment and facilities may not be used by District staff for personal reasons, either on or off District property, without explicit prior authorization from the Superintendent or designee, unless otherwise permitted by Board Policy.

 

Guidelines

 

Absent approval of the Superintendent or designee to the contrary, District-owned equipment and facilities shall be available for staff use only if such use is permitted by Board Policy or any applicable collective bargaining agreement.
