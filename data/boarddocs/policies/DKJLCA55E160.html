Purpose

 

The Board recognizes that a system of assessing and reporting student progress and achievement can help students, teachers, and parents/guardians understand and evaluate a student's progress toward educational goals and academic standards.  Further, the Board believes that communication between school and home is a vital component in the growth and education of each student.

 

Definition

 

Assessment shall be the system of measuring and recording student progress and achievement that enables the student, parents/guardians, teachers, administrators and other District professionals, to determine a student's attainment of established academic standards and to learn the student's strengths and weaknesses, plan an educational or vocational future for the student in areas of the greatest potential for success, and identify where remedial work is required.

     

    Delegation of Responsibility

     

    The Board directs that the District's instructional program shall include a system of measuring and reporting student progress.  The Superintendent or designee shall develop and implement Administrative Regulations to implement this Policy in accordance with applicable law and consistent with the general guidelines outlined below.  

     

    Guidelines

     

    The District's assessment system shall include a variety of assessment strategies.  Assessment strategies should objectively evaluate student progress, reward student effort, and encourage students to continuously assess their own progress.  Methods of assessment shall be appropriate to the course of study and maturity of students.  All assessment systems shall be subject to continuing review and revision. 

     

    Students with disabilities shall be included in the District's assessment system, with appropriate accommodations when necessary, in accordance with law.

     

    At the outset of any course, each student should be informed about the course expectations and academic standards to be attained.  Each student and their parents/guardians should be kept informed of personal progress during the course of study via report cards, parent-teacher conferences, and other appropriate means.
