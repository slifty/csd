Guidelines

 

District employees are encouraged to be on alert for early warning signs of fights and altercations such as stare-downs, verbal exchanges, posturing, audience formation, and other clues that an altercation is about to ensue. In the event an employee believes a fight or altercation is about to ensue, they shall immediately request assistance from a building safety officer, climate and culture administrator, building principal, vice principal, or assistant principal.

 

The following guidelines are provided for the information and direction of employees who observe or are made aware of a fight or physical or verbal altercation involving students:

  • Immediately call for help by contacting the building safety officer, climate and culture administrator, building principal, vice principal, or assistant principal. Be prepared to indicate the exact location and the nature of the incident. If, under the circumstances, an employee believes that sending a student to call for or summon assistance will result in a faster response, they are permitted to do so.
  • Verbally direct the student(s) to stop.
  • Get or direct other students out of harm’s way, if possible. Student bystanders should be directed to continue on to their designated locations and not involve themselves in or congregate around the altercation. Students are not permitted to record or photograph fights or other disturbances except for the limited purpose of reporting such incidents to a school official.

In addition to the procedures outlined above, all procedures outlined in Board Policy and Administrative Regulation 225 (Relationship with Law Enforcement) regarding notifying law enforcement of student conduct that results in injury shall be followed.

 

District employees, other than school safety officers, climate and culture administrators, building principals, vice principals, and assistant principals are not required or expected to intervene to disband or separate students involved in altercations. Such employees who choose to intervene in such an altercation are acting on their own free will at their own risk.

 

Notwithstanding the foregoing, nothing in this Administrative Regulation or the accompanying Board Policy shall be construed to prevent a District employee from using reasonable force under any of the following circumstances: (1) to quell a disturbance, (2) to obtain possession of weapons or other dangerous objects, (3) for the purpose of self-defense, and (4) for the protection of persons or property. To the extent that a District employee does intervene to disband or separate students involved in altercations, their involvement shall be limited to the following:

  • Placing oneself between the two combatants.
  • Taking a student or students by the arm (placing one’s hand between the shoulder and the elbow) for the purpose of escorting the student away from the area.
  • Holding a student by the arm (as described above) for the purpose of detaining the student until additional assistance is secured from administration or other authorities

Corporal punishment, defined as the infliction of physical force as punishment for a rules infraction, is not permitted.
