Purpose

The Board recognizes that certain written and oral communications between students and school personnel, as identified in the accompanying Administrative Regulation, are confidential.
 

Authority

The Board directs school personnel to comply with all federal and state laws, Department of Education regulations, and Board Policy concerning confidential communications of students.
 

Guidelines

Information received in confidence from a student shall be communicated in the manner specified in the accompanying A﻿dministrative R﻿egulations to the student's parent/guardian, building principal, or other appropriate authority when the health, welfare, or safety of the student or other persons is clearly in jeopardy.

 

Use of a student’s confidential communications to school personnel in legal proceedings is governed by statutes and regulations appropriate to the proceeding.

 

This Policy does not relieve any District employee from their mandatory child abuse reporting obligations.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

Employees such as school counselors, school nurses, school psychologists, and home and school visitors shall familiarize themselves with their confidentiality obligations and shall not reveal such communications without student or parental consent unless qualifying circumstances exist.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall develop and implement Administrative Regulations to guide staff with respect to disclosure and non-disclosure of confidential communications from students.
