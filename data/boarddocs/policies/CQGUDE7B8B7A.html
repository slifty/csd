Requirements for Use of Seclusion

 

Seclusion may be used only as a last resort safety procedure in extraordinary and limited circumstances after a less restrictive procedure or PBIS has been implemented without success.

 

Seclusion shall only be used when a student is displaying a behavior that could result in immediate or imminent injury to the student or others. There should be sufficient knowledge of the student to understand that the risk of immediate or imminent injury could be lessened if the student was removed to a safe environment away from other students.

 

Every effort should be made to prevent the need to place students in seclusion.  Each building within the District must have a Crisis Response Team (CRT) that is trained in positive behavioral interventions and supports and de-escalation techniques and, whose members are trained in methods of crisis prevention.  The CRT will have building specific procedures in place to ensure every effort is taken to prevent the need for seclusion of a student.

 

The District recognizes that at times it may become necessary for employees to use seclusion in an emergency to prevent immediate or imminent injury to the student or others.   The use of seclusion must comply with the following requirements:

 

  1. The seclusion must be the least restrictive possible alternative to prevent harm.
  2. The duration of the seclusion shall not exceed the time necessary to enable the student to function safely in the school setting.
  3. The room or space in which seclusion occurs shall have proper lighting and ventilation and remain unlocked, the room or space shall not be locked nor shall the door to the room or space be held closed by a staff member.
  4. The room or space shall comply with all applicable health, fire, and emergency safety requirements, and the student placed in seclusion shall be included in any evacuation or safety plan or drill for the school.
  5. District staff shall provide continuous supervision of students in seclusion, which shall involve the presence of staff within the same room or space, unless such presence is determined to be an immediate threat to the student or staff member.
  6. The staff member’s duties shall be assigned so that the staff member can carefully and continuously supervise the isolated student, have ongoing communication with the student, and see and hear the student at all times in order that they monitor the student for visual or auditory signs of physiological distress.  Every instance in which seclusion is used must be carefully, continuously and visually monitored to ensure the appropriateness of its use and safety of the student, other students, teachers, and other personnel.
  7. During any seclusion the student must be permitted to use the restroom upon request.  Under this circumstance, the student shall be escorted to and from the restroom.
  8. During any seclusion the student must be provided with water upon request.

 

Every behavioral intervention used must ensure the right of all students to be treated with dignity and respect, and free of abuse.

 

Required Response Post-Seclusion

 

  • If seclusion is used on a student, the use shall be recorded in writing.  This written report of the seclusion must be completed no later than one (1) working day after the seclusion.
  • The building administrator must notify the parent/guardian of the secluded student and the Director of Special Education as soon as reasonably possible, but in no event more than 24-hours after the use of seclusion with their child.  The student’s file shall include a copy of the written report which shall be maintained in accordance with applicable law. 
  • When there are repeated uses of seclusion with a given student, multiple uses within the same classroom, or multiple uses by the same individual, an administrative review and, if appropriate, revision of strategies currently in place to address the behaviors triggering the use of seclusion must be conducted. 
  • The Director of Special Education will be responsible for monitoring documented reports of seclusion and conducting an administrative review of any repeated uses of seclusion. 
  • The Board will be provided with updates twice a school year on any use of seclusion in District schools.

 

When Seclusion May Not Be Used

 

  • The use of seclusion with a student shall not be permitted except when the conditions described in these Administrative Regulations as well as Board Policy 113.2 are met.
  • Seclusion should not be used as a routine school safety measures; that is, it should not be implemented except in an emergency to prevent immediate or imminent injury to the student or others in situations where a child’s behavior poses imminent danger of serious physical harm to self or others
  • Seclusion should not be used as a routine strategy implemented to address instructional problems or inappropriate behavior (e.g., disrespect, noncompliance, insubordination, out of seat), as a means of coercion or retaliation, or as a convenience.
  • Seclusion shall never be used as punishment or discipline (e.g., placing in seclusion for out-of-seat behavior), as a means of coercion or retaliation, or for the convenience of staff.
  • Seclusion shall never be used when the student's known medical or physical condition would make the use of seclusion dangerous for that student.

 

Requirements for Seclusion Area

 

  • A seclusion area in each of the District's schools shall have substantially the same ceiling height, lighting, ventilation, and temperature as the surrounding room or rooms.  The room or space shall be large enough to accommodate the student being isolated and any other person necessary to accompany the isolated student and provide sufficient space for the student to be able to sit or lay down.
  • The room or space shall not be named, labeled, or referred to by any staff member in a manner so as to label or stigmatize a student placed in the room or space.
  • A room or space used for seclusion must be free of objects that could injure or be used by a student to injure themselves or others.  It must be designed so that a student cannot climb up the walls and shall allow for continuous visual monitoring and communication with the student by a staff member.  The staff member visually monitoring a student placed in seclusion must be crisis prevention (CP) trained, must be fully informed of these Administrative Regulations as well as Board Policy 113.2 - Behavior Support. 

 

Compliance with Individualized Education Program (IEP) or Behavior Intervention Plan (BIP)

 

  • If a student with a disability is placed in seclusion pursuant to a BIP or IEP, the time limitations identified in the BIP or IEP shall be followed and applied by the staff member.
  • The use of seclusion on a student with a disability shall also be subject to any conditions described in the student’s IEP and, if applicable, BIP.
