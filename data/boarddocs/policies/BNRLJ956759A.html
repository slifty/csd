Use of Bicycles

 

Individuals ﻿who use a bicycle to travel to and from school or work must park and secure their bicycle at the designated bicycle racks or other designated spaces outside of the school building.

 

Bicycles are not permitted to be brought or stored inside of school buildings.

 

Individuals who use a bicycle to travel to and from school or work must obey all posted signs and all rules of the Pennsylvania Vehicle Code applicable to bicyclists.

 

The District is not responsible for bicycles that are lost, stolen, or damaged on school property, or any injury resulting from their use or storage on District property.

 

Use of Motor Vehicles

 

The following rules and regulations shall be in effect with respect to the operation and parking of motor vehicles on school property.

 

Only  motor vehicles that have a current and proper state inspection and registration may be driven on school property.

 

All student drivers must properly register for a parking tag in the main office at Cheltenham High School prior to being permitted to operate or park a vehicle for purposes of attending school or school-sponsored activities on school property.

 

Students are to park in the designated parking lot(s) for students.

 

Individuals driving a motor vehicle on school property must observe safe driving regulations on campus.

 

A student’s driving privileges may be revoked for any of the following reasons, as determined by the building principal or designee:

 

  • Reckless driving;
  • Speeding;
  • Illegal parking;
  • Parking in lots or spaces not designated for students;
  • Persistent lateness to school;
  • Leaving school without permission;
  • Cutting classes;
  • Insubordination;
  • Violating school rules, the Cheltenham High School Student Handbook, Board Policy, any applicable Code of Conduct, or applicable law.

 

Students who park illegally (no tag, spaces not designated for students, etc.) are subject to loss of driving/parking privileges and may also be fined.  The student’s car may also be towed or booted with the owner responsible for any expense or fee for towing or boot removal. Non-students who park illegally or otherwise fail to follow the rules outlined herein may also be towed or booted with the owner responsible for any expense or fee for towing or boot removal.

 

No individual﻿ may interfere with the arrival or departure of buses or other school vehicles.

 

Individuals﻿ must park their vehicle within the lined spaces.

 

No individual may park their vehicle in any way that obstructs any roadway or pedestrian sidewalk.

 

Individuals are not permitted to park on school property overnight without the prior permission of the building principal or designee, except in connection with pre-approved school activities or District-related job functions, where such permission is implied.

 

Individuals driving on school property﻿ must obey all posted traffic signs and all rules of the Pennsylvania Vehicle Code.

 

Students are not permitted to access or use their vehicle during school hours without permission from a school administrator.  Students are also prohibited from loitering at their cars or in the school parking lot upon arriving to school.

 

The District is not responsible for motor vehicles that are lost, stolen, or damaged on school property.

 

Individuals﻿ are encouraged to lock their cars while they are parked on school property.

 

School parking lots are private property and parking on school property is a privilege, not an entitlement.

 

Vehicles parked on school property may be searched if an authorized administrator has reasonable suspicion that the vehicle may contain items which constitute a direct and immediate threat to the health, safety and welfare of the school community. In the event that an authorized administrator determines that this level of reasonable suspicion exists (i.e. direct and immediate threat to the health, safety and welfare of the school community), law enforcement shall be contacted in order to facilitate the search and in order to retrieve any items posing a direct and immediate threat to the health, safety and welfare of the school community.
