Name﻿

 

The Board of School Directors shall be known officially as the "Board of School Directors of the Cheltenham School District," but may also be referred to as the “Board.”

 

Cheltenham School District may be referred to in its policies and procedures and for all other purposes as “the District.”

 

 


Composition

 

The District is comprised of all lands that lie within the municipal boundaries of Cheltenham Township.

 

 


Purpose

 

The District is organized for the purpose of providing a program of public education to serve the needs of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, and specifically the school-age children of Cheltenham School District.

 

 

 


Intermediate Unit

 

The District is assigned to Intermediate Unit No. 23.


Classification

 

The District is classified as a school district of the second class pursuant to the Pennsylvania Public School Code.

 

Address

 

The official address of the Board of School Directors of the District shall be the address of the Central Administrative Offices of the District, currently 2000 Ashbourne Road, Elkins Park, PA 19027.
