Purpose

 

District-owned equipment is primarily intended to benefit and support the District's instructional program.

 

 

Guidelines

 

Except in accordance with﻿ Board Policy 707 (Use of Facilities), D﻿istrict-owned equipment shall generally not be used by individuals, groups, or organizations for non-school use. The Superintendent is authorized to make limited exceptions to this general policy in rare circumstances when the Superintendent determines that doing so is in the best interest of the District, consistent with the guidelines listed below.

Any requests from individuals, groups, or organizations to borrow District-owned equipment that fall outside of the scope of Board Policy 707 shall be directed to the Superintendent.

 

In the event that the Superintendent authorizes the borrowing of any District-owned equipment in accordance with this Policy, proper controls shall be established to assure the responsibility for, and return of, all such equipment. The user of such District-owned equipment shall be fully liable for any damage or loss occurring to the equipment during the period of its use and shall be responsible for its safe return. When equipment authorized for lending requires the services of an operator, the user shall employ the person designated by the District and shall pay the stated cost of services.

 

District-owned equipment﻿ may be removed from school property by students or staff only when such equipment is necessary to accomplish tasks arising from their school or job responsibilities and prior approval has been granted by the building principal, immediate supervisor, or designee. Removal of District-owned equipment from school property for personal use by staff or students is prohibited unless otherwise permitted by Board Policy or approved in advance by the Superintendent.

 

School books or equipment may be used by students during vacations when permission is granted by the building principal or designee.
