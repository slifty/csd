Definition

 

For purposes of these Administrative Regulations and the accompanying Board Policy, licensed practitioner of the healing arts shall be defined as an individual licensed by the Commonwealth to practice as a physician, physician’s assistant, certified registered nurse practitioner, or psychologist.

 

Guidelines
 

Applications for homebound instruction shall be made in writing by the student's parent/guardian to the building principal, who shall forward such applications to the Director of Student Services or designee for review.

 

An application for homebound instruction shall include a written statement from a licensed practitioner of the healing arts familiar with the student (who may not be the student's parent/guardian or close family member) certifying the nature of the illness or disability, explaining specifically why the illness or disability prevents the student from attending school, and noting the probable duration of the confinement. Such applications shall be reviewed and approved or disapproved by the Director of Student Services or designee.

 

If the student is a special education-eligible student and the student’s parent/guardian requests homebound instruction, an Individualized Education Plan (IEP) meeting should be held immediately to determine if the medical condition is related to the student’s disability and whether the IEP needs to be revised to provide appropriate special education services and placement. A permission to re-evaluate will be issued to obtain parent/guardian permission for an evaluation to determine the nature of the illness in relation to the disability and the impact on the student’s ability to access their educational program.

 

The District will provide homebound instruction only for those confinements expected to last twenty (20) consecutive school days or more, but exceptions may be made as the Director of Student Services or designee deems advisable and the Superintendent or designee approves. Eligible students shall receive up to 5 hours of virtual or in-person instruction each week for required courses.  Any exceptions must be approved by the Superintendent or designee.  The homebound instruction program provided hereunder will comply with standards established by the Secretary of Education.

 

The period of homebound instruction for an individual student shall not exceed three (3) consecutive months. The Superintendent or designee may request approval from the Department of Education to extend the period of homebound instruction for an individual, which shall be reevaluated every three (3) months.

 

Homebound instruction will only be provided in a hospital if the hospital is located within the boundaries of the Cheltenham School District.

 

The District reserves the right to require a medical examination by a licensed practitioner of the healing arts of its own selection in order to make determinations for initial or continued eligibility for homebound instruction. The District further reserves the right to require updated medical information as to the student’s progress during the period of homebound instruction.

 

The District reserves the right to withhold or discontinue homebound instruction when the instructor's presence in the place of a student's confinement presents a hazard to the health or safety of the teacher, if a parent/guardian or other adult in authority is not at home with the student during the hours of instruction, when the condition of the student is such as to preclude any benefit from instruction, or in the event that the student or their family miss or refuse more than two (2) scheduled homebound instruction sessions without making prior alternative arrangements.

 

When a student receives homebound instruction, the student will be counted for attendance purposes as if the student were in school.

 

Students who are receiving homebound instruction shall not participate in extracurricular or athletic activities sponsored by the District during the period of such homebound instruction, unless an exception has been granted by the Superintendent or designee.

 

Upon written recommendation of a licensed practitioner of the healing arts familiar with the student and approval of the Director of Student Services or designee, students transitioning into full-time school attendance will be permitted to participate in extracurricular activities sponsored by the district.

 

A student’s participation in employment outside of the home may disqualify the student from eligibility for homebound instruction.

 

A student receiving homebound instruction is required to participate in the PSSA testing. If the student is incapable of participating at school, the individual who is providing the homebound instruction shall be trained to administer the assessment and will then be required to administer the test to the student in their home.
