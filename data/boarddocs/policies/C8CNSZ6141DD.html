Purpose

Orderly operation of the District requires maintaining a file of all records relative to an individual's duties and responsibilities as an employee of the District and permitting employees to access their personnel files in accordance with applicable law.

 

Authority

The Board requires that sufficient records be maintained to ensure (1) an employee's qualifications for the job held; (2) compliance with federal and state requirements and conformance with District policies and rules; and (3) proper documentation of completed evaluations.

 

The District is required to, at reasonable times, upon request of an employee and in accordance with law, permit that employee or an agent designated by the employee to inspect their personnel file used to determine their qualifications for employment, promotion, additional compensation, termination or disciplinary action.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Board delegates the establishment and maintenance of official personnel records to the Superintendent or designee, who shall prepare guidelines for allowing employees access to information contained in their personnel file and appealing information included in their personnel file, with the understanding that the personnel file is District property.
