Complaints or grievances may begin at Level One, Two or Three, if the designated level is the appropriate one for the matter being complained of, or at the discretion of the Superintendent or designee. The level of authority required to remedy the situation shall be the decisive factor in determining the level at which the procedures shall commence.

 

Level One

 

The employee shall present their complaint to their principal or direct/immediate supervisor (“Level One Respondent”) in writing using the appropriate form (Attachment A). The Level One Respondent, after reviewing the written complaint, should arrange a meeting with the employee to discuss the problem within three (3) school days of receipt of the written complaint. The employee may request the presence of any fellow employee at this Level One meeting. The Level One Respondent may request the presence of another administrator.

 

The Level One Respondent shall issue their written response to the employee within three (3) school days following the Level One meeting, exclusive of the day of the meeting.

 

Level Two

 

If the decision of the Level One Respondent is not satisfactory to the employee, they may request, in writing, a hearing of the complaint by the Superintendent (or the Superintendent’s designee) within three (3) school days of receipt of the written Level One response. The employee shall present a file containing copies of the employee's statement, the Level One response, and any other pertinent information.

 

The Superintendent (or the Superintendent’s designee) shall contact the employee within three (3) school days after receipt of the file to set a hearing date, and shall set a hearing date within five (5) school days after receipt of the file. The Superintendent (or the Superintendent’s designee) shall render a decision, in writing, within five (5) school days after the hearing.

 

Level Three

 

Should the employee be dissatisfied with the decision of the Superintendent or designee, they may request a review by the Board of School Directors (“Board”) or a committee thereof, utilizing the following procedure:

  1. A written request for a hearing date shall be presented to the Board within five (5) school days after receipt of the Superintendent's or designee's decision.
     
  2. At a hearing of the Board, or committee of the Board, an oral presentation, not to exceed fifteen (15) minutes, may be made by the complainant, and questions may be asked by members of the Board.
     
  3. No new evidence shall be presented in oral or written form at the hearing that was not presented to either the Level One Respondent or Superintendent or designee unless good cause is shown why it could not have been (or was not) presented originally.
     
  4. The Board, or a committee of the Board, shall have twenty (20) school days after receipt of the complaint to schedule a hearing and render a decision.

The decision of the Board at Level Three is the final decision for all complaints.

 

Failure, at any step of this procedure, to communicate the decision on a complaint within the specified time limits shall permit the complainant to proceed to the next level.

 

Failure, at any step of the procedure, to appeal a complaint to the next level within the specified time limits shall be deemed acceptance of the decision rendered at that level. The time limits may be extended by mutual agreement in writing. Every effort shall be made, however, to process complaints as rapidly as possible and the number of days indicated shall be considered as a maximum.

 

Failure to appear at a scheduled meeting or hearing without good cause shall constitute abandonment of the complaint.


Confidentiality

 

In all phases of the complaint resolution process, every reasonable effort shall be made to maintain the confidentiality and protect the privacy of all parties, consistent with the District’s responsibility to investigate and address such complaints. However, even if one or more of the parties may have requested confidentiality, the request may not be honored if their safety and/or the safety of others may be compromised.
 

Retaliation Prohibited

 

Retaliation against an individual who files a good faith complaint in accordance with either the formal or informal procedures, or against an individual who participates in or cooperates with an investigation, is prohibited. Such retaliation will result in the same disciplinary action applicable to one who engages in harassment. Filing a good faith complaint will not affect the filing individual’s status with respect to an employee’s future employment opportunity, progress, or record.

 

Intentionally False Accusations Prohibited

 

False accusations and complaints can have serious effect on innocent persons. Therefore, it is prohibited to intentionally falsely accuse an individual or falsely file a complaint. Such intentionally false accusations and complaints will result in disciplinary action.
