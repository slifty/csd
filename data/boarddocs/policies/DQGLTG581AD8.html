Purpose

The Board recognizes that adequate records must be maintained on all buildings, equipment, and physical property under the control of the District.
 

Authority

The Board directs that a complete inventory be maintained by physical count of all District-owned equipment. It further directs that property records be maintained of all buildings and grounds under the control of the District. Such records shall be updated at such intervals as will coincide with property insurance renewal.  Useful lives and capitalization shall be set in accordance with Board Policy 622 (Capital Assets and GASB Statement 34).

 

Delegation of Responsibility

It shall be the duty of the Director of Business Services or designee to ensure that inventories of equipment are systematically and accurately recorded, updated, and adjusted annually by reference to purchase orders and withdrawal reports. Property records of facilities shall be maintained on an ongoing basis.

 

Guidelines

Major items of equipment shall be subject to periodic physical spot check inventory to determine loss, location, or depreciation; any major loss shall be reported to the Board.

Records of consumable supplies shall be maintained on a continuous inventory basis.

No equipment shall be removed for personal or non-school use, except in accordance with Board Policy.

 

The Director of Business Services or designee shall maintain a system of property records which shall show, as appropriate to the item recorded, description and identification, manufacturer, cost, year of purchase, and location.
