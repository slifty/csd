Authority


The authority to establish, equip, furnish, operate, and maintain the public schools of the Cheltenham School District is vested in the Board of School Directors, which is a body corporate and which is constituted and governed by Title 24 of the Pennsylvania statutes, the Public School Code of 1949, as amended, Article III of the Pennsylvania Constitution, and applicable federal and state law.


Powers


The Board shall establish such schools as are required for the education of every eligible person residing in Cheltenham School District between the ages of six (6) and twenty-one (21) years who may attend such schools; shall equip, furnish, operate, and maintain such schools; shall adopt and enforce rules and regulations for the management of school affairs and the conduct and deportment of employees and students; and shall levy and collect such taxes as may be necessary, in addition to the annual State appropriation, for the exercise of aforesaid powers.

 

The Board shall be governed by local board procedures (which shall constitute the Board's by-laws) as adopted by and periodically amended by the Board and in keeping with applicable state law. The 000 series of this Policy Manual shall comprise the local board procedures. Such local board procedures shall be consistent with law, have a rational and substantial relationship to a legitimate purpose of the Board, and be directed towards the maintenance and support of a thorough and efficient system of public education in the District.


The Board shall act as the general agent of the residents of the District in matters of public education. It shall establish educational goals and academic standards for District schools and govern an educational program designed to meet those goals and standards and to support student achievement. The Board shall be responsible for establishing, maintaining, and evaluating the educational programs in District schools and for enforcing mandatory laws and regulations.


The powers of the Board of School Directors are not vested in the individual school director. No such individual is authorized to act on behalf of the Board to carry out any of the Board’s statutorily authorized powers, except for those acts stated in law.
