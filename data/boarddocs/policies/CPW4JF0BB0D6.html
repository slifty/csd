Purpose

Home education programs for students of compulsory school age residing in the District shall be conducted in accordance with state law and regulations.

Guidelines


The Director of Student Services is the Superintendent’s designee for the administration, oversight, and record keeping of home education programs involving students who reside in the District.

The District permits the enrollment of home education students in the District to the extent required by applicable law.


The District does not issue a Cheltenham High School diploma, certificate of completion, or other document to certify the completion of high school or its equivalent by a student enrolled in a home education program. Home education students are not eligible to participate in Cheltenham High School graduation ceremonies. 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

The Superintendent or designee shall promulgate procedures implementing this Policy in accordance with applicable law and the guidelines outlined herein.
