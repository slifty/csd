Purpose

 

The purpose of this Policy is to communicate the District’s equal opportunity and non-discrimination policy with respect to employment and contract practices.

 

Guidelines

 

The District will provide to all persons equal access to all categories of employment in the District, free from discrimination, regardless of actual or perceived race, color, age, religious creed, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, ancestry, national origin/ethnicity, veteran status, marital status, handicap/disability, genetic information, pregnancy, childbirth, pregnancy-related medical conditions, or membership in any other protected class  

 

The District shall make reasonable accommodations for identified physical and mental impairments that constitute disabilities as well as pregnancy, childbirth and pregnancy-related conditions, consistent with the requirements of federal and state laws and regulations.

 

The Board also declares it to be the policy of the District to comply with federal law and regulations under Title IX prohibiting sex discrimination, which includes sex-based harassment. Such discrimination shall be referred to throughout this policy as Title IX sex discrimination.

 

The Board encourages employees and third parties who are believed to have been subject to discrimination or are made aware of discrimination prohibited by this Policy to report such discrimination to the Director of Human Resources, Title IX Coordinator and/or their immediate supervisor .

 

The Board directs that complaints of discrimination be investigated promptly, and corrective action be taken when allegations are substantiated. Confidentiality of all parties shall be maintained, consistent with the District's legal and investigative obligations.

 

No reprisals or retaliation shall occur as a result of good faith charges of discrimination.

 

Delegation of Responsibility

 

In order to maintain a program of nondiscrimination practices that is in compliance with applicable laws and regulations, the Board designates the Director of Human Resources as the District’s Title IX Coordinator. The contact information and procedures for contracting the Title IX Coordinator are included in the accompanying Regulations. Depending on the specific allegations raised in a complaint received pursuant to this Policy, the Superintendent may designate additional individuals to assist the Title IX Coordinator in carrying out their responsibilities outlined herein.

 

The Superintendent or designee shall ensure that procedures for resolving complaints involving discrimination prohibited by this Policy are developed and made part of the accompanying Administrative Regulations. The procedures shall be distributed periodically to all employees and posted in prominent locations for employees and other third parties to access.
