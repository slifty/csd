Purpose

The Board acknowledges the usefulness of a system of computing grade point averages for secondary school students to inform students, parents/guardians, and others of their relative academic performance.

 

 

 

 

Delegation of Responsibility

The Superintendent or designee shall develop and implement Administrative Regulations implementing this Policy which shall outline procedures for the computation of grade point averages.
