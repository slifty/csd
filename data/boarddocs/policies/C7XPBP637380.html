Use of Cell Phones and Similar Electronic Devices While Driving

 

District employees may only use a cell phone or similar electronic device while driving a vehicle on school business when such use is necessary due to an emergency. The District does not allow use of cell phones or similar electronic devices when such use is prohibited by federal, state, or local laws.

 

Occasions When Electronic Devices Must be Turned Off

 

Unless otherwise approved by the Superintendent or designee, District employees must turn off all personal electronic devices, or put those devices on silent mode, when the employee is present in a class during school hours with students and during school assemblies.
