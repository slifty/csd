Purpose

The purpose of this Policy is to recognize the District's responsibility to comply with the requirements of the Tax Reform Act of 1998.

 

Definition

The eligible taxes referred to under this Policy are the (1) real estate transfer tax, (2) business privilege tax, (3) mercantile license tax, and (4) earned income tax, which were adopted pursuant to the Local Tax Enabling Act (Act 511).

 

Authority

The Board shall adopt a Local Taxpayer Bill of Rights that includes the following components:

53 Pa. CSA Sec. 8423

 

  1. Taxpayer rights and the District's obligation during an audit or administrative review of the taxpayer's books or records.
     
  2. Administrative and judicial procedures for a taxpayer to appeal or seek review of a tax decision.
     
  3. Procedures for filing and processing refund claims and taxpayer complaints.
     
  4. Enforcement procedures.

 

The Board shall ensure that a taxpayer is notified about the District's Local Taxpayer Bill of Rights when the District is contacted regarding the assessment, audit, determination, review, and collection of the realty transfer tax, business privilege tax, mercantile license tax, or earned income tax imposed by the District.
 

Delegation of Responsibility

It shall be the responsibility of the Director of Business Services or designee to develop procedures to implement this Policy to include:

 

  1. Preparation and dissemination of the required notice of availability of the Local Taxpayer Bill of Rights.

 

  1. Preparation of a Local Taxpayer Bill of Rights.
     
  2. Preparation of a procedure for the District to request information from a taxpayer.
     
  3. Development of the form, content, process, and deadlines for taxpayers to file a tax appeal petition.

 

  1. Formulation of rules of practice and procedure for hearings, including an administrative appeals process.


The Director of Business Services or designee shall respond to taxpayer requests for the Local Taxpayer Bill of Rights by making copies available at the District's administrative offices or mailing at the District's expense.

 

Guidelines 

Appeals Process

The Board establishes the following administrative process to receive and make determinations on petitions from taxpayers relating to assessment, determination, or refund of an eligible tax.

 

  1. A taxpayer may file with the Hearing Officer designated herein a petition for (1) refund of the "Eligible Tax" or (2) reassessment of the "Eligible Tax" within the following deadlines:
     
    1. A refund petition must be filed within three (3) years after the due date for filing the report as extended or one (1) year after actual payment of the eligible tax, whichever is later.
       
    2. Petitions for reassessment of the eligible tax must be filed within ninety (90) days of the date of the assessment notice.
       
  2. The Hearing Officer shall, upon receipt of the petition, review it for completeness and accuracy and arrange for a hearing with the taxpayer petitioner in private for review of the petition, disposition, and decision.

  3. The decision of the Hearing Officer shall be issued within sixty (60) days of the date a complete and accurate petition is received in the office of the Director of Business Services.

 

The Board appoints the following:

 

  1. Earned Income Tax Collector for the District as Hearing Officer for the Earned Income Tax.
     
  2. The Director of Business Services as the Hearing Officer for the following:

 

  1. Business Privilege Tax.

  2. Mercantile License Tax.
     
  3. Real Estate Transfer Tax.

 

Information obtained by the District as a result of an audit, return, report, investigation, hearing, or verification shall be confidential. If a violation of confidentiality is committed by an officer or employee of the District, they shall be subject to discipline, including potential fines and dismissal from office or discharge from employment.
